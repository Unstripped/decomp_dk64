`for (g = 0; ...)` vs a do-while after `g = 0;` reorders a hoisted `%lo` pair; `for (i = 0, p = base; ...; i++, p++)` emits `move s0,zero` before `move s1,..`; moving the pointer bump into `while ((p += 0x34) != X)` emits X's `lui/addiu` first; post-call struct copies as a loop (`for (i..4) a[i] = tmpl;`, unrolled by IDO) split the `&tmpl` live range; an `s16` index stops unrolling of a copy loop.
