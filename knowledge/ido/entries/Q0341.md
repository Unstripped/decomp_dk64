the order of `lui/addiu` pairs hoisted before a loop follows how each address is spelled at first use: `(void *)(s32)&sym` on ONE address flipped mirrored pairs. The same cast on a one-use call argument (`void *p = (void *)(s32)&D_x;` at the head of its block) gets it an `$s` register or a `sw/lw a0` spill, where plain `&D_x` is rematerialised.
