Extends Q1418 (see Q1308, Q0591, Q1209): the `do { switch (...) { ... continue; ... break; } tail } while (0)` form gets every exit and the join right on a jump-table switch, and an empty `case 0x42: break;` table entry points straight past the switch (.rodata+0x10 = 0xb58). But the shared tail is always emitted at the END of the switch. The form matches only when the target's tail follows the switch, not when the tail sits in one case slot in the middle of the switch. Q1418's exact match (109660 gfxDrawMapBackground) relied on the tail being at the switch end.

Evidence: boss/3840.c bossScriptStep (unit CleanbossScriptStepc, ledger b3f6b9da; no merge): the target's tail is in a case slot at 0x59c, and the do/while(0) form scores full=394.
