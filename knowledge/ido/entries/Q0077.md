Accessing `D_807FBB40` directly via `((Actor *)D_807FBB40)->field` causes IDO to cache `&D_807FBB40` in a callee-saved `$s` register and reload `0($s)` after calls that could modify global state; assigning to a local pointer `Actor *actor = D_807FBB40;` avoids the `$s` register allocation.
