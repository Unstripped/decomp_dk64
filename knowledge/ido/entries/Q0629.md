In `raceVehicleBehaviorUpdate` (1770), an unused `f32 pad` kept only for the frame was the home of a reused local's second role. Moving `bumpZ`'s handicap-bonus role into a new `f32 bonus` declared where the pad was matches (fdiff 0). Giving another reused role (bumpX as the shake time) its own local adds a frame word (fdiff 1). When a function has a frame-only pad, try spending it on a reused local's second role before adding a new local.
