In `actorSpawnChildAtNode` (B2CE0), a file-local `void actorSetUniformScale(void *, s32);` steer plus PROTO_SKIP was paired with an `*(s32 *)&gCurrentActor->sub4->scaleY` pun at the call. With the shared f32 prototype the plain `scaleY` argument is passed in a1 the same way (raw float bits after an int argument, Q0026), so the steer pair and the pun go together (fdiff 0).
