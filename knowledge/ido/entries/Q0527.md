Making the pointer itself volatile (`void (*volatile callback)(void)`) forced its declared home at sp+108 without changing the 0x140 frame. Assign it before `func_80714950`, then pass it to `func_8071498C`; this preserves the original call order and callback value. The volatile qualifier is a tagged FAKE, not a recovered source claim.
