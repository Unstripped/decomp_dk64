playerUpdateCommon (global/CEAE0.c): with the body-order switch rewrite, the 8-byte switch temp (Q0296) grows the frame 0x78 -> 0x80; dropping the 2-byte `s16 snd` block local restores 0x78 and every stack home. The ternary form adds 4 instructions, and reusing the dead s16 `flags` leaves a 23-line a0/a1 swap.
