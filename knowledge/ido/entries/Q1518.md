boss/F0D0 bossSpawnPuffDebris passes a stored f32 scale to effectCreateAtPosition's f32 parameter. The target round-trips it through
double: `lwc1 36(sp)` 0x660, `cvt.d.s` 0x668, `cvt.s.d` 0x674, `mfc1 a1` 0x68c. `(f64)scale` reproduces it (the old `(f32)(f64)` outer
cast was redundant); plain `scale` and `scale * 1.0f` drop both conversions (.text 10192 vs 10208). The line carries `// ORIG:` with
those offsets.
