a `u16` parameter makes the prologue store the raw `$a1` to its caller home slot (`sw $a1, 0x1AC($sp)`) and mask it even when it is never reloaded; `s32` omits the store. Callers with constant/narrow arguments are unaffected by the prototype change (`func_8068C5A8` draft).
