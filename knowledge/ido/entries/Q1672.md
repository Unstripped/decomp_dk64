hudPauseMenuRender (frame 0x1A0): splitting a reused, register-only local into its own local shows exactly the frame-growth signature (frame 0x1A8, every home +8, registers unchanged: 47 lines). The same split matches when the new per-role local takes a word the function no longer needs (the case-1 slot index in slotBase's word after slotBase was dropped) or, for an s16 role, the 2-byte alignment hole between an s16 and an s32 home (slotDy at sp+0x154). Before tagging a reuse `// FAKE`, map the frame (named homes, holes, unused declared locals) and try each per-role local in each free word or hole; declaration order picks the slot. Narrowing s32 locals to s16 to free a word fails here (133-343 lines: sign extensions; confirms Q1665). Second case: 55B20 mapChunkTraversePortals `s16 target` (its field is read with lh and the callee parameter is s16) declared right after `u8 overlap; u8 emitted;` takes the 2-byte hole sp+0x98 above an s32 home (frame 0xD8 kept); only that one of 21 positions matches, an s32 split grows the frame by 8.
