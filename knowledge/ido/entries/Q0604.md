Replacing a raw-offset macro `*(T *)((u8 *)p + off)` with a real struct field can swap `$t0`/`$t9` in an `==` compare (a different candidate numbering, Q0474). Swapping the compare operands in the source restores the target (`effectUpdateAll`, 119370 round 5). Do not keep the macro just for this.
