Same function, c4e347 (0x1364). Extends Q1210 (see Q1069): when the other `+` operand is subscripted, a conversion on the variable's side (`(u32)off`, `* 4U`) no longer reverses the addu; subscript-first wins, only a compound `off +=` keeps the variable first (score 4 either way).
