`u8 idx * 10` shares the `li 10` with a nearby `sh` of `0xA` and becomes `multu`; indexing a row type `((s16 (*)[5])tbl)[idx][k]` keeps `sll; addu; sll`. `(x + x + x) * 12` gives the `addu, addu` then `sll/subu/sll` chain; `x * 3` forms do not.

**2026-11-16d extension (TypesOwner batch_6/8, merges f1fa7a52, b603f25c):** whether a constant is shared depends on the declared type of the store target: in race/52B0 raceOpponentRacerMotionUpdate a `u16` gTextNumberArg makes the `sh` of 0xA share `li 10` with the idx*10 multiply (multu), an `s16` keeps `sll/addu/sll` (0xf30-0xf3c) and a separate `li t5,10` (0xf80); in global/936B0 only a `u32` gGlobalOverlayFlags shares `lui v1,0x8` (0x7c00) between a mask test and the later `| 0x80000` (0x7c5c).
