a 64-bit store to a global emits `lui at; sw hi,0(at); sw lo,4(at)` with ONE `lui` only when the symbol is defined in the same TU; `extern u64` gives two `lui`s (loads use two either way). A single-`lui` pair in the ROM means the TU owns that `.data`/`.bss` object: 201B0's data (`0x807476A0`-`0x80747750`) and bss (`0x807F5B10`-`0x807F5D30`) are defined in C. Its tentative definition `u64 g;` lands in `.bss` as a strong symbol (never COMMON), so it cannot coexist with the asm label.
