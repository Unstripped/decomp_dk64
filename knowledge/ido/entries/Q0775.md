When a vertex/record loop shows uneven cursor steps with stores at negative offsets after the step, write the natural per-element walk (`index++; vertex++;` per written element); uopt's strength reduction produces the mixed steps itself. m2c's `vertex += 2` plus `vertex[-1]` spelling is a decompiler artefact.

Evidence: effectBuildGridVertices (global/119370.c): per-vertex blocks with `vertexIndex++; vertex++;` give the target's +32/+16/+16 steps with stores at -6 after the step. Also: with every candidate unconstrained (uopt -l 6) the kept dead expression tests only give load webs earlier candidate bits (width a1, height a2); removing them changes only register names (71 renames), never the shape.
