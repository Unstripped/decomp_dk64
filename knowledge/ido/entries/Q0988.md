The m2c clamp shape `t = G; if (-150.0f < t) G = t; else G = -150.0f;` compiles identically to `G = MAX(G, -150.0f)`: uopt CSEs the second read of G and both give `bc1fl` with `swc1 $f2` in the delay slot. The temp's only effect is a declared frame slot, so the natural rewrite is the MAX/MIN macro plus an untagged frame pad where the temp's word was.

Evidence: playerInputPostUpdate (global/E4090.c): without the temp the `sb zero` moves from 0x1b(sp) to 0x1f(sp); the macro plus a pad word restores it.
