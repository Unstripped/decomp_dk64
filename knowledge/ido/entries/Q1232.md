With ambient `* 0.5` in commandApplyToLight, a split piece of the joined 0.5 web over bb25-bb28 loads at 0x578 in $f16, one over bb27/bb28 loads at 0x7ec in $f14 (the target). The split flipped on unrelated candidate changes (address-taken actorY, falloff merged into dist), not on 100 declaration moves or commutative swaps. Before blaming a literal spelling for a hoisted constant, compare the split piece's `-l 5` blocks.
