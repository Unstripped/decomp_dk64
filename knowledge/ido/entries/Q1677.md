bossMainStep kept the u8 counter copy `v` in $v0 at 0x157c only because it also held the hit frame (0x35/0x17): that role gave it 6 points over 5 blocks (adjsave 3.00, Q1632). Reading case 6's `byteVars[0]` countdown through the same local (`count = byteVars[0]; if (count) { byteVars[0] = count - 1; ...}`: target 0x1dcc `lbu v0,24(s1); beqz v0; addiu t6,v0,-1; sb t6,24(s1)`) gives it 6 points over 4 blocks, the same 3.00 (Q1417's bucket(n) grows slowly), so it keeps $v0 and the hit frame becomes a plain `?:` (0x193c-0x1968 unchanged). The same role in another block is ordinary C (one local, same quantity); look for it before splitting. Q0861 measured the opposite while the hit frame still sat in `v`.
