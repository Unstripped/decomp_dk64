every declared word-sized local, including register-only ones and cfe value temps (`a && b`, `c ? x : y`, `return f() ? 1 : 0`), costs a home word in declaration order (first highest); u8/s8/s16 locals pack into one word; unused scalar pads reserve nothing but unused pad ARRAYS and read pads do; odd-count `s32` pad arrays round later offsets by 8; uopt spill temps sit 3 words below the lowest named local. A brute-force search over declaration order scored by absolute `sp` offsets fixes large frames fast once control flow matches (deadsweep's scorer masks `N(sp)`).
