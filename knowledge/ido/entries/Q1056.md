Any branch in the loop body drops the constant's adjsave to 0 and ugen rematerialises the `li` every iteration.

**2026-11-17 extension (Hackvsprintf8, ledger fe8bc4e3):** reading the pad byte from memory (`*"0"`) rebuilds it per iteration, but as lb, not li (vsprintf, 8 lines).
