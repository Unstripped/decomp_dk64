EXTENDS Q1238 and CORRECTS the earlier dossier claim "any real use emits code" (see Q1092, Q0908, Q0523): a selector local copied from a global pointer's field at a join, re-copied inside a guarded arm that calls, then switched on (`st = g->f; if (...) { call(); st = g->f; } switch (st)`) adds an appearance of the `&g` address constant at that join, keeping its adjsave positive so it gets a register (`lui`/ `addiu tN`, then `lw s0,0(tN)`). Reading g->f inline at the test and the switch splits the constant with adjsave 0.0 (no register: each access rematerialises `lui`/`lw %lo`); the dead trailing `if (!g->f) {}` only stood in for this.
