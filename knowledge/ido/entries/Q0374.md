`v & 0xFFFF` before an `sh`, `(w >> 8) & 0xFF` before an `sb`, or extra `& 0xFFFF` layers on an `(s16)` chain emit nothing (as1 deletes them, rewiring the chain's first instruction to the last register) but each shifts every later t-register by one; this closed three stage-6 parks with 2–10 register-only lines (`func_80717100`, `func_8071A1E4`, `func_807245F0` to 2 lines).

The mask may also sit on a loaded byte before a shift: `textBlitGlyph` (leftovers revisit 13) matched only with `*dst |= (*src & 0xF) << 4` (also `& 0xFF`/`& 0xFFF`/`& 0xFFFF`); plain `*src << 4` does not. ugen emits the `and` after the `lbu`, as1 deletes it (demanded bits: `& 0xF` goes too) and renames the load to the mask's register, which leaves a hidden alloc/free pair in the shift statements only. An outer `(u8)(*src << 4)` rotates the ring after the `sll` instead (full 8).

Signature `addu tX,sY,tZ ... move sY,tX` (a variable updated through a scratch register, then copied back): ugen emits `addu sY; and/sll ...; move sY,tN` for a narrowed store (`(u16)`, `(s16)` or `& 0xFFFF` on the sum), as1 deletes the narrowing and renames the add's destination to the mask register, keeping the move; the ugen corpus has no plain "addu into a temp, then move" otherwise (`hudPauseMenuRender` loop 1, leftovers revisit 13). The tested plain expression cast put the computed operand first (`addu t7,t6,s0`), whereas `narrowvar += X` kept the variable first (7CA80 `totalDataSize +=`). This is not an exclusive requirement: operand type can also change the order.

In the HudFinal `hudPauseMenuRender` coordinate candidate, `off` remains `s32`: after `off = (u16)(off * 4)` and the float-product addition, changing `off = (s16)(off + E->unkC * 4)` to `off = (s16)((u32)off + E->unkC * 4)` changed the raw and final `addu` to variable-first. The coordinate sequence interleaves `y *= 4; y += (s32)(fa * E->unk4);` before that final x update, with an initialized dead `if (off) {}` after the initial x/y loads. `E` abbreviates the repeated entry expression, not a pointer local. The pre-dispatch normalized difference dropped 8 to 7, but the whole function remained full=318, shape=73; this is a compiler observation, not a completed match.

Count instructions separately from relocation records when comparing these experiments. `deadsweep`'s `n` includes both: the intermediate full=299 candidate and target both have n=993, but that candidate has 892 instructions plus 101 relocations, against the target's 890 plus 103. Equal `n` does not establish equal machine-code length.
