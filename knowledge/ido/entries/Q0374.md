`v & 0xFFFF` before an `sh`, `(w >> 8) & 0xFF` before an `sb`, or extra `& 0xFFFF` layers on an `(s16)` chain emit nothing (as1 deletes them, rewiring the chain's first instruction to the last register) but each shifts every later t-register by one; this closed three stage-6 parks with 2–10 register-only lines (`func_80717100`, `func_8071A1E4`, `func_807245F0` to 2 lines).

The mask may also sit on a loaded byte before a shift: `textBlitGlyph` (leftovers revisit 13) matched only with `*dst |= (*src & 0xF) << 4` (also `& 0xFF`/`& 0xFFF`/`& 0xFFFF`); plain `*src << 4` does not. ugen emits the `and` after the `lbu`, as1 deletes it (demanded bits: `& 0xF` goes too) and renames the load to the mask's register, which leaves a hidden alloc/free pair in the shift statements only. An outer `(u8)(*src << 4)` rotates the ring after the `sll` instead (full 8).

Signature `addu tX,sY,tZ ... move sY,tX` (a variable updated through a scratch register, then copied back): ugen emits `addu sY; and/sll ...; move sY,tN` for a narrowed store (`(u16)`, `(s16)` or `& 0xFFFF` on the sum), as1 deletes the narrowing and renames the add's destination to the mask register, keeping the move; the ugen corpus has no plain "addu into a temp, then move" otherwise (`hudPauseMenuRender` loop 1, leftovers revisit 13). A cast on the expression puts the computed operand first (`addu t7,t6,s0`); only `narrowvar += X` keeps the variable first (7CA80 `totalDataSize +=`).
