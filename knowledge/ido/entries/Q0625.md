In `raceInitGateActors` (A960), an earlier draft copied the u8 counter into `u32 j` to index `node->actors[j]`, which saved 4 instructions over the plain u8 index. The permuter found the natural form: `if (i)` plus `node->actors[i]` with no copy matches (fdiff 0), so the `u32` copy was a hack.
