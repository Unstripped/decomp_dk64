Casts that repeat C's usual conversions compile identically and can be dropped: `(f64)(u32)` on a u32 global, `(f32)`/`(s16)` on an assignment's right-hand side, `(f32)(...)` around an f32 result, `(f64)` beside a double literal, the `(s32)` in `(s16)(s32)floatExpr`, and a literal `* 1.0` on a double product. The exception is a `(f32)` on an s16 used in double arithmetic (`(f64)(f32)s` as a divisor): the `(f64)` is noise, but the `(f32)` keeps the s16 -> f32 -> f64 conversion path and must stay.

Evidence: actorUpdateBannerFade (global/A2F10.c, merge 166da21c). Rereview11BC10c (global/11BC10.c, merge c69e341d) removed casts 172 -> 60 in 16 functions with no code change, including the `* 1.0` in effectGrowFadeUpdate, and three steering temps.
