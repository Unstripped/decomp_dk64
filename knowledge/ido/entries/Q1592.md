eepromGetSectorOffset (global/104F0.c, 6 parameters, non-leaf) took an ANSI `(u16 *, u8 *, s32, u8, u8, u8)` prototype in place of its K&R definition with the object unchanged: its four same-file callers already pass u8 values. Counter-case in the same file: eepromClearSlotData needs K&R because the caller eepromSetCurrentSlot re-masks (`andi t6,a0,0xff` at 0x1044 becomes `move a2,a0`), and `int slot` loses the home store at 0xf78. Also: an offset-first `addu` is not proof of source order by itself: in eepromGetSectionBuffer base-first and offset-first both give `addu v1,t8,t9` (0x2b5c/0x2ba8), while the sprintf (0x1b84) and data (0x14b8) sums there need offset-first.
