A byte global declared `extern s8 g;` loads with `lb` even when the value is stored into a `u8` field, so a matching body needed `u8 t = g & 0xFFu` inside `if (1)` blocks to force `lbu` (`resQueueAttachActor`, 112080). Declaring the extern `u8` gives `lbu` with the plain body and removes the hack. Its only other user (`resQueueSetValue`) just stores it with `sb`, which is unchanged. An `& 0xFF`/u8-temp hack on a global read means the extern really is unsigned.
