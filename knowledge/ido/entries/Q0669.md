Making a char-pointer parameter const in its prototype raises cfe W709 (Incompatible pointer type) at callers whenever the signedness differs: `strlen(const char *)` warned at 9 callers passing `u8 *`, while `strlen(const u8 *)` warned at none; under `const u8 *` a string literal argument (2C910 `":"` to `_strcspn`) adds one W709. So const-qualify with the element type the callers already pass (`const u8 *`), and leave a parameter whose callers mix `u8 *` and literals unchanged (boot/3630 `_strcspn`). Evidence: CleanBoot3630 stand-in caller copies and cwarn counts (round 18).
