A `u32` expression passed to a pointer/`s32` parameter puts the `ucvt` in the argument slot and the shared sub-expression lands in `s4`; with `u32` parameters it is `a0` plus a spill. The callees' parameter types are part of the lever (`heapMoveWords`/`heapRebaseHandles` now take `u32`).
