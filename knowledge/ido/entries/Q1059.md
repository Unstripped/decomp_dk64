HackeepromSaveRecords4 (unit "2026-11-16b: cleanup HackeepromSaveRecords4", ledger-only d4b7b65d), src/global/104F0.c eepromSaveRecords (uopt, regalloc/width): a real use of a u16 variable in a block before a callee-saved stretch raises its priority above its widening, but when that use is an assignment to another u16 local the narrowing `andi` is emitted at the assignment, not deferred to the later loop-entry delay slot (target 0x1e38). Evidence: `idx = sector;` hoisted ahead of the poll loop scores 4 vs the dead `if ((u32)sector) {}` at 0. See also Q0549, Q0453, Q1008.
