in `actorListContainsId`, `items[i]` (index) makes IDO strength-reduce into an induction pointer in `$a3` (`move $a3, $v0`), while `node++` keeps the pointer in `$v0`. In leaf predicates, caching only the narrow index (`u8 kong = beh[0x1A4];`) rather than the pointer puts the index in `$v0`/`$v1` and the table/base in `$t6`–`$t8` as in the original.
