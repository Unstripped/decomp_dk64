cfe folds a redundant same-width cast on an argument (`(s16)(s16)x`, `(s16)(s32)x` with x an s32) into one narrowing, and uopt then CSEs it with identical narrowings at later calls, so such a cast cannot separate calls the way `(s16)(u16)x` does. Evidence: camPathCommandDispatch case 0x2F call 1 with `(s16)(s16)d` or `(s16)(s32)d` scores full=5, the same as plain `(s16)d`; passing `(s16)params23` at calls 2/3 instead gives 432 (a cvtl of the reloaded argument replaces the target's `lh a1,58(sp)` of T3).
