In `HackenemyGridStateStep2` (2026-11-15 cleanup unit), two dead tests (commits 716d92 and 85e7fb) act as one coupled register-allocation knot, like the case in Q0602. The coupling runs through uopt priority weighting. The `cell & 3` expression (adjsave 1.0) and the reload of `ones` together lower the priority of the `gridX`/`gridY` webs to 0.75. At that priority, those webs no longer get `v1`/`a3`. This means removing either dead test changes which webs win `v1`/`a3`, so the two have to be evaluated together, not one at a time. The evidence is a priority trace comparison, `trace_base_5.txt` vs `trace_nodead_5.txt`, in `.scratch/HackenemyGridStateStep2`. When dead tests seem to be load-bearing, compare web priorities with and without them before treating each one as a separate fake-match steer.
