At `-mips2` the `u64` helper calls m2c prints are what plain 64-bit C compiles to: `OS_CYCLES_TO_USEC(c)` gives the `__ll_mul(x, 0x40)` + `__ull_div(.., 0xBB8)` pair, `u64 / 1000000` and `u64 / 10000 % 100` give `__ull_div`/`__ull_rem`, and an implicit `u64` to `double` conversion gives `__ull_to_d`. Write the natural expressions, not the helper calls (fdiff 0, `hudCountdownTimerUpdate`).
