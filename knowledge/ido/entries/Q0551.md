as1 rewrites uses of a copied register in the fall-through arm of a conditional branch (after `move s0,a0`, the arm's `sw t3,52(s0)` becomes `sw t3,52(a0)`). `if (arg0 && arg0 && arg0) {}` as the first statement of the arm leaves a `beq s0,0,L; L:` label block (deleted later, Q0548) that resets the forwarding, so the store keeps `52(s0)` as in the target. In `jetpacBonusItemUpdate` (revisit 12) this plus eight Q0548 window guards before the next test took the park from full 7 to 2.
