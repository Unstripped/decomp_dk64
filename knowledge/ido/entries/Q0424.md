a K&R definition (`void f(a0, a1) unsigned char a0; int a1; {`) gives the entry home stores, lets the body reassign the formal straight into the caller's home byte (`sb t6,0x1B(sp)` with a 0x18 frame), works in a leaf without a frame, and coexists with the file's unprototyped `void f();` (an ANSI narrow definition there is cfe error 642). ANSI and K&R narrow formals emit different entry masks (`andi` vs shift pair): try both. A callee declared `s16` re-extends an ANSI `u16` formal; `s32` passes it through.
