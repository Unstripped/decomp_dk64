Refines Q0806 (see also Q0687). Q0806 says a function reading a global struct partly through a pointer and partly through split labels matches once every field read goes through the pointer, and that mixed forms fail. A narrower case also works: one split label can fold on its own through a pointer local the function already holds, while the function's other reads stay in the direct global form. In jetpacDrawPlayer (jetpac/1A60.c), `player = &jetpacPlayer.player` already serves x, state and explosion; y and color are read as `jetpacPlayer.player.y` / `.color`. `D_8002F080` (+0x30) rewritten as `player->flying` is RELOC-EQUIV: its lui/lw stays separate and only the LO16 addend moves to jetpacPlayer+0x30. Spelled `jetpacPlayer.player.flying` (what `rename.py fold` writes), uopt loads &jetpacPlayer into a0 once (lui/addiu at 0x10a8) and reads y and flying as 4(a0) / 0x30(a0) (Q0687): fdiff 1. Rule: when a direct parent.field fold fails because of base sharing, try the pointer spelling on that one read before keeping the label (TypesU3, ba194732).
