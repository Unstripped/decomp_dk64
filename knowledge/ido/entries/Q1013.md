A ternary assigned to a narrower integer (`u8f = d > 255.0 ? 255.0 : d;`) converts each arm separately, with a full float->int sequence per arm, and does NOT fold the literal arm: the literal is loaded and converted at run time. Two such ternaries CSE the literal into one register. The if/else form (`if (d > 255.0) f = 255.0; else f = d;`) folds that arm to `li 255`. So a target that converts a constant at run time once per arm is a ternary on the literal, not a value-named constant local.

Evidence: func_806B0848 (global/B2CE0.c, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup Hackfunc_806B0848"): two ternaries share the literal in $f2 (0x2898); the if/else form gives 237-238 lines and the old cap-local version also needed a one-line `// LAYOUT:` if/else for the li order (Q0149). Same rule for f32 in effectDecelerateDriftUpdate/effectSpiralRiseUpdate (global/11BC10.c, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackeffectDecelerateDriftUpdate", 15c0e8e8): `u8dst = (x > 255.0f) ? 255.0f : x;` gives two float->u32 sequences and two `sb`, the literal arm converted from a register (`cvt.w.s $f16,$f12` at 0x2e1c). Both conversions share one `.loc`, so as1 hoists their `li tN,1` rounding constants else-arm first (0x2dbc/0x2dc0): a one-line if/else matches, a multi-line if/else swaps them (2), MIN(x, cap) gives 5.
