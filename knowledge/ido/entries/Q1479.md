cfe folds the literal quotient `1.0f / 30.0f` to its f32 value, so `x * (1.0f / 30.0f)` emits the same `mul.s` by the literal 0x3D088889 as `x * 0.03333333507f`. It is the readable spelling of a target's reciprocal multiply (Q0134 spells such reciprocals as decimal literals). `x / 30.0f` is not turned into a multiply, unlike a power-of-two divisor (Q0354, Q0134), and keeps `div.s` (fdiff 1). Related Q0092, Q0579 (division spellings that keep `div.s`).
