Extends Q1134 (only identical literal text merges) and Q1414/Q1429 (literal choice in halvings): in a double expression the promoted float literal `0.5f` and the double literal `0.5` are separate constant entries. `(f64)(sag - acc) * 0.5f` and `(sag - acc) * (f64)0.5f` match; `* 0.5` and `0.5 * x` fail (`mtc1 zero,$f21` at 0x191c, the FP registers shift). An `f` suffix on a literal in double arithmetic can be load-bearing.
