consecutive `u8` locals share one word, allocated downward byte by byte (`pan`/`vol`/`pitch` at `sp+0x57`/`0x56`/`0x55`); a lone `u8` sits in the top byte of its own word (`found` at `sp+0xC3`, `sel` at `sp+0xB3`), an `s16` in the low half (`flag` at `sp+0x6E`). A `switch` whose variable is unassigned on some path (`sel`, `flag`) is memory-resident: every case stores to the home slot and the use reloads it; initialising it before the switch colours it (`func_8063FA48`).
