`res |= call()` makes cfe create a temp (dead ustr) that sits above uopt's two size temps and grows the frame 0x48 -> 0x50. `res = res | call()` gives frame 0x48 and exact homes, but uior then takes the call result first (`or v1,v0,v1` vs target `or v1,v1,v0`). Also: copying a u8 field into an s16 local instead of a u8 one moved it from t0 to a3 (`lbu a3,140(t9)` at 0x44); the lead kept that width tagged as a FAKE.
