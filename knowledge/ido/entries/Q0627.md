In `raceParticipantMotionUpdate` (6650), a local pointer refreshed after every call (`actor = gCurrentActor;`, 6 untagged reloads) can be replaced by direct `gCurrentActor` reads. Removing the local moves a u8 local's stack slot by 4; declaring `playerIdx` before `colorMode` restores the slot, and the result is plain C (the permuter's only change was that declaration order).

**2026-11-16c correction (HackraceParticipantMotionUpdate, merge 23caa475):** with the shared EFDC0 prototype `playerIdx` is a `u8` local (no andi before passing lbu a2,0x28(s3) at 0x87c), and a u8 next to the u8 `colorMode` packs into its word (frame 0x50 -> 0x48, colorMode sp+0x43 -> sp+0x3e). It must be declared second (`racerState; u8 playerIdx; s32 i; u8 colorMode; u32 flags`) so a non-u8 local separates the two u8s; 12 of 120 declaration orders match.
