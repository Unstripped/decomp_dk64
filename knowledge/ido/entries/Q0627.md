In `raceParticipantMotionUpdate` (6650), a local pointer refreshed after every call (`actor = gCurrentActor;`, 6 untagged reloads) can be replaced by direct `gCurrentActor` reads. Removing the local moves a u8 local's stack slot by 4; declaring `playerIdx` before `colorMode` restores the slot, and the result is plain C (the permuter's only change was that declaration order).
