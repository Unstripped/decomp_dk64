Q1115's rule (a frame word's slot follows the declaration's textual position relative to spilled macro `_g` temps) also holds for referenced, register-allocated locals: a used `Gfx *dl` declared at the alpha block's top or at function scope still puts the spilled `_g` at 0x54 instead of the target's 0x58. Any frame word declared textually after the spilled `_g` (pads after the later never-spilled B/sync/PopMatrix `_g` temps) gives the target frame 0x138. Evidence: renderModelAnimatedSlot probes p1-p4 full=0, r7, r8 (site 1a2082 kept).
