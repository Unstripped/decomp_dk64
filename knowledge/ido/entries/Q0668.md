A never-read extra K&R parameter used to keep a home slot can be replaced by a local `s16` declared after the neighbouring local, with `pad[N]` shrunk by one word: the local takes the freed word and the other homes move down (103AB0 lensFlareDrawSource, pad2[4] -> pad2[3]).
