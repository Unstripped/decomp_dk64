Q0649's spare argument matters only when something else would otherwise occupy a1. In B5EC0 func_806B13B4 the selector is already in a1 at the default jal (0xb2c), so `(0x203, sel)` and `(0x203)` give identical objects; B2CE0 differs. For 72fa9a, the jal at 0x9d8 has a1 = sel by residence (`move a1,a0` after `lbu a0,0x154(s0)`, and no write to a1 between `jr t9` at 0x450 and 0x9d8). Owner 1295B0 overwrites a1 at 0x5a0, so the site stays FAKE: there is no ORIG and no owner retype.

Correction (2026-11-23, issue #1): B2CE0 does not differ at HEAD. Its two `behaviourUpdateContactAndState(K, state)` calls match as one-argument calls under the shared prototype (full ROM OK), so the `// ORIG:` spare argument there was removed; BB300's still fails without it. See Q1607.
