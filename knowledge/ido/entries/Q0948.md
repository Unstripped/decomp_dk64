In `spawnNodesSetRespawnHold` (global/1295B0.c, e9a088 fixed), the target keeps the u8 parameter `command` in a callee-saved register for the whole loop: `andi s3,a0,0xff` before the loop, then `move s1,s3` each pass. Writing the loop body with an early `continue` on `node->flags & 0x80` and no `do {} while (0)` makes uopt reload `command` from its home every pass (`lbu 83(sp)`, score 39). The earlier match only got there with an empty `do {} while (0)`, which was faking the block shape. Writing the body as `if (!(node->flags & 0x80)) { ... }` gives the right shape on its own, so the fake can go. This is the converse of Q0773: there an early continue replaced a dead test, but here the wrapped `if` block is the natural shape. When the permuter inserts a fake block inside a loop, try switching between continue and if-wrap in both directions. Merge 79fc2dd0, ledger cfe24734, base 3144bc00.
