a `.data` global used by exactly one function can be a function-local `static T x = init;`: uopt treats it as non-aliased, so its stores no longer kill earlier field loads (PRE reloads land before the store) and a store may follow the return-value copy. A file-scope static does not do this. Needs the TU's `.data` carved into C (cameraStepAspectScale, effectCircleRiseFadeUpdate, bossSpawnerUpdate).
