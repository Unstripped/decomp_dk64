In `enemyAggroStateUpdate` (12E800, round 4) three steers act as one register-allocation knot: reusing the local `me`, the dead `if (ang ...)` test and `(ret ^ 0)`. Removing any one of them alone fails, so try removing or rewriting them together before calling each one exhausted. Second case, `textLayoutAppendRows` (112080, round 6): with a `u32` callee prototype, the constant argument `heapAlloc(0x20)` shares a hoisted register with an equal unsigned compare constant (`0x20U`). The `heapAlloc` steer and the two `0x20U` tags only work together, and removing all three at once matches.
