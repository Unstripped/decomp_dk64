In `enemyAggroStateUpdate` (12E800, round 4) three steers act as one register-allocation knot: reusing the local `me`, the dead `if (ang ...)` test and `(ret ^ 0)`. Removing any one of them alone fails, so try removing or rewriting them together before calling each one exhausted.
