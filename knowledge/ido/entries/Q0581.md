In IDO 5.3, single-use expression results (such as `((cnt & 0x20) / 32)` in a store `dest = expr == 0`) receive low savings weight in uopt and are colored into ugen's temporary scratch register ring (`$t5`). This shifts every subsequent temporary register in the basic block by one (`t5->t6, t6->t7, t7->t8`). Decomp matches often used hacks like `expr == (expr * 0)` to duplicate the subexpression, doubling its reference count and elevating its live-range priority into `$v0`. Replacing the hack with a standard named temporary (`s32 t = expr; dest = t == 0;`) requires declaring the local at the enclosing function scope, but can also trigger basic-block register spilling if the temporary lifetime overlaps callee-saved registers.
