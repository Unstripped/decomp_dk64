`x += K * call()` vs `x = x + call() * K`, `v = f(); v = load * v;`, `*(f32 *)p` vs `p->f`, and repeated `*ptr` vs a local copy each flip `mul`/`add` operand order; `* 2.0f` becomes `add.s x,x` (int `* 2` keeps `mul.s`); `x + -0.63` vs `x - 0.63` picks `add.d`/`sub.d` and a different constant.
