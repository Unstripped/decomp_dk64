an `s32` search function ending in `return found;` pushes `found` off `$v0`; dropping the final return (falling off the end) gives the target's `found`=`$v0`, pointer=`$v1` and removes a trailing `move v0` (`volumeTriggerSetEnabled`, `volumeTriggerSetPosition`).
