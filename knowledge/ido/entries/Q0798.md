A no-op `^ 0` on a switch selector creates a separate selector copy that ugen schedules after the zero-range branch; a real narrowing cast also creates the copy but ugen schedules it before the branch. So the two are not interchangeable: a target with the copy after the range branch needs a non-converting copy.

Evidence: actorBannerTextRender (global/A2F10.c, 0c76ca kept).
