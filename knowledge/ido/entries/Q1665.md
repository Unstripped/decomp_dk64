This refines Q1198 (related Q1574, Q1220). Packing a narrow partner beside an existing narrow local, to free a frame word for a split role local, helps only if the partner needs no sign extension where it is assigned.

hudPauseMenuIconUpdate: the nine named homes fill sp+0x34-0x57. An `s16 type` (the switch selector) packed beside `s16 kongCount` frees the word, and the spill temps stay at 0x24/0x30. But the `sll`/`sra` before the switch's `sltiu at,v1,16` shifts the t-register ring: 106 with a dedicated `wave` local, 157 for the packing alone. `s16 shade` beside `s16 i` scores 81. Dropping kongCount for an inline sizeof count runs into Q1220's strength reduction (30). The six FAKE role reuses stay.

Correction (2026-11-25c, CleanhudPauseMenuRender, 6446f1ba): the packing result stands, but the six role reuses no longer stay FAKE: they passed the three-part census (Q1666) and are untagged in hudPauseMenuIconUpdate.
