an 8-byte diff around a call, or a leftover `move a1,zero` in one arm, often means the callee takes fewer arguments than m2c shows (`func_806C9F74`, `func_806E607C`); `li a1,0` instead of `move a1,zero` means the parameter is `f32`; a live local passed as an argument forbids that argument register for it across the call (`func_806C9830`).
