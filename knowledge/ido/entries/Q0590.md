For a u8 local, the bare truth test and `!= 0` produce different cfe ucode (ucg1/fjp vs explicit compare), which shifts the local's spill/colouring. One spelling change replaced three deadsweep fakes in modelSegmentFreeUnusedTextures and a `volatile u8` in raceUpdateRacerMotion. Try both spellings of every u8 test before adding guards. Similarly `(*p).f` vs `p->f` are not codegen-identical in cfe (behaviourFlyPatrolWithCallback).
