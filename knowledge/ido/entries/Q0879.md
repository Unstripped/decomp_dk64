IDO -O2 gives every declared scalar a frame slot even when it is folded or register-held, so the frame and the offset of the address-taken `glist` (0x6c in a 0x78 frame; a sixth scalar gives 0x80) fixed two locals before it in arcadeDrawStage4Background (arcade/0.c): one was a real inner-loop `y`, retiring a value-named `one` local. With a literal step uopt rewrites `x < K` into `!=` only on loops whose bound gets a callee-saved register (0x3b80/0x3ca4/0x3bd8/0x3ce4; 0x3b54/0x3d10 stay slti), and a variable-vs-constant `!=` compiles to uneq{var}{const} in either source order; only an expression on the left (an isop such as uxor) puts the constant first (bnel s2,s0 at 0x3e34).
