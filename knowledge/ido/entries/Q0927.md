collisionTestStaticMeshFloor (global/6AD90.c, scored 356775 and kept). With separate `f32 floorY; u8 hit;` locals the stack homes match. But `hit` (M -141, a vreg) is never coloured, while the expression ucvt(hit) (candidate 364) is coloured $s0 across the jal at 0x2438. So the final read uses s0 (`lbu s0,203(sp)` at 0x23a8) instead of the target's post-call reload (`lbu t5,203(sp)` at 0x2440). A `u32` spelling does reload. Only the aggregate spelling reproduces the reload: there `hit` loses its vreg and the jal kills the value. No callee takes a byte pointer (the only one is `addiu a0,sp,204` = &floorY), and no struct in include/ or src/ with u8 at +3 and f32 at +4 fits. Rule: when the target reloads a byte local after a call, storage is being forced into memory (Q0742). It is not a missing address escape. Ledger-only 39a18f93, base 1d00db9a (unit HackcollisionTestStaticMeshFloor2).
