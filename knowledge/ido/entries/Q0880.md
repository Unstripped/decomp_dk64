In menuRenderOptionDescription (menu/E550.c, frame 0xE8) the declared locals sit at -4..-44 and each GBI macro `_g` block at -48..-132 (22 here), spill temps below them; a `gDPSetCombine` macro adds a 23rd `_g` slot and grows the frame to 0xF0, so a macro packet needs exactly one fewer named local. Unused function-level locals can be declared in any order without changing code. Assigning the last computed value to the address-taken `off` makes ugen evaluate the truncation before the call arguments (target t6/t7/a2/t8 order) at the cost of the escape store. Confirms and extends Q0419.
