In slotQueueGetMaxValue (global/FCCD0.c) `u8 bits` loaded from a field and used as the source of a plain `while (rest != 0)` loop moves `player` from a1 to a2 (fdiff 17); declaring it s32 restores a1 and the redundant `if (bits)` guard can go (the beqz a0 at 0x1164 is the loop's own rotated entry test, Q0083). In slotQueueStateMoveToTarget, `if (ABS(d) < step) step = ABS(d)` makes uopt merge the second ternary into step, while the target keeps a separate value (v0, then move a3,v0 at 0x2908): only a named copy local reproduces it (stays FAKE).
