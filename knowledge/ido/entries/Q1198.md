When a register-reused local cannot be split because one more declared local grows the frame, look for s32 locals that only ever hold narrow values. In global/ACDC0 hudPauseMenuUpdate (frame 0x58, nine declared word locals at sp+0x34..0x54) every dedicated role local scored 33-50 only because the frame grew to 0x60. The stick locals `x` and `stickY` only hold `gCurrentContPad->stick_x/stick_y` (s8, lb); declared as adjacent `s8 x; s8 stickY;` they share the home word sp+0x3c (Q0361 packing), every stored home stays put (hubWorld 0x38, buttons 0x40, moved 0x44, diff 0x4c, i 0x50), and a new `s32 step` declared last takes the freed sp+0x34: uopt colours it a1 (bit 353, unsplit) as in the target (move a1,v1 at 0xc88) and the carousel-step reuse of `i` (FAKE af58f9) is gone, fdiff OK. Packing alone without a new local is full=4: the uopt spill temp moves from sp+0x28 to 0x2c (it sits 3 words below the lowest named local).
