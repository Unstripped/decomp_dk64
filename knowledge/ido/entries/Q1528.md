In 55B20 mapChunkEmitLayerDisplayLists an element pointer `cell = &cells[i]` assigned in the preheader puts the hoisted
`lw gMapCells` after the address computation. Reading `cells[i].f` without a local makes the address a hoisted expression too,
placed after the loop pointer's initial load, so as1 can move the `lui` above the preceding branch and the `lh` before the `lw`
(8 lines with the local, 0 without).
