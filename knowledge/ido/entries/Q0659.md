Changing the 3C10 owner `mapGetHubWorldIndex` to `s32 (u8, s32)` gives fdiff 1 in six callers (3C10, 36880, 478F0, 9DD70, B5EC0, C2A90). The real prototype is (u8, u8) with a u8 return; 8F4B0's local steer stays (its caller frame grows 0x68 -> 0x70 with the u8 return).
