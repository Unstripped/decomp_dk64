Restructuring a loop body's final test as an early `continue` (`if (!in) continue;` before the hit code) gives uopt the same extra blocks a dead `if (x) {}` inside the hit block would, enough to drop such a dead test; converting further range tests to early continues separates the remaining steer effects.

Evidence: zoneCheckActorInside (global/1295B0.c, FAKE 5 -> 4, 7b005b fixed): an early continue replaced `if (zone) {}` between two u8 stores; turning the y-range test into an early continue too left be434b controlling only the caller-saved count/flags pair and 1aaf2a only the callee-saved constant-5 web.
