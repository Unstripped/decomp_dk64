An f32 argument that lands in an integer register (3rd argument, a2) compiles to a plain `lw a2` from memory when written as a direct field read, but to `lwc1 $f0` + `mfc1 a2,$f0` when it goes through a local. So `lwc1`/`mfc1` before the call is evidence of a source local. Related: every declared local, block-scoped too, reserves its own frame slot (Q0331), so a correct one-use temp that "fails by 8 bytes of frame" can mean another local did not exist in the original.

Evidence: playerFireWeapon (global/E4090.c, merge 1f7eec9a): `lwc1 $f0,536(v0)` (camZ) at 0x33b8, `mfc1 a2` at 0x33c4, jal projectileSetTargetCoords at 0x33c8; FAKE 13c383 retired as ORIG. The extra local was the block-scoped `s32 v` unk8F clamp temp; written as a ternary, the f32 z local fits.
