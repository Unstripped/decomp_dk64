After a store through another pointer that may alias a byte field (`state->holdTimer--`), one read of that field after an if/else join stays at the join; uopt does not move it into the one arm that needs it even when the other arm stored a constant to the field. A target that reloads the field in one arm (textBoxUpdateRows: lbu v0,340(s3) at +0x350 after sb t5,18(s2) at +0x348) and uses the stored constant in the other (andi at +0x33c) had an explicit `x = field` in that arm: ORIG.
