In launchBarrelUpdate (site bd5eb8), `if (0) {}` between the `state = 1` and `timer = 10` stores leaves an orphan label (lab 17) in uopt's ucode. With it, the `unal $v1` pair sits after the 22-entry jump table, before case 1, and the reloads after `camStartPath` come out as rlda $v1 (&gActorGlobalState), rldc $a3 1, rldc $t0 2. Without it, the unal pair follows the camStartPath call and the reloads become rldc, rldc, rlda. as1 then hoists case 1's `lui a1,0x3f80` above `bne v0,at` (0x364 vs 0x378, score 2). A selector local (80), a one-case switch (6) and an `&&` merge of the init ifs (2) do not reproduce the label.
