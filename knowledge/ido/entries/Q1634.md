80150 tagBarrelActorUpdate: a loop over a pointer array bounded by a u8 count that a store in the body may alias. As a pointer walk `for (ids = arr; ids < &arr[count]; ids++)` it compiles to an sltu/beqz pointer entry test instead of blez count (0x58d4), hoists the case constant (li a0,140) and recomputes the bound only after the aliasing store; the target recomputes it on both edges, which the counter loop's LFTR gives (scores 289 / 347). The 71b822 empty-default steer stays. ReMeasureB18H (2026-11-24b).
