`s16 h = call() + cmd->b; if (h < 0) h = 0; f(h)` re-extends `h` at the call when `f` is prototyped `(s16)` and `h` cannot take `$a2`; `s32 h = (s16)(...)` with a K&R `void f();` declaration puts the single `sll/sra` at the assignment (`func_8063FA48` case 0x4C).
