cfe keeps the text of single-precision literals, so `0.0f`, `0.00f`, `0.f` and `.0f` are distinct constants to uopt (separate `mtc1 zero` webs) while double literals are canonicalised. Also distinct zero webs: `0.0` stored into an f32 field, `(f32)0`, and an int `0` in a float compare (`if (f != 0)`); `(f32)0` as a clamp's lower bound can instead share the upper bound's register. A double literal passed to a prototyped `f32` parameter (`hudDrawScaledText(..., 0.5, ...)`) is converted at compile time and loads with `lui a3,0x3f00` like a separate `.5f` spelling, so it replaces a tagged `.5f` spelling lever with ordinary C (`menuRenderOptionDescription`).
