With `records` retyped to `u8 *` in `actorListSpawnFromRecords` (8D3E0), `count = *(s32 *)records; records += 4; item = (ActorListRecord *)records;` matches the original's `*records++` on `s32 *`; `(T *)(records + 4)` and `((s32 *)records + 1)` do not.
