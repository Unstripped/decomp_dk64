The target's d-first `add.d $f16,$f0,$f4` (mapPolyClipToFrustumPlane 0x75a4) comes from `plane->d + (...)` and from `(...) + gFrustumPlanes[planeIdx].d`; `gFrustumPlanes[planeIdx].d + (...)` puts the sum first (24 lines). With an array element instead of a pointer, write the field as the right operand to keep the pointer form's order (LeverScout27).
