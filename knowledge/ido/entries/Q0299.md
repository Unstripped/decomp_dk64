every declared local that survives (including each gbi macro's block-local `Gfx *_g` and arrays) takes a home in declaration order; never-used locals after the last used one vanish; CSE temps get homes at the frame bottom after all named locals. A stack-offset-only diff is usually a missing named local of a repeated sub-expression or a spurious named induction pointer, not padding.
