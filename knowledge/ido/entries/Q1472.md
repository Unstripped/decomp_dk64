menu 0.c carve (2026-11-18 static-data unit): `s16 D_800330D8` at 0x800330D8 is followed by `Vtx D_800330E0[20]` at 0x800330E0, a 6-byte gap instead of the 2-byte 4-alignment gap of other .data objects (Q0395, Q0913): Vtx is a union whose long long member forces 8-byte alignment. A gap before a Vtx array is padding, not a hidden object.
