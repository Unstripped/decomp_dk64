`x / 2.0` becomes `mul.d d, x, 0.5` with the constant second, so it cannot replace a source `0.5 * x` (constant first): in global/126260 commandApplyToLight `factor / 2.0 * factor` scores 1, only `mul.d $f4,$f0,$f6` against the target's `$f6,$f0` at 0x41c.
