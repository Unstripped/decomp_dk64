In a compare between a variable and a field load, a narrow (u8) local goes through a code-free widening op, so the field load is numbered first and becomes the first operand. 32-bit variables, parameters included, stay plain and come first. Evidence: 12E800 enemyAggroStateUpdate 97a9f3.
