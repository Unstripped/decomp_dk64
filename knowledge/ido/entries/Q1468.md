Converting rodata GLOBAL_ASM labels to C (2026-11-18 static-data unit: global 136820, A2F10, 7CA80, ACDC0, 104F0, EFDC0, 100180; boss 0, 52B0, 6240, 9230, 9C60, A7A0, B100, C920): definitions with their natural size reproduce every splat stride. IDO aligns file-scope const aggregates in .rodata to 4 bytes whatever their size or element alignment (unlike .bss, where arrays of 8+ bytes align 8, Q0395):

- strings `const char X[N]`, N = length + NUL: in 136820 D_80760048[10] at 0x80760048 is followed by D_80760054 at 0x80760054, and D_80760054[13] is not 8-aligned; in A2F10 "K."[3] at 0x8075A440 is followed by 0x8075A444;
- boss scripts `const u8 X[N]` ending at their last opcode: in boss 0.c D_80035C00[18] is followed by D_80035C14 at +0x14, D_80035C3C[6] by D_80035C44 at +0x8 (sizes 5-25 all match);
- structs of 1/2-byte elements: 104F0 `char[30]` then `MenuHighScoreEntry[5]` (30 bytes) twice at a +0x20 stride; EFDC0 a 10-byte struct with a 12-byte stride; 100180 a 0x3A-byte struct with a 0x3C stride.

A file's last object's trailing padding is section padding (A2F10 "KONG" [5], 8-byte splat stride; 9C60's last 7 zeros). So drop splat's `.align 2` padding (and a script's zeros after its final opcode) from explicit sizes; tools/fdiff.sh compares .rodata bytes and confirms.
