In eepromValidateSaveSlots (global/104F0.c, 37b7c4), a flag local `ok` was reused for a second, separate CRC check, which also left a dead `ok = 0`. The fix gives the second role its own local, `u32 globalOk`. It takes the spare pad word, so the frame stays at 152. With the two roles split, the first role's uopt priority rises to 13.3, above the stored value of 11.0, and `ok` keeps s0. This is the integer-flag counterpart to Q0739. There, reusing a local merged live ranges and changed register choice. Here, separating the roles is what wins the callee-saved register. Under Q0400, a new local normally costs a frame word. Check for an unused pad word before you expect the frame size to change. Merge b670a4bc, record 7aab1f3a, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackeepromCopySlot".
