a constant that lives in the function's own `.s` (`dlabel D_8075E9D0` inside `func_8071C914.s`) must be a C literal of the right type (`1.07`); `extern f64 D_8075E9D0` gives identical code but the `.rodata` section differs. Repeated literals are separate entries: `0.35f` written three times gives the target's three labels `D_8075E950`/`54`/`58` (`func_8071B1AC`).
