Seen under IDO 7.1 -g in 140600 `rebuildLowPassCoefficients`. Reading a member of a struct local folds the address into the load: `lwc1 fN,0x30(sp)`. Reading an element of an array local first computes the base, `addiu t5,sp,0x30`, and then loads `lwc1 fN,0(t5)`. When the target shows the two-instruction shape, the original local was an array, here `f32 numCoeffs[3]`. That is why the struct view and the punned casts did not match. Indexing `f32[3]` arrays in for loops matched with fdiff=0.
