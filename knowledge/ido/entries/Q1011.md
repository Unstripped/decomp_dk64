Wrapping a loop in a natural `if` with the same condition as its entry test is not equivalent to a dead read: the guard is likely CSE'd with the loop's own rotated guard and adds nothing to the competing range.

Evidence: vsprintf (boot/3D20.c): `if (elen < width)` around the e-arm zero-pad loop is natural but only reaches 155 (not 190) and does not move the exp/prec>0 race that the dead-read trio moves; the permuter's best (290) is again a dead read of d.
