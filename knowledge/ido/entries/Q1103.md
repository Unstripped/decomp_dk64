Same unit/function: a `?:` around a `||` term adds one join block to every range spanning it (`((fneg2 || plus || space) ? 1 : 0)` in elen fixes the exp/prec>0 s3/s4 race, 190 -> 157) but materialises 0/1 by branches (move a1,zero / b / li a1,1) instead of the sltu chain, so it cannot replace a dead-block steer. See also Q0895.
