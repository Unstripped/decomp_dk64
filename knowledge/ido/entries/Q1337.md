(uopt regalloc; see Q1155, Q0763, Q1067 and the 2026-11-17f 126260 compute_save rule) A parameter read after a call-containing loop through a local copy that still holds it (`head = list`) is copy-propagated back to the parameter. The copy-propagated range charges no register save/reload across the call, so the parameter stays in its argument register. Reading the parameter directly charges that cost and leaves it uncoloured, reloaded from its home. Evidence: adjsave 2.20 (coloured a0, saved to 64(sp) at 0x60c) vs -4.0 (split 5/160, `lw t8,64(sp)` before the 0x658 test).
