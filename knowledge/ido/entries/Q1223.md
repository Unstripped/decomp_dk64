Supersedes Q0816's "p[i].f ... 85 lines" (measured with an element-pointer local kept for other fields) and narrows Q0813 ("only &p[0] + i gives base-first") to an address held in a local. In a loop, with `entry = &p[i]` / `p + i` in a local the strength-reduced pointer starts offset-first (addu dst, idx*size, p); with every access written `p[i].f` and no pointer local, uopt merges them into one induction pointer starting base-first (`addu s1,a0,t1` at 0x110 in global/7A510 actorCollisionProcessHit), and a second `p[i].f` read after an aliasing store reloads off that register (`lbu t8,8(s1)` at 0x184). All fields `interactions[i].f` and no entry: fdiff 0; `(interactions + i)->f` everywhere 1; entry kept + entry->phase 40.
