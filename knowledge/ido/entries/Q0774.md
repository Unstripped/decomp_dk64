A callee that returns an `lbu`-loaded byte without masking compiles identically whether declared `u8` or `s32`, so its own match cannot fix the return type. Its callers differ: with a `u8` prototype a caller may keep an `andi`-narrowed copy of the result, and a narrowed copy held across calls costs an extra 8-byte home. When callers in different files need different widths, the original source declared the callee with different return types in different files; use a per-file declaration rather than a cast.

Evidence: mapGetHubWorldIndex (global/3C10.c, no andi) matches with u8 or s32 return; with u8 global/36880.c keeps `andi s3,v0,0xff` at 0x160, and actorCutsceneDispatchEvents (global/8F4B0.c) grows its frame 0x68 -> 0x70. Details: .scratch/HackactorCutsceneDispatchEvents/header-request.md.
