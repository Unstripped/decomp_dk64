`for (i = 0; i < 0x28; i++) D_807F6960[i].unk0 = 0;` over a 0x10-byte struct is unrolled 4x by `-O2` into `addiu v1,v1,0x40; sw zero,-0x30/-0x20/-0x10(v1); bne v1,v0; sw zero,-0x40(v1)` with the pointer in `$v1` and the end-address constant in `$v0`; the preheader is `lui v1(hi start); lui v0(hi end); addiu v0(lo end); addiu v1(lo start)`. The same 12 instructions from a hand-written pointer `for (;;)`/`while (1)`/`do {} while (1)` + `break` give the mirrored registers and `addiu` order, and no such form flips them (all 24 store orders, `u8`/`u32`/`void *` pointers, casts, return types, chained and comma stores). Pointer loops with a real condition (`do {} while (p != end)`, `for (; p != end; p++)`, `while (p != end)`) instead unroll 4x into a much larger loop, so they cannot be the target. A swapped `$v0`/`$v1` pair in a 12-instruction clear loop therefore points at the counted index form (`func_8065051C`, global 54150.c).
