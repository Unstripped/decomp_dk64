Consecutive bit-clear operations on the same struct field (`a->unk6A &= ~8; a->unk6A &= ~0x400;`) are folded by IDO into `0xFBF7` with one dead-store-eliminated `sh`. Re-accessing the field via an explicit byte-offset cast (`*(u16*)((u8*)a + 0x6A) &= ~0x400;`) prevents alias analysis from folding the stores, preserving both distinct read-modify-write stores.
