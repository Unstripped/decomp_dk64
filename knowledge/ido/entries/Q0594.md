When an `s32` struct field is passed straight to a `u16` parameter, IDO emits a single `lhu` of the field's low halfword (offset +2) instead of `lw` + `andi`. So a pun `*(u16 *)((u8 *)a + 0x5A)` reading the low half of `actorType` is just `f(a->actorType)` when `f` takes `u16`. Evidence: `playerStateSetLaunchVelocity` calling `playerStatePlayAnimationByDamageType(gPlayerSlots[i].queuedActor->actorType)`; fdiff 0.

The same holds for `s16` parameters: an `s32` global passed to an `s16` parameter loads its low half with one `lh` at +2 (`modelSlotsLoadMap`). Narrow locals behave the same way (3C10, round 6): `((T *)&g)[n]`, the low half or byte of a big-endian `s32` global assigned to a narrow local, can be written as plain `g`. The same holds for a signed narrow cast: `(s8)actor->chunkIndex` on an `s16` field loads only the low byte with `lb` at offset +1 (109660, cleanup round 10).
