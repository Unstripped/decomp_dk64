When an `s32` struct field is passed straight to a `u16` parameter, IDO emits a single `lhu` of the field's low halfword (offset +2) instead of `lw` + `andi`. So a pun `*(u16 *)((u8 *)a + 0x5A)` reading the low half of `actorType` is just `f(a->actorType)` when `f` takes `u16`. Evidence: `playerStateSetLaunchVelocity` calling `playerStatePlayAnimationByDamageType(gPlayerSlots[i].queuedActor->actorType)`; fdiff 0.

The same holds for `s16` parameters: an `s32` global passed to an `s16` parameter loads its low half with one `lh` at +2 (`modelSlotsLoadMap`).
