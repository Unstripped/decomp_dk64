In boot/3D20 vsprintf the tagged PUTC macro's `do { ... } while (0)` (d0cc22) and the in-loop dead test e415e5 steer by adding a no-code block inside the pad loops (Q1174). Moving one statement into the `for` increment as the second block does not do the same: `for (; width-- > 0; count++) *buf++ = '0';` 62, `for (; width-- > 0; buf++) { count++; *buf = '0'; }` 59, store-first 58 at the numeric zero pad; at the e-arm zero pad `for (; elen < width--; count++)` 154 (42 with the trio's other two parts kept). The step block merges into the loop body, so a for-increment is no stand-in for a do-while(0) block.
