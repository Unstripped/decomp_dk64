actorSub140TransformTriangles (global/70FD0.c) hoists -1 into $s7 (`li s7,-1`) and the target compares with `bne s7,v0`. With a `u32 boneOffset` local and the byte-offset address `(Mtx *)(((boneOffset >> 6) << 6) + (u8 *)mtx)`, every compare spelling (`boneOffset == -1`, `-1 == boneOffset`, `(s32)boneOffset == -1`, `== 0xFFFFFFFF`) differs only in that operand order, and only the tagged `(boneOffset ^ 0) == -1` matched (round 34). Round 35 removed the steer: reading `curr->boneOffset[j] == -1` directly (no local) and writing the address as `&mtx[curr->boneOffset[j] >> 6]` matches. Try the natural address and drop the copy local before spelling the compare.
