In menuRenderOptionDescription (menu/E550.c, commits 4f8c03/bc74f9/cd8c4b, merge 333200cc, ledger fc8faef2, base ba4c85bb) a stack home was being forced with the FAKE escape `actor = &off`. It was really the split live range of one reused local. When the tile-loop screen y and the description y are a single `yo` local (5th declared, home sp+0xD4), uopt colours its loop range t0. It then splits the range that crosses hudDrawScaledText into the home: `sw t0,212(sp)` at 0x93c and `lw` at 0x978. Dropping the separate local also frees the slot that the block-local `_g` of the gDPSetCombine macro needs. The macro then replaces the hand-written packet steer at frame 0xE8 (see Q0880). The final `yo += fontHeight * 0.5` reproduces the t6/t7/a2/t8 ring at 0x990-0x9c8. Rule: before you keep an `&local` escape steer for a stack home, try merging locals of the same role into one variable.
