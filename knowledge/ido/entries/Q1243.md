Supersedes Q1083's explanation ("a constant s32 flag keeps one register; narrow types or an initializer copy-propagate it"). In global/3C10 mapSaveSlotAndTransition a literal 1 stored to an s8 global and to a u8 field gives two typed constant nodes ({1|0} and {1|1}, Q0449): each is used once in one block, adjsave 0, uncoloured, so ugen emits `li t2,1` and `li t6,1`. With both stores unsigned they share one node across bb39 (`sb gPlayerStateSaved` 0xee0) and bb42 (`sb inUse` 0xf18), adjsave 0.5, coloured a2 (`li a2,1` 0xed8). The s32 carrier of Q1083 only worked because propagation gave both stores the same signed node; the fix retyped the store-only flag global to u8 (extern in 3C10.c and CC800.c) with literal TRUE stores. Traces: uopt -l5 of the base, the literal s8 and the u8 versions.
