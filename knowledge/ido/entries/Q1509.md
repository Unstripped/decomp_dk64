Extends Q1363 point 1 (no declared width explains a per-test `lbu home+3`; related Q0569, Q0935, Q1229): an explicit mask does not either. For an s32 parameter passed on the stack, `(param & 0xFF) != 0` compiles to a `lw` of the whole word plus `andi`, never an `lbu` of the low byte (0x157), and the masked value becomes its own candidate coloured into a saved register.

Evidence: global/7BBD0.c actorCollisionResolveSurfaceHit (unit CleanactorCollisionResolveSurfaceHitc, ledger d0dab3d8), s32 parameter through a PROTO_SKIP stand-in:
- mask in both tests: `lw s1,340(sp)` + `andi t8,s1,0xff` at 0x84c (score 95);
- mask in the second test only: `lw s2,340(sp)` (score 122).
