In heapRegisterSurvivor, the target's `addiu v1,v1,-16` at 0x1280 is b's second definition (`b = (HeapBlock *)payload - 1`). It is kept because b stays live into the `if (1)` block (site 6c7f1e). as1 schedules it after the tag store and rewrites the store's offset from 0xB to -5. Spellings in which b folds into the store (one-line body, payload local) emit no addiu (score 6-11). The `if (1)` scope stays FAKE.
