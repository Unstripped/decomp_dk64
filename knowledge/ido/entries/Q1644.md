Check the clamp shape in the target before trying MAX/MIN (Q0988). In csCmdShowRemainingDigits the target is `subu v1,t7,t8` (0x69d8), `bgez v1,69e8` (0x69dc), `move v1,zero` (0x69e4), `move s2,v1` (0x69e8). That is a ONE-arm clamp that stores into the value's own register, then copies it to the destination at the join. It is a named variable clamped in place, then assigned.

`v = MAX(0, goals - counts)` and `v = goals - counts; t = MAX(0, v);` both score 105: the ternary's arm stores go straight into the destination (`b` + `move s2,zero` + `move s2,v1`), one instruction more. Q0988's MAX rewrite applies only when the target stores in both arms. The same one-arm shape occurs in bossSpawnerUpdate (0xe60-0xe7c) and hudPauseMenuIconUpdate (0x4738-0x4760).
