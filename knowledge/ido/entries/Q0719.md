menuCheckMapTransitionOnLoad (global/117190.c, site 318113): `*(u16 *)((u8 *)D_8075E5C2 + i * 4)` and `D_8075E5C2[i * 2]` give identical uopt colouring; the array form adds two uncoloured isop candidates (umpy+uixa) and that alone shifts the ugen temp ring (sb t2 vs t3 at 0x98c). The fix is a 4-byte entry type for D_8075E5C0.
