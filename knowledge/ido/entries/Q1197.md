In global/42630 csCmdExec the target stores the s16 `csId` argument word-wide and reloads it halfword-wide per iteration (sw a2,208(sp) / lh t5,210(sp) at 0x3694), which looks like a mixed-width home. Typing the formal as `s32` (PROTO_SKIP_csCmdExec stand-in) does not make it memory-resident: without the `dm = &csId` escape 637, with `(s16)csId` casts at the 0x2D/0x76 compares 787, with the escape kept 22. The mixed-width access is the address escape's effect, not a parameter-width lever.
