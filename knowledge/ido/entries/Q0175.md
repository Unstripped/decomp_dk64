`&D_807F6000[hashTableLookup(id)].unk28` emits `addu a0, off, base` (offset first); writing the array field as a `u8[]` that decays emits `base, off` (`func_8063FA48` cases 0x8E/0x8F).

`p = &s.u.arr[0];` and `p = s.u.arr;` also compile differently: the `&arr[0]` form gave `camUpdate` a frame 8 bytes larger than the decay form.
