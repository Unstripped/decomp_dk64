The negative byte offset `(u8 *)D_807F5D6C + -(a * 4)` in `camPathCommandDispatch` (2C910) can be written against the real handle array: `&D_807F5D70[-1 - a]` reproduces the target `sll; negu; addu` exactly, because IDO folds the `-1` into the LO16 addend (`D_807F5D70-4`, which is the split label `D_807F5D6C`), so the instruction words match and the split label can be retired (RELOC-EQUIV, raw words differ only in that addend). The order of the terms is load-bearing: `&D_807F5D70[-a - 1]`, `&D_807F5D70[-(a + 1)]`, `D_807F5D70 + (-a - 1)`, `&D_807F5D70[-a] - 1` and `D_807F5D70 - a - 1` all fail fdiff. This supersedes the conclusion of Q0621 that the byte-offset spelling is required.
