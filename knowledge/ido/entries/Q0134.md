rodata doubles with the low mantissa bits zero (`0x4060666660000000`) are `(f64)` of an `f32` value; write the exact decimal (`131.1999969482421875`) because `(f64)65.8f` rounds differently. `x / 2` with an integer literal emits `div.s`; `x / 2.0f` becomes `mul.s 0.5`. Reciprocal constants are spelled in source (`* 0.033333335f`).
