every referenced declared local gets a home slot even when it lives in a register (unreferenced ones get none); spill temps are placed after the named locals, so a spill-slot offset is fixed by counting named locals' bytes (dropping one 8-byte `f64` local moved a spill from `0x68` to `0x74`, `func_8061DBD4`).
