In C0EE0 enemyGridStateStep (047f57) the target does dy's subtraction (subu into dy) before the dx narrowing records and dy's sll/sra after them, so `dy = (s16)dy;` must be its own statement. Merging it into dy's assignment makes ugen emit dy's sll/sra before dx's sll, which swaps t6/t4 at 0x74c/0x75c (4 rows). See guide ido-uopt-statics-and-live-ranges#6. 92ac80's `(u16)` is Q0374's as1-deleted mask: without it every later ring register is one slot behind, while s16 casts/locals land one ahead.
