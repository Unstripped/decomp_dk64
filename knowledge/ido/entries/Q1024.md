A redundant `(x & 0xFF)` mask on a u8 formal burns one ugen expression register (as1 later deletes the `and 255` and renames the `lbu`). A named u8 RESULT local, `u8 r = formal & MASK; if (r)`, reproduces that effect; an s32 local does not, and a u8 copy of the formal is copy-propagated away. The local needs its own home word, so it matches only where a named home is free.

Evidence: dmaReadAssetDecompress (global/FE40.c): it matched after dropping `size` (its re-read is per iteration in the target) and declaring the new local in its slot. Frame accounting: cfe `def Mmt len 32` vs uopt `len 40`; the two uopt temp words (0x40/0x44) have no code, and any added named word grows the frame by 8.
