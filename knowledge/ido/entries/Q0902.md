In global/FCCD0.c, a u8 argument passed to the unprototyped (K&R) `slotQueueGetAddress` is promoted through an `(int)` conversion. uopt treats that conversion as the same value as the u8 passed to `s32` parameters, so the call reuses that register or spill instead of doing a fresh `lbu`. In `slotQueueInit` the reload is `lbu v1,155(sp); move a0,v1`. In `slotQueueAddValue` it is `lw a0,0x28(sp)`. Writing `(u32)i` or `(unsigned)` skips the conversion and uses the raw byte load. In `slotQueueInit` this gives `lbu a0,155(sp); sw a0,120(sp)`, matching the target at 0x48c/0x4a0. In `slotQueueAddValue` the post-call `lbu a0,87(sp)` then feeds both the 0x306 index and the call. The casts `(u8)`, `(unsigned char)`, `(u16)`, `(s32)` and `(int)` all keep the shared conversion. Treat `(u32)` as a stand-in for a missing prototype, unless the callee really takes u32 (look for an `andi` at its 0x0). The real fix is an ANSI `s32 slotQueueGetAddress(u8)`: `slotQueueAddValue` then matches with the plain argument, and `slotQueueInit` grows from 0x750 to 0x754. Q0808 gives a natural alternative: an s32 local assigned `(u8)i`, which retired guard ab9e8a (director-accepted).
