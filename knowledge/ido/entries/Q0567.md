In IDO 5.3, assigning 0.0f to a float variable may reuse an existing 0.0f register from an earlier block. Writing an integer literal 0 ('x = 0;') natively forces IDO to emit 'mtc1 zero, $f0' as a fresh zero register without referencing or reusing existing float registers, avoiding hacks like assigning 0.0 (double).
