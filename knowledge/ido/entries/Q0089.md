an `s32 pad; s32 idx;` pair where `idx` is only assigned inside a loop makes IDO load it from `0x38($sp)` after the hoisted `addiu` of a global address, matching the original prologue; an explicit `idx = sp38;` scheduled the load first (`renderDepthSortedTargets`).
