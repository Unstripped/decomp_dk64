In `eepromLoadPlayerData` (104F0, round 5) the target schedules the `lui`/`addiu` of `gPlayerInventoryTable` into the prologue. One line, a store to offset 0x2FC made directly through `gPlayerInventoryTable` (a KongInventory row), reproduces that and makes three hacks unnecessary together: a one-line header, a u32 cast and a dead test.
