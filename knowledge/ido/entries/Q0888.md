In `bossSpawnerUpdate` (boss/DAA0.c, 3e88fe; unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackbossSpawnerUpdate2", ledger 6570909b, base cf3a64b4), placing a dead guard (Q0561) right after `v = E;` ends the basic block there. v's uses in the next block then become late copy substitutions with late candidate numbers. Because spilltemps slots are handed out top-down in candidate order (Q0521), a temp that is live across a call gets a lower slot: 0x44 instead of 0x48. Without the guard, same-block store forwarding (Q0499) produces a single early candidate, and that candidate takes the first slot. The traces show this: ucvtl{and} is 443 with the guard and ucvtl{level} is 427. Writing `i <<= 4` gives cvtl(i) 424. The target offsets are 0xeec/0xf3c. Use the guard's position to control whether a substitution candidate is numbered early or late, which in turn decides its slot.
