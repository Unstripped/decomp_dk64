In `modelEmittersUpdate` (was `actorUpdateEntryEmitters`), a dead `dx = modelId;` between a float sum and its `(f64)` compare stops uopt folding the sum into the `cvt.d.s` compare: the sum becomes its own register candidate (`$f14`) where natural C uses the expression temp (`$f6`). Kept as a tagged FAKE; the permuter only swapped it for an equivalent dead store.

**2026-11-16d correction (HackmodelEmittersUpdate2, ledger c179fcb3; HackeepromSaveRecords6, merge 72ea5f55):** a uopt -l 6 / -l 5 trace with and without the kill shows no candidate, priority or colour change for the sum: the dead store only blocks uopt's copy propagation of the sum tree into the compare (cand 43 ucvt{269} vs cand 46 ucvt{1088}, 68EA0.c @ ef40eb4d). Accumulating the sum in steps (`sum = a; sum = b + sum`, `+=`) does not block it (3 lines each). The same kill works with a REAL store to an operand between the local's def and its single use (Q0499, Q1008): eepromSaveRecords' `offset = sector << 3;` between `b = ...` and the CRC store replaced its dead `offset = 0;` (position-sensitive: elsewhere 66/125).
