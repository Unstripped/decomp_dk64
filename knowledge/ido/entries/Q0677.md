In `modelEmittersUpdate` (was `actorUpdateEntryEmitters`), a dead `dx = modelId;` between a float sum and its `(f64)` compare stops uopt folding the sum into the `cvt.d.s` compare: the sum becomes its own register candidate (`$f14`) where natural C uses the expression temp (`$f6`). Kept as a tagged FAKE; the permuter only swapped it for an equivalent dead store.
