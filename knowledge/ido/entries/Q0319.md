`if (!v) {}` is an IL-only use that raises `v`'s adjsave. It only matters in a block after a call killed the register copy (otherwise copy-propagation folds it); `if (!v) {}` before AND after a use inside an arm makes a spilled variable reload from its home slot; `if (global) {}` before a loop stops uopt hoisting that global's address into an s-register (frees it for a loop-bound constant); an `if (p->f) {}` between two calls re-colours a stack-home pointer and adds the target's redundant `sw` in the second call's delay slot. Several dummies interact; prune old ones when a better one is found.
