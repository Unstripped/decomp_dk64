a register-only local declared before the last memory-resident local still reserves a word (u16 two bytes, u8/u16 pack below; u8[12] 4-aligned); declared after, nothing. Unused locals reserve nothing. Nested call results `f3(f2(f1(x)))` each reserve a word (uopt M vars): splitting the chain frees them. A macro expression instead of a named pointer local saves a word. Constant-valued locals passed as gbi macro args add named homes without code, which fixed a 630-insn texture loader's frame.
