uopt strength-reduces a double `x / 2.0` to a multiply by 0.5, but its constant is a separate `5.0E-1` candidate (visible in `uopt_trace -l 5`) that does not join the web of a source `0.5` literal. Choosing between `/ 2.0` and `* 0.5` therefore splits or joins a constant range without changing the instructions. Chosen only for that effect it is a codegen-chosen respelling (Q0816), not a natural fix.

Evidence: commandApplyToLight (global/126260.c): with ambient `* 0.5` the ambient piece joins the `0.5 * base * base` web (hoisted load at 0x578, $f16); with `/ 2.0` it is separate (load at 0x7ec, $f14). This replaced a dead `if (dist_d == 0.5) {}`.
