File src/global/103AB0.c, gfxDrawTexturedTileGrid. 89672b and 732a72 FIXED: the "redundant copies" are now per-role locals `left`/`top` (the cell edges passed to vtxSetPosTex ob[0]/ob[1]), read at EVERY use of that edge, including the vtxCallback calls. Other locals were renamed with rename.py: posX/posY (advancing cursors), stepX/stepY, right/bottom. FAKE 3 -> 1. - DIRECTOR RULING turn 132: accepted as per-role locals. Each names a distinct role (cell edge vs advancing cursor) and is used at every use of that role. Unlike the 12E800 `near = cond` hybrid, it adds no reuse and no extra steer. - The lead checked the condition: posX/posY are only modified in the for headers (`posX += stepX`, `posY += stepY`) and the row init, never inside the cell body, so left/top equal the cursor at every edge use. 1. NEW (uopt substitution; related Q0888, Q0521, guide ido-uopt-temp-slots): if an alias copy (`c = u;`) is substituted back to the original, its plain reads (call arguments, especially unprototyped ones) are interchangeable with reads of the original. So moving every use of that role onto the copy costs nothing and turns a FAKE alias into a per-role local. Its codegen effect comes only from expressions built on it (here the (s16) conversions), whose candidate bits come late through substitution. Limit: moving an EXPRESSION operand onto the copy does change its bit (`right = (s16)(left + stepX)` full=735, bottom full=110). Evidence: the callbacks reading colS/rowT score full=0 alone and together (fdiff 0).
