In global/ACDC0 hudPauseMenuUpdate a dedicated credits-flag local (`= hubWorld == 8`, then `= menuGetTimerFlag() != 0`) declared `s8` or `u8` gets `sll 0x18`/`sra 0x18` (or the u8 mask) after the `sltiu`/`sltu` at 0x814/0x82c (full=171 s8, 170 u8): IDO does not know a 0/1 compare result already fits. A 0/1 role local that the target keeps unextended in a register must be word-sized (s32); narrow widths only pack for locals loaded from narrow fields.
