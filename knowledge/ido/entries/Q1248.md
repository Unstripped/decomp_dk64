In global/936B0 vehicleUpdateMotion the FP field-load candidates follow Q1179's formula: waterY with 4 uses scores 12/4, speedY with 5 uses 15/5; ties go to the lower candidate number. A store to a may-aliasing field between a compare and the reuse of the loaded value (`speedY = ...` before `y = waterY`) forces a reload and removes that region from the candidate (the tagged block split 6c8d63 keeps waterY in $f2 at 0x1370/0x14e8, speedY $f0 at 0x13b8).
