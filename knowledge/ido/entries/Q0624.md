The target of `raceGetPathDistanceBetweenTrackers` (A960) has three real `xor` instructions for a value swap, so `a ^= b; b ^= a; a ^= b;` is the original code, not a hack. A temp-variable swap gives three `move`s. Do not tag xor swaps that the asm shows.
