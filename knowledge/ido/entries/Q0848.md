`u8 lap = tracker->lap;` with `s8 lap` at RaceParticipant 0x37 compiles to `lbu` with no sign extension: cfe folds the `lb` plus the u8 truncation, so a u8 union alias of a signed field is not needed just to get `lbu` (the alias was dropped from race.h, a93fcc82). Contrast Q0620 (an `extern s8` global stored into a u8 field keeps `lb`). Evidence: raceDrawHudText (race/7BD0.c).
