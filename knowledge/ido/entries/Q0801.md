A single-use local declared inside a block takes no function-frame slot ahead of the other locals, and its assignment makes ugen emit that argument's load before the rest of the call's argument setup, which decides as1's delay-slot pick. A target that loads a later argument first, with the frame of the local-free version, points to a block-scoped temporary.
