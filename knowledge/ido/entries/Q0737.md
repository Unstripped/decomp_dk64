uopt puts the lower-numbered operand of a commutative umpy on the left. In playerStateHoldPickup (global/D5E10.c) an embedded assignment `/(d = 0.15)` renumbers the double constants (3.0 goes 1152 -> 301) and flips the mul.d operands of a different product (trace hp_*.l6).

Corrected 2026-11-14 (HackplayerStateHoldPickup, ledger 8b10b243): the phase is cfe, not uopt numbering. For a commutative FP multiply uopt keeps the operand order cfe emitted. cfe normally evaluates a variable-index subscript (`ixa`) operand before a lighter field chain whatever the source order; when a sibling operand has a side effect (an assignment or a call in the divisor, `/ (baseScale = 0.15)`), cfe evaluates it first (`swp` before `div`) and emits the rest in source order, giving `mul.d $f4,$f18,$f10` at 0x960c. The renumbered constants are a side product; a call divisor shows the same source-order emission. A constant-index member array (`scale[0]`) still counts as lighter.
