a caller that hands its own argument on in `$a0` needs the callee declared unprototyped (`void f();`); a function-pointer cast gives `jalr` instead of `jal` (`func_80678CC8`, `actorTableRemove`). Unprototyped also lets one TU call `f` with different argument counts.
