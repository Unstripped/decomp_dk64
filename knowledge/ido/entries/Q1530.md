13980 schedMain case 0x29F performs its empty `osSendMesg(...) == -1` test (`li at,-1` 0x4f0, `bnel v0,at,0x250` 0x4f4, `b 0x250`
0x4fc) because its fall-through is the case's jump to the loop head; adding one statement after it (`D_807F04D0 = 0;` before the
break) so it falls through to the next instruction removes both. In schedHandleRetrace both empty tests fall through (after `jal
osRecvMesg` 0x7c0 and `jal osSendMesg` 0x810), so only the hoisted constant survives (`li s2,-1` 0x7b4). Its two
`if (msg == (OSMesg)-1) {}` steers stay FAKE (director ruling R2: merged forms add compares the target never performs).
