Resolved in `ab63494e` (2026-11-08): the final 890-instruction function matches the ROM. The first x-coordinate expression is:

```c
off = (u16)(off * 4) + (s16)(s64)(s32)((f32)fa * entry->unk0);
```

The real source spells `entry` as the existing byte-offset `HudTextEntry *` expression. `off` remains `s32`; the redundant casts carry a trailing FAKE tag. The following statement adds `entry->unkC * 4` and narrows the result to `s16`. Both operands above preserve their low 16 bits, their sum cannot overflow `s32`, and the final coordinate is unchanged wherever the original float-to-integer conversion is defined. Native-C ASan/UBSan smoke passed 9,175,040 combinations: every signed-halfword base offset, 20 positive/negative/fractional and signed-word-boundary translations, seven origin offsets, both entry indices and both fade signs.

The decisive ugen sequence is:

- `mul t3,s0,4; and t4,t3,65535`: the scaled coordinate ends in `t4`.
- Float conversion uses trunc scratch `t5`, then `mfc1 t9`. The `(s64)` conversion allocates the even/odd `t6/t7` pair (`move t7,t9; sra t6,t9,31`).
- Narrowing copies the low word to `t8`, then emits `sll t3,t8,16; sra t5,t3,16`. The sum is `addu s0,t4,t5`, releasing the two operand temporaries in the required order.
- as1 removes the redundant mask, sign extension and moves, forwarding the surviving `sll` into `t4` and `mfc1` into `t5`. Every later scratch name then matches.

Reversing the original sum and casting `fa` to `f32` first reduced full 105 to 8, but left those two destination registers swapped. The paired/narrow conversion resolves them without changing the loop header.
