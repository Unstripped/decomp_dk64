A lone empty statement `;` at the end of an `if` arm adds one uopt basic block under IDO 5.3 -O2 (63 blocks against 62 in `actorCollisionProcessHit`). That extra block reproduced the effect of a dead `if (G) {}` read exactly (fdiff 0), but only at the end of the inner arm or of the enclosing arm; other positions failed. So a dead-read guard kept for colouring can be a block-split lever rather than a use of the global. The ordinary-C fix there was a `switch` with two cases over the queued event, which supplies the same block split without a dead read.
