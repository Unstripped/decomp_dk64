HacktextDrawAnimated (unit "2026-11-16c: cleanup HacktextDrawAnimated", merge 5214ab53), src/global/1025E0.c textDrawAnimated (cfe/uopt; extends Q0878): a u8 local tested as a truth value (`while (ch)`, `while ((ch = p[i++]))`) is compared to 0 as the variable (uneq{ch},0); `ch != 0` makes cfe compare a converted copy ucvt(ch), a separate uopt live range that can take another register than the variable or a call's precoloured argument register (adds a move, reshuffles v1/a1/a2). With u8 ch + shared (s16, u8) prototype: `while (ch != 0)` 890 (bit 61 ucvt{ch} gets v1, move v1,a1 at 0x8b0/0x1378), `while ((ch = ...) != 0)` 890, `while (ch)` fdiff 0.
