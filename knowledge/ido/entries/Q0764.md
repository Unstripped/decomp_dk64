A local assigned a constant and passed on (`origin = 0;` then used in macro arithmetic) is only propagated by uopt: expressions such as `width - origin + 1` and `origin << 2` stay real isops (usub/ushl) in the IL, whereas a literal 0 folds them away in cfe. The surviving isops change which gbi-macro temps get spill slots, so a constant local can be load-bearing for the frame even when every register matches. Where Q0145's inlining fails, keep one constant local feeding the arguments.

Evidence: dlAppendTextureLoad (global/1135F0.c, FAKE 4 -> 2): `origin` passed as both uls and ult of gDPLoadTextureTile: case 1 spills only its SetTileSize gfx temp (sp+0x74); with literal 0s the LoadSync/LoadTile temps spill instead (sp+0x78/0x74) and the frame shrinks 0xc8 -> 0xb0. One constant local feeding both arguments is enough; the earlier `lrt = height` copy was only a cfe home word and was replaced by an untagged pad.
