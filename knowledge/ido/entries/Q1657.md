This extends Q1451/Q0602. In bossSpawnerUpdate, four reused locals (loop counter / step threshold, case-1 offsets / light position, case-7 counter / repeat limit) each steer their own registers independently. A frame-kept per-role body (one local per role, frame 0x80, every named slot at its target offset) still recolours every role: loop counter a3 for t0 at 0x80-0x150, thresholds v0 for t0 at 0x640-0x798, repeat limit v1 for a1, case-1 offsets f2/f12 for f12/f0. That is 89 lines. Adding the 3e88fe dead guard back on that body gives 437: the guard only helps while the locals stay shared. A permuter zero from the split body re-merges the repeat limit into the case-7 counter, which is the steer itself.
