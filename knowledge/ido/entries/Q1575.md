ACDC0 hudPauseMenuIconUpdate case 6: `effect->y = mathSinF(ang) * 10.0f + (...)` matches (fdiff 0) while `effect->y += mathSinF(ang) * 10.0f` adds one frame word (spill temps move down 4, score 10). Narrows Q0487: the hidden temp comes from the compound assignment, not from the call. Operand order next to the call is free: `a = f() * (b * K)` and `(b * K) * f()` compile identically.
