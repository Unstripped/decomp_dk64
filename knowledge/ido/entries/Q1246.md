In global/936B0 projectileSteerTowardTarget (base c5578e18, callee still K&R and unprototyped), passing `!x` for a K&R u8 formal as a call argument makes cfe store the boolean into a new stack temp (M -72vreg, below the last local): the instructions are identical but the frame grows 0x80 -> 0x88 and every sp offset moves +8. Writing `x == 0` creates no temp.
