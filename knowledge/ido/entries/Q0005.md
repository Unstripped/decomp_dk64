`hardware_regs: true` (all splat configs) emits names such as `*(HI_PI_STATUS_REG)` from spindisasm's `SymbolsSegment.N64HardwareRegs` table. All 131 names are defined as KSEG1 linker symbols in `undefined_syms.us.txt`, which every link (boot, global, overlays) passes with `-T`.
