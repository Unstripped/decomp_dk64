a parameter gets a callee-saved register only above an adjsave threshold (0.625 stayed in its arg register, 0.875 took `s0`); the unconstrained pass assigns in first-reference order, so copying a param into a local at the top keeps it in `a1`. Reusing one counter for two loops makes one long live range that interferes with params.
