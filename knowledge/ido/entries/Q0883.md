In cannonUpdate (global/80150.c, fixed 9f7928/2c0b1c, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackthrowableBarrelUpdate"), the x/y local-reuse FAKEs (Q0739) were standing in for a statement-order effect. FP colouring follows statement order, not only which locals share a variable. In the camera setup, assigning y before z lets separate function-scope `f32` locals dz/dx/cosV match (fdiff OK). The frame stays 0x60, the y web gets `$f2` and z gets `$f14` at 0x70a0/0x70a4, and the cosine product gets `$f2` at 0x7504. Earlier attempts had measured separate locals as 8 words off with frame 0x68, but every one of those attempts kept z before y. Rule: before keeping a local-reuse or dead-guard steer for FP colouring (Q0589), try reordering the assignment statements of the competing FP locals first, following the first-appearance ordering in Q0468.
