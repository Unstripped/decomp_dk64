A dead test used to fix the temp-register ring order does not have to open its block: any position before the store whose registers it fixes works.

Evidence: vsprintf (boot/3D20.c, site u8169d): the else-arm sign steer also matches as a dead test after `count++` inside the else arm (20 -> 0); the record fixing the t7/t9 order only has to precede the '+' store.
