In src/global/F56F0.c (`HackplayerStateWaypointAimUpdate2` cleanup, merge 8239b1cc, ledger f01e8f23, base 5c8c62b7), `playerStateWaypointAimUpdate` (14204c) passes 4 arguments to the 3-arg `playerStateQueueCallback`. The extra `a3 = &gWaypointCursor` is a value left over from 0x3434. Widening the callee doesn't help. Under IDO 5.3 -O2, an unused trailing parameter still makes the callee home it (`sw a3,0xc(sp)`). A variadic `...` definition also homes a3 and adds `addiu sp,-8`. Both variants were tried (efdc0/EFDC0_4p.c, EFDC0_var.c, fdiff 1). The extra argument therefore has to come from the caller's side, and that is blocked on views. Related to Q0486 (callee arity first). The same unit also re-confirmed Q0580 for `playerWeaponCalculateTrajectory26` (c2b7df). It matches only with no final return, so `ret` falls through in `$v0`: 0x1170 `sltu v0` / `beqz v0` -> 0x1198, 0x1188 `move v0,t8` in the `beqz t8` delay slot, 0x1198 `jr ra`. The lead verified this in the expected F56F0.o (see Q0230, Q0457).
