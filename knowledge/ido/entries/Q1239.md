Extends Q1142. In global/36880 renderModelAnimatedSlot, `{ Gfx **listsB = obj->displayListsB; gDPPipeSync(gfx++); gSPDisplayList(gfx++, osVirtualToPhysical(listsB[sel])); }` supplies the frame word the target needs after the spilled displayListsA `_g`: uopt folds `listsB + sel*4` into the existing obj+sel*4 temp at 60(sp), so the local adds the word and no load (the B list load stays `lw a0,168(t8)` in the jal delay slot); fdiff 0. A loaded `Gfx *dl = obj->displayListsB[sel]` hoists `lw a0,168` above the sync stores (10; around the B list only 14); a symmetric listsA block 7. `{ s32 _g; }` at the same position also matches, so such a local only stands in for the pad: the merge kept the tagged `{ s32 pad6; }` (1a2082) by lead ruling.
