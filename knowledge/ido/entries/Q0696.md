In an unsigned `sizeof` multiply chain the order of the operands decides which sub-product is formed first. In 67890 `actorTargetTableLoad`, `heapAlloc(gActorTargetCount * 4 * sizeof(Vtx))` does not match, while `gActorTargetCount * sizeof(Vtx) * 4`, `gActorTargetCount * 64` and `gActorTargetCount * 4 * 16` all match the target `sll 6`. In the failing form the signed `n * 4` product comes before the promotion to `size_t`, so cfe builds a different multiply tree. When replacing a shift with a size expression, put `sizeof` next to the variable.
