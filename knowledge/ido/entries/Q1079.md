Same unit, textDrawStringVtx site 2 (extends Q0462 to resets after a loop): with nothing defining count between `count / 4` and the post-loop `count = 0`, uopt hoists the reset to the loop entry (beqz delay slot `move s8,zero`, slt + beqz instead of blez t5 / addiu a3,t5,-1 at 0x1728) and count's range spans the quad loop.
