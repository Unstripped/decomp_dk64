The type of a switch selector local reaches the colouring of unrelated loop ranges: a u32 selector can leave every piece of a strength-reduced loop pointer's live range uncoloured, while s32/s16 or no local colour one piece. A dead empty `if` inside a case can have the same effect as the u32 selector.

Evidence: actorCutsceneDispatchEvents (global/8F4B0.c): with a u32 selector range 23 stays uncoloured (reload `lw t0,0x40(sp)` at 0x670); s32/s16/no local colour piece 285 with a0; the dead empty-if in case 0x1C acts like the u32 selector (21 vs 220 vs 216).
