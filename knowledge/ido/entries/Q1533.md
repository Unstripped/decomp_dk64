Minimal IDO 5.3 -O2 test (CEAE0 playerUpdateCommon site 318197): plain `&p[i].f` gives `addu v0,t7,t8` (index first); a member
array (`&p[i].a[0]`), a nested member (`&p[i].v.x`) and `&(*(p + i)).f` change nothing; a cast-like base gives `addu v0,t6,t8`; a
decayed member array (`p[i].a`) breaks the shared address calculation into two adds.
