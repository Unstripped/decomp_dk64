Extends Q1049. In bonus/2690 bonusSearchlightSeekGameUpdate, `s32 rawX = gCurrentContPad->stick_x; stickX = rawX; stickX *= 0.06f;` folds the conversion away: stickX (1581|0) leaves the register candidates and `(f32)rawX * 0.06f` becomes shared expression 1369|1 (3.0, $f16). `stickX = gCurrentContPad->stick_x; stickX *= 0.06f;` (the first assignment contains a load through a pointer) keeps the conversion in stickX's register: 2 counted definitions (5.0) and `mul.s $f2,$f2,$f10`.
