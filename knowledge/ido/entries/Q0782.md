When a callee "prototype steer" exists only to keep a constant ternary argument (`cond ? 0x8A : 0xA1`) from being narrowed into a narrow parameter, compute the ternary into a named local of the parameter's type (`s16 sfxId = cond ? 0x8A : 0xA1;`) and pass that. The local's declaration position decides whether its slot shares a word with an existing narrow local or grows the frame.

Evidence: effectSpawnJointBeam (global/89250.c, FAKE 2 -> 0): the s16 sfxId shares a word with `u8 bones[2]` when declared beside it.
