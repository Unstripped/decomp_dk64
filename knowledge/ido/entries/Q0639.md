A local prototype that passes a spare argument keeps the caller's selector live in $a1 across the call (B5EC0/B2CE0 behaviour callers, Rev16F round 16). Dropping the spare argument moves it to another register. Before treating such an argument as an m2c leftover, check whether it is what pins the register.
