This is from the same unit as the HackspawnNodeActorOutOfRangeScaled cleanup (2026-11-15). `spawnNodeSpawnFacingPoint` was fixed in 97e318 by adding a new `s16 speed` local, declared right after the first-declared `s16 yaw`. That local takes the free halfword at 68(sp), next to yaw's home at 70. Because of this, later local slots and the CSE spills stay at 48/44. Adding an s32 local anywhere in the function moves those slots and spills to 44/40 instead. A new local whose width matches its neighbour can pack into the spare halfword without changing the rest of the frame. A wider local pushes every later slot down. Before you reuse an existing variable to save a slot (as with Q0729's parameter reuse), check the callee's parameter width. Here `spawnNodeSpawnById` takes `speed` as s16. A separate local of that exact width can fit in the free space at no cost, so splitting variables as in Q0465 does not need to grow the frame.
