`forward + vx` (two variables) keeps source order (add.s $f18,$f14,$f10 at 0x3550, playerUpdateHorizontalVelocity); a computed product comes first in either order, and `v = a*b; v += var` too. So a target add.s with the spilled variable first points to `v = a*b; v = var + v;`. Likewise on f32 fields `y += vy` and `y = y + vy` differ in add.s/lwc1 operand order (arcadeUpdateBarrelEntity 0x912c/0x91f4/0x92d4).
