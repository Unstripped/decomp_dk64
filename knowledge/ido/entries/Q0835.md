Storing an s16 local into an s16 function-local static emits a plain `sh v0` (the S-variable `ustr` path), while the same store to an extern global narrows first (`sra t7; sh t7`, the `istr` of an `lda`). This extends Q0761 and is a cheap discriminator: a target that stores a narrow local into a global's address without the sll/sra pair was a function-local static.

Evidence: gfxBuildScrollingBackground (global/109660.c, 0x2cfc/0x2d04).
