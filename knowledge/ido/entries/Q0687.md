Rewriting the split labels `D_8002F054`..`D_8002F078` as fields of one symbol (`jetpacPlayer.player.*`) lets uopt share one base address, and `jetpacRocketItemUpdate` comes out 48 bytes short. Q0264's reloc-only parent+field rewrite holds for an isolated read; several labels of one block read in one function keep their own addresses.
