uopt keeps one shared widening (ucvt candidate) and spills it (`sw v0,0x38(sp)` in the jal delay slot). A `(u32)` cast on the pre-call use plus `!x` after the call removes the candidate, so the byte reloads from its home (`lbu t8,143(sp)` at 0x2434). May apply to actorCollisionResolveSurfaceHit (7BBD0) and collisionTestStaticMeshFloor (6AD90).
