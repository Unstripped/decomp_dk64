An empty guard right before a final `return;` inside an if-block is its own uopt block, which places the `.alias` reset before the epilogue jump so as1 can hoist across the next label. The natural form drops the redundant `return;` (only `break;` follows) together with the guard. Separately, a dead global read far from its uses stretches the address web until uopt leaves the address uncoloured and rematerialises `lui` per access: check that before treating such a guard as a value steer.

Evidence: arcadeUpdateDonkeyKongStage1 and arcadeUpdatePlayerEntity (arcade/0.c, FAKE 31 -> 26).
