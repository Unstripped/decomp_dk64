In `bossSpawnerUpdate` (boss/DAA0) the reuse sites `dx = randomFloat()` (a0a7fe) and `cutsceneId` as the light level (82a569) each own one spill-area word. Splitting either into its own local moves every spill home down 4 bytes, and no unused pad at any declaration position restores the layout; unlike Q0633 the slot is a spill home, not a local's home, so the reuse stays a tagged FAKE.
