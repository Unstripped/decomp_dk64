In `bossSpawnerUpdate` (boss/DAA0) the reuse sites `dx = randomFloat()` (a0a7fe) and `cutsceneId` as the light level (82a569) each own one spill-area word. Splitting either into a newly declared local moves every spill home down 4 bytes, and adding an unused pad does not restore the layout (unlike Q0633 the slot is a spill home, not a local's home). What works is turning an existing unused `s32 pad` into the split local; when two reuses need splitting, sharing one float pair between two disjoint uses (case-1 dx/dz and the light x/z) frees the second slot.

Corrected 2026-11-14 (HackbossSpawnerUpdate): the earlier claim that no pad restores the layout was wrong; a0a7fe and 82a569 are fixed this way with homes 0x48/0x44 kept.
