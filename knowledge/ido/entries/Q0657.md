In 11BC10 the K&R no-argument call `effectShrinkOffsetUpdate();` forwarded the caller's `$a1`. The caller really takes that parameter: giving `effectShrinkAndVelocityUpdate` an `s8 *isDone` parameter and passing it explicitly matches, removes the FAKE and fixes W630.
