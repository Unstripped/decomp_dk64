zbufferDepthToDistance (global/103AB0.c) keeps `mid = -1` before the loop. The only permuter zero (perm_c99c9b, 9 s) computes `mid = (lo + hi) / 2` before hi is assigned, an uninitialised read, so the preassign has no natural spelling and stays tagged.
