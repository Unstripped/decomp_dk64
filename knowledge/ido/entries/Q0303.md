`i * 0x2F0` or `i * 0x90` with a u8/masked index in a loop, or through a local copy of the global base, becomes `multu` with a hoisted constant; an unmasked `s32` index on the global gives the `sll/subu` expansion. `(u8 *)D_807F6000 + i * 0x90` gives base-first `addu`, `&D_807F6000[i]` offset-first.
