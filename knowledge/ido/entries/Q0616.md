With `p = node->unk4;` read once before a loop guarded by a count (`node->unkA > 0`), indexing `p[i]` in a `for (i = 0; i < n; i++)` keeps the pointer load above the `blez` guard and copies it to a saved register in the preheader (`animListFree`, 8CA50). `*p++` or a guarded do-while instead sinks the load into the loop block and leaves a nop. If the target loads the pointer before the guard, write the indexed `for`; if the load sits after the guard, use the pointer-increment or do-while form.
