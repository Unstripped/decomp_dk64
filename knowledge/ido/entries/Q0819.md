Pointer arithmetic on an `s16 *` with an index that is a multiple of 3 lowers to `umpy k,3` followed by a separate `ushl 1` element scale, and ugen puts the shift in a fresh scratch register. An `n * 6` integer byte sum, or indexing over a 6-byte element type, gives a single multiply. Relatedly, `&((V *)p)[n - 1]` (a cast of the `s16 *` to the 6-byte vertex type) puts the base first in the addu, while a parameter declared with that type puts the offset first (Q0813, Q0717).

Evidence: clipPolyAgainstAxis (global/2F550.c, target 0x3ba8-0x3bb8; FAKE 26c2da fixed with PlanePoly.v typed s16[4][3] in render.h and a 6-byte-vertex parameter).
