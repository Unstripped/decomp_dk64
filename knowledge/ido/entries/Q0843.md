With `s8 *p`, the 1 in `*p = 1` stays a local `li` next to the `sb`; with `u8 *p` it joins the function's unsigned-1 constant web (here the u8 switch's `case 1`) and is hoisted into a saved register (s32 and u32 contexts form separate constant webs, Q0523). So a `(u32)` cast on a switch operand (Q0067) can be a compensating steer for a wrongly signed out-parameter: retype the parameter and remove the cast together.
