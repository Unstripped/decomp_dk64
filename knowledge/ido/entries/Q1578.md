7CA80 actorAllocate: a u16 aligned-size local whose stores are all copy-propagated still gets a home: the local area grows 20 -> 24 bytes and the two spill temps move from sp+36/40 to sp+32/36 (the value spills from the shared temp with a word sw). Adding `s32 pad` restores the slots but grows the frame 64 -> 72. Extends Q1283.
