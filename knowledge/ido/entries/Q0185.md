uopt puts a plain leaf (`cmd->b`) on the left of a commutative `==`; spelling the other side `((s16 *)cmd)[2]` flips the emitted `bne` operands. `X - cmd->b == 0` gets the order but costs a hidden temp that shifts the ugen `$t` ring (`t4,t8,t3,t5,t9,t7,t6`, busy registers skipped) for every later case; delete one case at a time and watch a probe register to count ring slots per case. This matched `csCondEval` (1099 insns) from 108 register-only lines.

Another instance in arcade/0 `arcadeUpdateDonkeyKongStage4` (cleanup round 15). In an `else if` chain testing x==0, x==1 and x==2, uopt orders each compare constant-first. `(0, 2)` keeps uequ{K3}{x}, and writing `2 == x` in the source does not change it.
