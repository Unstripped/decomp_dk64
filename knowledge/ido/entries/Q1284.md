NEW rule (uopt `unal`): after an alias reset (here the `unal R $sp 0` uopt puts in an early-return `ret` block) uopt re-emits `unal R $sp 1` before a direct store to a global whose `la` stays in R ($s7), so as1 may hoist an $sp reload (`lw t9,80(sp)`) above that store (`sw t8,0(s7)`). Only a store address uopt cannot fold avoids it (the `(u32)` round trip keeps the cvt pair); a pointer local for every access (propagated into direct %lo accesses), a store-only pointer local, `*(s32 *)&` and a `*(u32 *)&` view all fold back to `istr` on the $s7 base. Evidence @ 3da2d0eb: ucode positions 47/130/145, ugen records 41/88/99, deletion probes 41 -> 17 and 88 -> 4 lines. See also Q0503, Q0811, Q0482, Q1111, Q0973.
