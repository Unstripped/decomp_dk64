A constant folded into an addiu immediate (`x + ~0x3FU`, `x + -0x40`, `x - 0x40U`) does not count as a use of the constant's uopt candidate, so it cannot stretch the unsigned -64 web across calls (eepromSaveRecords: all spellings 118).
