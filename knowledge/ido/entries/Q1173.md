A value computed after `if (x == 0) x = K;` can land in that if's `bnez` delay slot: as1 fills the slot from the join block. If the same statement is placed first, uopt emits it right after its source load instead. Evidence: 42630 csCmdExec case 0xF (83069d). A dedicated `s32 spread` placed first gets the target registers (spread t0, c t1), but `and t0,v1,0x7f` comes right after `lh v1,4(s1)` at 0x3164 rather than in the 0x31b0 `bnez a2` slot (4). After both ifs it lands in the slot, but c takes t0 (6). Joining statements onto one line changed nothing (4-9).
