Supersedes the 201B0 claim behind Q0998/Q1152 that the orbit arm's goto/break joins are an "unthreaded break" rule of cfe/ugen. In global/201B0 camComputeEyeFocus, removing the goto label drops one needreglod block of the u8 ucvt `type` piece (1029, bit 245: 4 blocks instead of 5, adjsave 0.143), so the piece is coloured t0 and each goto/break block needs `lw t0,0x5c(sp)`; that reload is what stops threading (0x8064 `b 8074`, 0x8074 `b 89dc; lw t0`).
