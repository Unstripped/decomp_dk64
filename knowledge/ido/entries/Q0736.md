lensFlareDrawSource (global/103AB0.c) was K&R (header `Gfx *lensFlareDrawSource();`), and its caller needed an explicit `(u32)` cast on the u8 argument. The ANSI definition with a local prototype matches without the cast; because it takes a file-local struct by value, the declaration stays in the .c under a `// PROTO:` skip.
