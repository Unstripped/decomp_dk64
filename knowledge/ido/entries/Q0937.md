bonusSearchlightSeekGameUpdate (bonus/2690.c, 2818d5/60fd8d -> ORIG) has a 0x88 frame that holds exactly 12 declared words. The lead verified this with the own-home rule. The aim point is stored into the x/y homes with `swc1` at 124/120(sp) (0x5f5c/0x5f68). The clamp spills offsetX/offsetZ into the x/z homes at 124/116 (0x60a8/0x60ac). When the declared locals fill the frame exactly like this, you can move a register-only role into a spare `s32 pad` word, but only one role per spare word. Every other register-only role has to share a declared variable. Colouring fixes which variable each role shares: the velZ pre-read matches (0 lines off) in the pad, the 3rd or the 4th variable, while stick_x matches only in the 5th variable and every other choice leaves it 10-13 lines off. This is unlike Q0729, where a separate local in a pad slot was enough. The three register-only shares were tagged as pre-existing hacks (lead). Merge 3d3b8b0f, ledger afe1a25b plus tags record.
