bonusSearchlightSeekGameUpdate (bonus/2690.c, 2818d5/60fd8d -> ORIG) has a 0x88 frame that holds exactly 12 declared words. The lead verified this with the own-home rule. The aim point is stored into the x/y homes with `swc1` at 124/120(sp) (0x5f5c/0x5f68). The clamp spills offsetX/offsetZ into the x/z homes at 124/116 (0x60a8/0x60ac). When the declared locals fill the frame exactly like this, you can move a register-only role into a spare `s32 pad` word, but only one role per spare word. Every other register-only role has to share a declared variable. Colouring fixes which variable each role shares: the velZ pre-read matches (0 lines off) in the pad, the 3rd or the 4th variable, while stick_x matches only in the 5th variable and every other choice leaves it 10-13 lines off. This is unlike Q0729, where a separate local in a pad slot was enough. The three register-only shares were tagged as pre-existing hacks (lead). Merge 3d3b8b0f, ledger afe1a25b plus tags record. **2026-11-15c correction:** the 0x88 frame is NOT exactly full. Locals declared AFTER the last declared local (`s16 spawnAngle`) use up to 3 spare words without growing the frame (`s32 pad`, `s32 pad[2]`, or velZ+scale+stickX: fdiff 0 or colour-only), while the same word declared earlier makes the frame 0x90 (addiu-only diff). Replace "one register-only role per spare pad word" with: try role-named locals declared at the END of the list first (bonusSearchlightSeekGameUpdate, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackbonusSearchlightSeekGameUpdate3", feb215b5; see Q0729).

**2026-11-15c refinement of that correction:** declaring last is not enough when the word is owned. In modelEmitterSpawnScaledParticle (global/68EA0.c, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackmodelEmitterSpawnScaledParticle2", 0012cea1) a pointer local declared last still pushes an existing call-crossing spill down (the new local takes word 36, the idx*12 spill moves to 32; frame unchanged). To decide whether a spare word is free, count every word owner: declared homes (Q0456), cfe hidden temps for nested call results (Q0858/Q0487) and uopt spills (Q0342). A word no instruction touches (48 here) is NOT free when a declared local or call temp owns it; declared-last helps only for unowned words. textDrawStringVtx agrees: an untagged `s32 pad` first or last grows its frame 0xc8 -> 0xd0 because every word is owned (Q0941).
