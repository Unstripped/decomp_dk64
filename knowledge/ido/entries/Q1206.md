cfe gives `||` values and the results of `va_arg(ap, double)` ternaries the same temp slot (-328vreg), so they form one uopt candidate. Evidence (vsprintf): target 0x970-0x974 `move a0,s1` / `move v1,a0` and 0xac0 `move a0,v0`.
