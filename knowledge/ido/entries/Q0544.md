as1 gives a switch's non-return `jr` the live-ins of every jump-table target of the procedure, so a register those targets do not read counts as dead on the path to the switch and as1 hoists across it (Q0412's default-arm hoist). The natural FAKE-free C of `func_806F03C4` and `scriptOpPlaySoundAndShake` matches exactly (full 0) once that jump is treated as live (`as1probe try --insert <j>:live`, `live=0x40` resp. `0x06220000`, or an as1 patched to make a `jr` without `.livereg` all-live while keeping its successors); the same patch changes 129 matched functions, so the original's jump liveness was precise elsewhere and no C producer is known. Details: guide ido-backend-record-pipeline, section 'Switch jr liveness'.
