In `eepromCopySlot` (cleanup unit Hack, 2026-11-15, commit 57b6f7), the old `stride | 0` hack was removed. The function matches when the slot-size expression is written out in full at every use and buffer addresses use the `&buf[x]` form. Naming the slot size with a local or a macro gave 33-35 lines of diff. This fits Q0088: IDO schedules and allocates registers differently when the subexpression is repeated than when it goes through a temp. Writing `buf + x` instead of `&buf[x]` swapped the operands of the `addu`. This fits Q0175: the source spelling of an array address decides the base/offset order in the `addu`. Together they show that removing a fake `| 0` can need both levers at once: repeat the size expression, and use the subscript address form.
