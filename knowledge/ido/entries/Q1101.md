Same unit/function (uopt code motion/regalloc, float): a loop-invariant f64 expression written inline in the loop (`(tri->x0 + hx) * 0.5` inside c) is hoisted (no stack slot), but its candidate bit stays at its source position: on equal adjsave (16.4) it loses to earlier candidates (ucvt(x)/ucvt(z): f26/f28 instead of f22/f24), and constants used inside the hoisted expression get no savings for those uses (0.5: 2 vs 6, loses f0 to f14). An unnamed invariant cannot stand in for a named local when tie order or the constant's register matters. Evidence: scratch trace_orig/trace_v1, t5_*. See also Q1089.
