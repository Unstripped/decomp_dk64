In `critterSteerToTarget`, `obj->accel` requires the difference between the updated `obj->speed` and `old`. A previous hack used `*(volatile f32 *)&obj->speed` to prevent IDO from optimizing the subtraction into an expression of the previous value. Writing the update as a compound assignment `obj->speed += (tgt - old) * rate; obj->accel = obj->speed - old;` causes IDO to update the field in memory and reload it naturally without requiring volatile casts.
