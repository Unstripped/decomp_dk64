This refines Q1200 and Q1198. One local is reused for flags and for a value whose range rules out the lower registers. Splitting that value off does not let the flags keep the register: the split value takes the reused local's register, and the remaining flag web is coloured later and gets the lowest free register.

hudPauseMenuUpdate: a u8 flag packed into the x/stickY word plus an s32 limit+half local. limit/half (bit 361) gets a0 and the u8 flag (bit 169) gets v1. Score 8: `move`/`li`/`beqz` use a0 where the target has v1 at 0x570/0x598/0x5b4/0x5d0 and 0xce8/0xd28/0xd40/0xd64. The FAKE reuse stays.
