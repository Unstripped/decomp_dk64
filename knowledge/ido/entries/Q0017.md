a leaf with a `u8`/`s8` parameter (K&R or prototyped narrow) spills the argument register to its caller home slot (`sw $a1, 4($sp)`) and masks it (`andi 0xFF`); that is the usual cause of an unexplained home-slot store in a leaf.
