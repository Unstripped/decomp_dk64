HackeepromSaveRecords5 (unit "2026-11-16c: cleanup HackeepromSaveRecords5", ledger-only, base 6fe2a3af), src/global/104F0.c eepromSaveRecords (uopt frame): a dead read of a local in the loop can grow the uopt frame by 8 bytes (an unused home/spill word, no load/store in the final code); an unused pad local declared after the last-declared local reproduces it. When a dead-if removal changes the frame, separate the frame effect from the colouring effect. Evidence: with `if (a) {}` frame 4216, without 4208; `if (j)`/`(sector)`/`(idx)`/`(offset)` ahead of b's def also 4216, `if (b)`/`(height)`/`(i)` not; untagged + `s32 pad` after contReadPending = exact layout (179 -> 173).
