uopt does not coalesce a local copied out and back around a clamp (`newState = state; ... state = newState;`): the pair survives as a real `move` plus a stack spill of the copy. Such a pair in m2c output reproduces a target `move` and spill word that no single variable explains, but it is a steer (tag it FAKE), not a natural spelling.

Evidence: effectDecelerateDriftUpdate (global/11BC10.c): `move v0,v1` at 0x2bb8 and the copy's spill at 0x1c(sp); removing both lines gives full=9; tagged FAKE 3658da/5c1124.
