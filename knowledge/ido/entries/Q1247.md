Extends Q0925. Against a later K&R definition whose formal is u8, a block-scope prototype with a `u32` parameter is accepted and reproduces an explicit `(u32)` argument cast exactly; an `s32` parameter behaves like no prototype; a `u8` parameter is a cfe redeclaration error. So a `(u32)` widening steer at the call points to an ANSI u8 callee: converting global/936B0 projectileFindHomingTarget to an ANSI definition (u8 formals, Actor **outTarget) retired the caller steer 8fcc57 in projectileSteerTowardTarget with whole-object fdiff 0; the callee prologue shows the Q0411 `andi` pattern and the two 103AB0 callers match with the prototype.
