201B0 camComputeEyeFocus: a case arm ending in `goto L` can drop its goto when placed directly before `default: goto L`; IDO still threads the jump-table default entries and the bound-check branch straight to L (0x8060 b 0x89bc, 0x7ec4 beqz at,0x89bc).

**2026-11-17b correction (Hackvsprintf9, ledger 08f85115):** this does not apply when the switch's range check or its empty table entries go somewhere other than L (boot/3D20 vsprintf, 0x139c). Also, `c - '%' == 0` folds back to uequ(c, 37) (9bc6c1).
