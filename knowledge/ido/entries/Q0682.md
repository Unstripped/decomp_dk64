In the leaf `inputRemapButtons` uopt colours only 7 registers; with 7 interfering webs live across the loop, the `u16` parameter `buttons` gets no register and is reloaded from its home slot `2(sp)`, the target shape. An extra pointer local (`u16 *to = map + 1`) creates the seventh web naturally (fresh reconstruct score 395); the ghost0/ghost1 FAKEs do it artificially.
