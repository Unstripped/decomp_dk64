In IDO 5.3, binary addition 'x = a + b' emits 'addu dst, a, b'. If the target asm has 'addu dst, b, a', writing a compound assignment 'var_a0 += (s16)(var_a0 - t)' evaluates the RHS 'b' first and then emits 'addu dst, b, a', matching reversed operand order without comma-operator expressions.
