Replacing a literal element size with `sizeof` makes the product unsigned (`size_t`). Assigned to an s32 local the result is identical code, but passed directly as an s32 call argument the unsigned multiply and its conversion back change register allocation of the surrounding values; keep the literal (or go through a local) there.

Evidence: schedSubmitGfxTask (global/14CF0.c): `osWritebackDCache(glist, numCommands * sizeof(Gfx))` moves &D_807F05A4 to a1/a3 and numCommands to s3/v1 (fdiff 1), while `data_size = numCommands * sizeof(Gfx)` alone is fdiff 0; both sites kept `numCommands * 8`.
