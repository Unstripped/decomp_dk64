HacktagBarrelIsSpecial (unit "2026-11-16c: cleanup HacktagBarrelIsSpecial", merge d3f3644e), src/global/80150.c tagBarrelCheckKongFlag (cfe/uopt; related Q0743): a u8 call result stored into an s32 struct field adds a ucvt node and a ring temp (register shift t0 -> t1 after `info.isSet = flagGet(...)` at 0x7f48, full=11); a u32 field takes the zero-extended value directly (no ucvt), which is what the target does. With it the shared `u8 flagGet(s16, u8)` applies and the file's local `s32 flagGet(s16, s32)` steer + PROTO_SKIP retire. Same family as Q1066's 2026-11-16c correction and Q1116 (signedness/width of the receiver of a u8).
