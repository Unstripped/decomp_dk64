In `arcadeUpdateDonkeyKongStage3` (src/arcade/0.c, 56b8d0) the earlier match relied on a dead constant def into a reused temp, `tmp = 0; if (D_8004C71D != tmp)`, with `tmp` later reused as the animTimer. That fake was standing in for a candidate that a named local reading a global supplies. Writing `frame = D_8004C6DC; if (frame % 120 == 0)` adds a candidate between the D_8004C71D test and the modulo. D_8004C6DC then lives in a0 (`lw a0` at 0xc08c, reused by `andi t9,a0,1` at 0xc1d8), and the animTimer truncation goes to a1 (`mfc1 a1` at 0xc130). The temp becomes a plain `timer` local with no reuse. This is the reverse of Q0477's advice to delete temps: when a fake dead def is needed to get the colouring, the missing isvar candidate is often a global read captured in a local.
