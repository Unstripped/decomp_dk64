EXTENDS Q0908/Q1179 (uopt): the block shape of a dead read decides register order: a one-case empty switch or `if (x) {} else {}` gives one more block than `if (x) {}` / `x ? 0 : 0;`; with the one-arm shape the kongState piece is coloured before the constant-2 piece (v1/a0 swapped, 7), with the two-arm shape the constant is split and coloured first (0). When one dead read nearly replaces two guards and only a register pair is swapped, try the two-arm shape (still a steer without target backing).
