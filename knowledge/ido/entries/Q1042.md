(a) `op = w0; op |= K; op &= ~K;` keeps op a register variable, while `op = w0 | K` folds into temps. (b) A dead test can be what blocks uopt's 2x do-while unrolling: check unrolling when removing one; `for (;;)` with `break` on `++p == end` keeps the loop scalar. (c) The operand order of a constant compare mattered (`bne t1,v0` vs `bne v0,t1` at 0x260), qualifying Q0474. (d) Only op itself as the first-appearing candidate helps; a separate guard local does not.
