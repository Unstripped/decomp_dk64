Under IDO 7.1 -g an `else` arm with no statements still emits `b join; nop` (1417C0 n_alSynAllocVoice is 784 bytes with the two arms, 768 without). The libultra source has `else { #ifdef _DEBUG __osError(ERR_ALSYN_NO_UPDATE, 0); #endif }` there; restoring that spelling matches with no FAKE tag (Rev18B, round 18).
