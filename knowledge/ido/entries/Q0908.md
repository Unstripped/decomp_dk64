In `HackraceSteerTowardTargetWaypoint2` (unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackraceSteerTowardTargetWaypoint2", commits 11f5b9/b8545a), two empty guards sit in different blocks: one on `gCurrentActor->x` in the first arm, one on `waypointRadius` after the threshold. Each one alone changes nothing. Together they put steerAngle/angleDelta in `$f18`/`$f16`. Without both guards, steerAngle spills to `100(sp)`. This is another coupled-steer knot like Q0602, so remove or add the guards together. An empty `;` at either position does not reproduce the effect. So unlike Q0698, these guards are not block-split levers. They act as real uopt uses (Q0561). There is also a negative result for the statement-order lever from cannonUpdate (Q0468/Q0739). Once the copy is optimised away, the order of the post-call copy, radius and threshold statements makes no difference here.
