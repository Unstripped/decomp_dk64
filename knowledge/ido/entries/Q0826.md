A K&R definition with a `u16` parameter and its prototyped ANSI form compile to identical code: the callee narrows the promoted int at entry either way. The definition style is therefore not call-site evidence for an unprototyped call (Q0486), and a home reload of such a parameter (`sw a0,0(sp)` / `lhu 2(sp)`) does not come from the K&R style; it comes from colouring pressure (Q0682).

Evidence: __n_removeEvents (global/13A7A0.c, IDO 7.1 -g): the K&R definition and `void __n_removeEvents(ALEventQueue *, GfSndState *, u16)` give a whole-object fdiff OK (merge 62f01bf0). Under IDO 5.3 -O2 the K&R u16 definition of inputRemapButtons (global/E4090.c) still narrows with `andi` at entry (HackE4090InputA, merge 7a453f41).
