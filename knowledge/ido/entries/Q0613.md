In minecartUpdateObstacleType3, `goto` from the case-0 then-arm to the case-1 body (the label sits at the head of the next case) changes register colouring even though control would reach the same code anyway. Without the goto, LR127 (`&gCurrentActor`) is coloured whole to one register: `sw s1,0x30(sp)`, then `lw s0,0(s1)` at each use. The goto adds a CFG edge, so the shared case-1 block has two predecessors (the then-arm and the jump table), and uopt splits that live range off $s1, which is what the target shows. The fallthrough form (score 4269) and an inverted early return (5224) both re-colour LR127. Evidence: .scratch/CleanMinecart25F0/uopt_obstacle3_goto.txt vs uopt_obstacle3_gotofall.txt. Keep such gotos as a Q0591 shared-tail goto.
