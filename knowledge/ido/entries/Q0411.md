an early `sw a1,home` plus `andi t,a1,0xff; move a1,t` is an ANSI `u8` formal, not `s32` + `&= 0xFF`; a K&R `u8` param promotes to unsigned (`lbu` home+3, `bgez`/2^32 cvt fixup). A callee's unused narrow param emits the caller's `andi` in the `jal` delay slot: fix the callee prototype instead of `& 0xFF` FAKEs. A K&R definition after an ANSI prototype with narrow params is only cfe warning 642.
