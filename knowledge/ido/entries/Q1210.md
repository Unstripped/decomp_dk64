Supersedes Q1164's claim that an unsigned sum reverses ugen's addu operand order. In global/ACDC0 hudPauseMenuRender with `u32 off`, `off + offsetX * 4` still gives the computed operand first (`addu t8,t7,s0` at 0x1364): a bare register variable is a leaf and is evaluated last. Only a conversion node on the variable's side reverses it: `(u32)off`, or `offsetX * 4U` converting off (site c4e347, 26 lines with plain u32 off).
