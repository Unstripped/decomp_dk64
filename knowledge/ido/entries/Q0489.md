`D[i]` (or `*(T *)((u8 *)D + i * 12 + k)`) is strength-reduced by uopt itself: ugen emits `.noalias`, as1 fills delay slots differently, LFTR rewrites later `i < N` tests into pointer compares against the next splat label (write them `&D[i] < (T *)D_<label>`); a hand `p++` walk does none of that. A target comparing a pointer against `lui/addiu D_xxx`, or with in-place `sll tN,tN,2` index chains, used an index.
