In `actorLightPulseUpdate` (A2F10), a reused `Actor *` local also held a `PlayerState *`. Moving that second role into a `PlayerState *playerState` local declared where a frame-only `s32 pad` had been matches (fdiff 0). This is the same mechanism as Q0629, with a pointer of a different type.
