In `raceUpdateWaypointActivation` (A960), the target compares against 302500.0f with `dist` held as its own coloured FP value (frame 0x50, the hoisted constant in callee-saved $f22). Writing the squared distance with dx/dy/dz temps and a literal compare shrinks the frame from 0x50 to 0x48 and moves a value from $f22 to $f20.

Corrected 2026-11-14 (HackraceUpdateWaypointActivation, merge 5b09ade0): a natural spelling exists. A float local copy-propagates into its single use unless its assigned value contains an indirect load (uoptcopy find_replacements has_ilod): `dist = (p[0] - node->x) * (p[0] - node->x) + (p[1] - node->y) * (p[1] - node->y) + (p[2] - node->z) * (p[2] - node->z); farAway |= dist > 302500.0f;` keeps dist a variable and matches. The old `dz = 302500.0f` FAKE only imitated that by making dist's assigned value unavailable at the compare.
