Pointer arithmetic on a typed pointer and on an integer address emit opposite `addu` operand orders.

Evidence: modelSlotReplaceSegment6Commands (global/36880.c): `Gfx *start = (Gfx *)head; f(start, start + head)` emits `addu a1,t0,a0`; the u32 address form `(Gfx *)start + head` emits `addu a1,a0,t0`; `&start[head]` and `head + start` behave like the typed form.
