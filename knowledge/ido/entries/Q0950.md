In spawnNodeActorOutOfRangeScaled (unit "cleanup HackspawnNodeActorOutOfRangeScaled", kept FAKE at 7d9940), the target reuses `r`, the `sqrtf` result, to hold dy. That reuse pins `r * 1.5` in place. It also pins the hoisted `lui` of the 1.5 constant ahead of the loads in the initialState block at 0x3930. If dy is a separate local, uopt sinks the `cvt.d.s`/`mul.d` down to the compare. The register colours are identical either way (31 in every placement), so this is a schedule-only lever. Unlike Q0739, the reuse changes no adjsave, no callee-saved register and no frame size. All 127 permuter zero-score results reassign `r`, which is strong evidence that the reuse is required even though it looks like non-original source. This extends the pinning-against-sinking rule in Q0324 to a variable that is reused only for its register, not as an accumulator.
