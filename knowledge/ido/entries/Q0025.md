`sw $a0, 0x18($sp)` then `lbu $a3, 0x1B($sp)` is an old-style definition `s32 f(arg0) u8 arg0; { ... }`; same-TU callers need an unprototyped `s32 f();` (a prototype `f(s8)` errors against it, and `f(u8)` makes the caller emit `lbu` instead of `lb`).
