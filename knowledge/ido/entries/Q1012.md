After `s->f = a - b; x = s->f;` uopt replaces the reload with the stored value unless `a` or `b` is redefined in between. When the target reloads the field, the natural spelling is a compound store `s->f -= b;` followed by a direct field read, not a redefinition of an operand.

Evidence: effectDecelerateDriftUpdate (global/11BC10.c): the old reuse `clampedAlpha = 255.0f` only worked by redefining an operand; with `-=` the target's reload (`lwc1 $f0,20(v0)` at 0x2dfc) matches.
