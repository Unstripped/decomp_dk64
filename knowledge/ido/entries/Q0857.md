playerCameraUpdateFromTarget's D_807FC624 is a function-local `static s16` (uopt isvar S): it gets a0 at every access (0x167c/0x169c/0x16cc/0x1708), which the extern only reached with two dead `if (G && G) {}` tests; a file-scope static does not (text=16). Making it a static needed the TU to own its .bss (yaml carve 634ab5f5, definitions in address order, Q0565).

**2026-11-15c addendum:** statement shape matters on a function-local static: `s += K; s &= M;` matches the target exactly (0), while `s = (s + K) & M;` and a temp `t = (s + K) & M; s = t;` both score 33. Two separate compound statements keep the target's two-step a0 sequence (0x16cc-0x170c) (playerCameraUpdateFromTarget, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackplayerCameraUpdateFromTarget3", 880ea585).
