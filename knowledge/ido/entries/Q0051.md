global `77B90` (and `15010`, `16B60`, `61690`) uses `ld`/`sd`/`dmult`, saves `$k0`/`$k1` and passes values in `$t4`/`$t7`/`$s0`; IDO `-mips2` cannot emit it, so it stays `asm`. Check with `grep -cE '\s(dmult|ld|sd|daddu?)\s|\$k[01]'` before converting a file.
