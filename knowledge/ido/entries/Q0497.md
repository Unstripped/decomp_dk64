IDO emits `.align 5` before an unreachable epilogue (endless-loop function) relative to the .o start, so the tail-nop count and `bnez`/`bnezl` depend on the TU's start (`schedMain` matched only once 131B0 was split at `schedCreate` into 13980). A text-identical match whose `.rodata` references all sit 4 bytes off is a hidden TU (boss DAA0).
