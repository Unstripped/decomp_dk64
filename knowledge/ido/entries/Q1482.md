Extends Q1134 (only identical `0.0` literal text merges) to non-zero values: in a double compare, `(f64)x < 640` with an int literal and `x < 640.0` give the same instructions but different FPR numbers, because the int literal stays a constant of its own, separate from a `640.0` double. When only FP register numbers differ around a double literal, try the other spelling.

Evidence: menu/3E10.c menuUpdateElementTargetAndColor, from 0x8fec (unit Polishmenu3E10c, merge b0f36e37).
