In jetpacStateGameplay (jetpac/0.c) one `goto l1; l1:` grows the &jetpacGame range 21 -> 22 blocks and adjsave 1.0 -> 0.857, but it keeps $s0 at 23 CFG nodes: the first-use test compares unbucketed savings 6.0 with curstaticno*0.25 = 5.75; only a 24th node drops it. One code-free node between two calls (an empty `switch (x) {}` or a goto/label) is enough for the caller-saved reload/store pair at 0x954/0x95c; `if (1) {}` adds two nodes.
