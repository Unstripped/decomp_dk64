For a typed pointer `p`, `&p[i]`, `p + i`, `i + p`, `&i[p]`, `&(p + i)[0]` and `entry = p; entry = &entry[i]` all lower to `addu dst, idx*size, p` (offset first). A conversion on the base (an int-to-pointer cast or an identity cast, Q0717) or the address-of form `&p[0] + i` gives base-first `addu dst, p, idx*size`. Retyping an s32 that was cast at the base therefore loses the base-first order; `&p[0] + i` is the cast-free spelling but is still a steer (tag it FAKE).

Evidence: actorCollisionProcessHit (global/7A510.c): target `addu s1,a0,t1` at .text 0x110 (site 818f85, lead-ruled FAKE). critterUpdateZoneProximity (critter/0.c): only `&gPlayerSlots[0] + gCurrentPlayerIndex` keeps `addu v1,t9,t4` at 0x2144; `&gPlayerSlots[i]`, `gPlayerSlots + i` and `i + gPlayerSlots` give offset,base and recolour 0x2108-0x21dc (28 rows each).
