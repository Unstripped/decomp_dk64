An alias local (`lrs = width;`) cannot be retired by re-reading the field it copies (`lrs = gCurrentTexture->width;`): uopt CSEs the second load, but the second variable still gets its own copy register, so the two names never form one live range.

Evidence: dlAppendTextureLoad (global/1135F0.c): the re-read form still emits `move t1,t0`, as the alias steer does.
