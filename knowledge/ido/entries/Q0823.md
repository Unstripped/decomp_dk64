cfe emits an f32 compare against a literal with the constant as the second `c.eq.s` operand whichever side it is written on. A target with the zero first (`c.eq.s $fZ,$fX`) compared a variable holding zero; a declaration initialiser (`f32 t = 0.0f;`) reproduces it while keeping one `mtc1 zero` web.

Evidence: menuComputeElementPosition (menu/3E10.c, 0x9a58; FAKE fa9d5b fixed).
