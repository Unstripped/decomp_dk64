mpUpdateCrownSpawns (multiplayer/0.c) had `MpSpawnPoint * volatile spawnPoint` to force a stack home. Rewriting the outer `if (... && !(flags & 2)) {` as `if (... == 0 || (flags & 2)) return;` adds CFG nodes, spawnPoint's adjsave drops 1.40 -> 0.00, and it gets the same stack home without the volatile (trace_crown_*).
