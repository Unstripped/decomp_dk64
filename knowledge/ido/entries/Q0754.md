In scriptCmdSpawnFallingEffect (global/19A00.c) `if (!imm4)` loads the u8 into v0 and copies it to a1 in the branch delay slot (`lbu v0; bnez v0; move a1,v0`); `if (imm4 == 0)` loads straight into a1. The old copy temp `node = imm4` stood in for the `!` spelling.
