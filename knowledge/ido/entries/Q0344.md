an empty case whose jump-table entry points at the switch end is `case N: break;` written after the last case (before the next case it adds `b`/`nop` and splits a block); a bound one larger than the written cases needs an explicit empty last case; a final `default:` without `break` is laid out last; 2–3-case inner switches (not if/else-if) give the `beqz/beq/b` chains; with a `goto` into a case, put the label before `case N:`.
