Introducing a named pointer local for repeated reads of one array element is not free: it takes a frame word and changes candidate pressure enough to drop other CSEs.

Evidence: hudPauseMenuRender loop 1 (global/ACDC0.c): `HudTextEntry *e` for the eight entry reads grows the frame 0x1A0 -> 0x1A8 and loses the s8 CSE of the `style == 2` compare constant (`li at,2` at 0x12e4); full=385.
