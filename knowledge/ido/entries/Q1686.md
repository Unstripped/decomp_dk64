Its only effect is one ugen `and $11,$25,65535` record that as1 deletes but that shifts the scratch ring (sra cell>>2 t9 -> t3 at 0x750, dx sll t4 at 0x74c); no real type supplies it. Also: function-scope `s16 dx, dy` reproduce the loop's explicit `(s16)` casts byte for byte (re-narrowing at `dist = dx`, 0x650/0x654), but an s16 local narrows inside its own assignment, so a narrowing the target performs only after a later statement's subtraction cannot come from a declared width (extends Q1182).
