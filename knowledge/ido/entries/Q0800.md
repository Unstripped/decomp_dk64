`default: x = K; /* fallthrough */ case 1:` without a goto: as1 copies the default's store into the dispatch delay slot but leaves the original block as an orphan (+4 bytes). With `goto join` the default block ends in a jump and nothing is left. A target without the orphan store needs the goto form.

Evidence: behaviourFairyInitPlayerScale (global/BDEE0.c, ud4ecc kept).
