A loaded value used once is folded into its use; given any second use (a `x * 0` no-op, a dead `if`, a copy temp) it becomes its own uopt web and competes for registers. When registers are scarce it can take the last colourable one, so a parameter with a long range is split: no register across a call, kept in its home slot and reloaded. When a target reloads a parameter from its home after a call, look for the second-use load the original had.

Evidence: actorTriggerPlayerKnockbackSound (global/7BA00.c, FAKE kept, key abfc8a): with the `ps->playerIndex * 0` no-op (or any dead `if`/copy temp the permuter found) the lbu 0x1A4(v0) web (lr 12, adjsave 3.0) takes a2, and `attacker` (a0, lr 43, adjsave -0.5) has no register across `jal playerCanTakeHit`, stays in 0x38(sp) and is reloaded into v1 at 0x6c. Without the second use attacker is coloured and fdiff fails.
