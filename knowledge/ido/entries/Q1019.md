Q0333's "reuse it for other temps" lever in a natural instance: giving a local an extra definition and uses on another path raises its adjsave and can tie or beat a competing candidate, replacing dead guards that were raising it artificially.

Evidence: commandApplyToLight (global/126260.c): two dead `if (base == base) {}` raised an f32 local above an f64 local (adjsave 3.5 vs 2.0); reusing the f32 local for a later independent temp (`factor = 127.0f / dist` for three direction bytes) lifts its adjsave from 1.5 to 3.0, tying the f64 `clamped` (3.0), and the lower candidate index wins. The reused value still compiles into the target's CSE register (`div.s $f0` at 0xc80).
