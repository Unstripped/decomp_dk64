The free-word placement (split a register-only reused local into a dropped local's word or an alignment hole) needs such a word. hudPauseMenuIconUpdate (frame 0x58) has nine one-word locals and no hole: only kongCount's word can be freed, by writing the count as the literal 5, which alone costs 30 lines (Q1220 strength reduction), and every per-role split in that word then scores exactly 30 (distSq 34): the count local blocks all six radius/brightness splits. hudPauseMenuUpdate's only space is the 2-byte x/stickY hole; any narrow role placed there gains a sign extension and recolours (41-119 lines; Q1665's mechanism).
