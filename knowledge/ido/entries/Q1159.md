Store forwarding happens only from the assignment directly before the read. A partial-product layout, as in the sibling collisionTestDynamicActorFloor (`nzPart = dx01 * dy02;` and then `(nzPart - dy01 * dx02)` repeated at each use), therefore avoids forwarding a full normal component. IDO keeps source operand order for expression operands, so the target's operand order is evidence. In 6AD90 collisionTestActorBoxFloor, ff3fab was fixed with z1 written first: `mul.d f6,f4(z1),f22(nz)` at 0x41cc and `mul.d f10,f22(nz),f4(z)` at 0x425c. The lead verified this and accepted it untagged.
