Same unit/function (cfe; refines Q0944): two switches on the same FIELD (an init-block one-case switch plus the dispatch switch) do not give the shared selector temp a surviving second definition: both temps are copy-propagated from field loads (6 lines). A second definition only counts when the selector is a user variable.
