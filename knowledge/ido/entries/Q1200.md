In global/ACDC0 hudPauseMenuUpdate the target loads `hud->page` into a1, the register of the loop index `i` (lb a1,18(s0) at 0x61c and the branch-likely delay slots 0x4d0/0x4e4; case compares 0x624-0x63c). A dedicated `s8 page` selector packed into a free home word keeps the frame (0x58) and every home but is coloured v0 ahead of `i` (-27vreg, bit 189): full=7, also u8, also first in the word, and still v0 when i's credits role is split away as well. A separate selector local is a new uopt candidate with its own priority; it does not take over the register the reused local held, so the reuse stays the only measured match (FAKE 38b069).
