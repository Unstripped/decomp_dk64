writing `(s32 *)(arg0->unk54 + (u8 *)arg0)` twice rather than through a temp let IDO fill the `lw` delay slot with the next `addiu` (`triggerZoneInit`); likewise repeating `((s32)v & 0xFFF)` in a packed GBI word stopped a temp being pinned across pointer increments (`effectBuildMultiBlockTextureGdl`).
