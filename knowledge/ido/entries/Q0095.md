a caller forwarding an `f32` in `$a1` must be ANSI `(s32 arg0, f32 arg1)` with a `(s32, f32)` callee prototype; a K&R `s32 arg1` caller needs an unprototyped callee declaration, which cfe rejects next to the prototyped definition in the same TU, and a K&R `f32 arg1` is promoted to double (`seqRouteChannelVolume` / `seqSetTargetVolume`).
