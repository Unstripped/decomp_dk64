`x == 1` in a loop can hoist `1` into a `t1` candidate that shifts every later ring temp; `x - 1 == 0` keeps `li at,1`. A dead compare with the same typed constant (`(u32)x == 0xFFFFFFFF`) before a loop moves its `li` to the preheader front (`bnez` delay slot). Flags typed `u8`/`u16`/`u32` get their own `li 1`; `s32` shares a hoisted case constant. `timer == 11U` flips `bnel` operand order. Preheader constant order follows the constants' first appearance in source.
