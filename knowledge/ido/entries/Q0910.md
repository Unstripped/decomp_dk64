In `func_806BD170` / `actorNodeRingUpdate` (src/global/C1E70.c, ledger 20681833, 726a32), the call `actorSpawn(0x73, 0xC4, &gCurrentActor)` passes a third argument even though `actorSpawn` is really `s32 (s32, s32)` (7CA80, which never homes or reads a2, 0x228-0x290). That spare `&GLOBAL` argument is what loads the address into `$a2` (`lui a2` at 0x2b0, `addiu` at 0x2c8). Afterwards `lw t3,0(a2)` reuses it as the `gCurrentActor` base. Q0169's leftover-register explanation does not fit here, because dropping the argument moves the register. This is the only caller among 44 files that passes a third argument. The lead ruled it not ORIG-eligible: `$a2` is also the CSE base, so there is no way to tell the extra argument apart from a register-preference steer. It stays as `// PROTO:` with views blocked.
