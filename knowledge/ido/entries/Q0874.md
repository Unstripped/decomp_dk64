A function-scope pointer holding `&p->field` and reused for two stores keeps `addiu vN,vN,off` + `sw x,0(vN)` (texAnimUpdateNodes, global/36880.c, 0x7eb8/0x7f18); block-scoped pointers score 18 and direct field stores fold the offset into the sw, so the addiu is evidence of the original pointer local (ORIG).
