Extends Q0810 beyond loop bounds: `sizeof(t) / sizeof(t[0])` is an unsigned constant anywhere, so it reproduces an unsigned table-length compare outside a loop (`sltiu`) and an unsigned wrap-around modulo (`divu`) without a `U` literal. Prefer it to a bare `NU` literal when the operand is a table's length.

Evidence: global/117190.c (unit Polishglobal117190b, merge 2a63bee8): menuTimerUpdateCountdown (`sltiu` compare) and menuCheckMapTransitionOnLoad (`divu` modulo, previously spelled `5U`).
