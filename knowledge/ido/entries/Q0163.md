`compute_save` (uoptreg2.c) = per-block load/store refs x frequency, minus 1 per block needing a reload, divided by `((nlive-2)>>2)+2`; ranges colour in descending priority, each taking the lowest free register; only `v0`, `v1`, `a0`–`a3`, `t0` are globally coloured, `t1`–`t9` are as1 temps (a `t9` value in the target means uncoloured). Ties on `adjsave` break by the symbol's first mention.
