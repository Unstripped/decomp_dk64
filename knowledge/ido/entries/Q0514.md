Reusing `sz` in the rebuild (`sz = size - 0x20; ... sz = start + (sz - freeList)`) moves pool-field loads before the indirect store without FAKE preloads and flips the `last`/`&D_807F0988` priority; an early `last = NULL` is not rematerialised (callee-saved across calls); dead stores to register locals vanish; `-Wo,-varref,N` is inert here.
