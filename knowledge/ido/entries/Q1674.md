Joining the case-2 and case-3 fade assignments of hudPauseMenuRender on one source line does not let as1 reorder their loads; the cfe order stands (14 lines, 2 with fadeInput0 first). `.loc`-based reordering (LAYOUT) applies to dependent pairs and stores, not to independent loads.
