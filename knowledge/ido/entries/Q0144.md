declaring the first parameter of `func_8072AB74`/`func_8072D13C` unsigned (`u8`/`u32`) changes the caller's size and emits `andi a0,reg,0xff` where `s32` or K&R gives `move` (`func_806AEE84` needs it; a `(u32)` cast kept `func_806AFB58` matching); an all-`u8` prototype plus two `u8` locals reproduced a split `u8` live range in `$a0` (`func_806B4194`). Because the `u8` prototype already emits the `andi 0xFF`, a caller's explicit `x & 0xFF` argument mask is redundant: with the shared `sfxStopSlotSound(Actor *, u8)` prototype, plain `i` matches and both the local `void *`-taking prototype steer and the mask go (`raceVehicleBehaviorUpdate`, 1770, cleanup round 16). Sibling narrowing steers can only be removed together. In `func_806B13B4` (B5EC0), switching either `actorMoveWithinZone` or `patrolPathStep` alone to its shared `u8` prototype spills the s32 `sel` (`sw a1,0x2c(sp)`/`0x28(sp)`); switching both matches. With `effectSetColor(u8 x4)`, plain `(tint, tint, tint, alpha)` matches in `func_806B1F50` (B6C50): the target's `andi a2,a0,0xff` comes from the prototype, and a kept `& 0xFF` adds `move a2,t5`/`move a1,a0`. In `bossMainStep` (F0D0), the shared `lightAdd(..., u8, u8, u8, u8)` fails only where an s16 `i` is passed (it is narrowed once); a dedicated `u8` local holding the value matches.
