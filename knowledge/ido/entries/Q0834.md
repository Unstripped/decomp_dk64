IDO 5.3 emits a file-scope `const` aggregate at the start of the object's .rodata, ahead of the literals and jump tables of functions defined before it: defining it at the top or at the end of the .c gives the same object. So an asm .rodata table at `.rodata+0` of its TU can move to C anywhere in the file (Q0394, Q0822). A non-const definition goes to .data instead (fails), and the shared extern must become `extern const` too (a non-const extern conflicts).

Evidence: global/CC800.c Kong table (merge 03d13888).
