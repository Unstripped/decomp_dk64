uopt does not copy-propagate an expression containing an ilod (Q0510 has_ilod). A dead store/test kept only to stop a local's propagation into its use can often go when the operands are written as memory reads instead of locals: 68EA0 modelEmittersUpdate (inline squared deltas keep distSq its own variable, Q0677 2026-11-17) and F56F0 playerStateWaypointAimUpdate (inline distance keeps the compare in place). The operand order must stay the natural one.
