equal-adjsave candidates colour by first definition/mention in the ucode, not declaration order (assign the loop counter before the pointers); colours are tried `$v1`, `$v0`, `$a1`, `$a2`; candidates with interference degree >= register count ("constrained") colour first. Five FP candidate registers (`f0 f2 f12 f14 f16`): a twice-used double constant can evict an FP param.
