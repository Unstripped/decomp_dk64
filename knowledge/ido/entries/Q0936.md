In effectSineHorizontalDriftUpdate and effectSineDriftUpdate (global/11BC10.c), cfe folds an f32 literal multiplied by a double, `0.35f * 0.5`, into a single double rodata constant at compile time. The target instead has `lwc1` 0.35f, then `cvt.d.s`, then `mul.d` by 0.5 (at 0x4444/0x4454/0x445c and 0x457c/0x458c/0x4594). That sequence appears only when the 0.35f is held in an f32 VARIABLE. Three spellings match: a role-named `f32 riseSpeed` declared after `speed`, the initialiser `f32 speed = 0.35f`, and the original reuse of `speed`. So reusing `speed` was not what fixed the match. Any f32 local holding the literal works. The fix took 6881fc's FAKE count from 10 to 8. The lead merged the role-named local as plain C rather than the worker's `// ORIG:` tag.
