In bonusReelGameUpdate (bonus 8002570C, target 0x940) the target computes the divisor at run time: `li 2; mtc1; cvt.s.w; div.s`. Only a float divided by a constant held in an `s32` local (`two = 2; x / two`) reproduces it; every literal spelling (`2.0f`, `2`, `2.0`) folds to `mul.s` by 0.5 or a loaded 2.0f. The local stays (tagged); compare Q0209/Q0354 for the literal-divisor cases.
