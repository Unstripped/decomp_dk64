At -O2, a local that never reaches memory (no home store, no spill) and is declared after the lowest-addressed local that IS stored (or that fits an alignment hole above it) gets no stack word: the frame ends at the deepest home in use (Q0408, Q0561). A register-only local declared ABOVE a stored local takes a word, pushes the stored locals down and grows the frame. This refines Q1016 ("every declared local owns a frame word"): only locals at or above the lowest stored one do. See Q1688, Q1672.

Census consequence (Q1666 part 1, rulings.md; R1 2026-11-26): a reuse split can show stack evidence only at declaration positions above the lowest stored local; every position after it (and every block scope) keeps the frame and every home, so part 1 fails on register-only evidence and the FAKE stays.
