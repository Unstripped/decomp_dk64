In `actorMapModelsSetup` (8D3E0) the s32 `modelAsset` parameter is reused as asset pointer, asset id and loop cursor. Retyping it `u8 *` moves the u8 `loadMode` parameter's `andi s2,a1,0xff` even when every use is cast back, so the warning stays (0604cd).

Corrected 2026-11-14 (OwnerWarnings450Chain): actorMapModelsSetup now matches as `(u8 *, u8, u8)` with no warning. A new local declared in the position of the existing `s32 pad` takes over the parameter's integer roles and reproduces the frame and the loadMode `andi` (Q0836).
