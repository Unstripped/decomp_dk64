In `actorMapModelsSetup` (8D3E0) the s32 `modelAsset` parameter is reused as asset pointer, asset id and loop cursor. Retyping it `u8 *` moves the u8 `loadMode` parameter's `andi s2,a1,0xff` even when every use is cast back, so the warning stays (0604cd).
