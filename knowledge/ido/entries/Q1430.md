(Q0228, Q0237, Q0647): a 3-trip `for` over three u8 colour channels converting double/float to u8 is NOT unrolled by uopt in commandApplyToLight (pointer-walk latch bne a0,a1 at 0x8d4, 0.5 hoisted into $f12: 498-572), so a hand-written three-statement sequence cannot stand in for such a loop.
