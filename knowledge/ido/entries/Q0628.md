Under IDO 7.1 -g (13A7A0 `__n_sndpPostPitch`), `*(s32 *)&f32local` emits `addiu tN,sp,off; lw tM,0(tN)` with the address not folded. A union member read emits `lw tM,off(sp)`, and an f32 field store `ev.arg8.f = pitch` emits lwc1/swc1. So in a -g function, an sp-relative `addiu` followed by `lw 0(reg)` shows the original source took the local's address, and the pun is the original spelling rather than a hack.
