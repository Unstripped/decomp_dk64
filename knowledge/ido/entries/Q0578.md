In `actorCollisionResolveSurfaceHit`, a parameter `flag` was tested via `if (*(u8 *)&flag != 0)` under the belief that taking the address was necessary to prevent cvt CSE and force a parameter reload. Testing the boolean directly with `if (flag != 0)` compiles to the exact same instruction sequence and register usage without taking the address of the parameter.
