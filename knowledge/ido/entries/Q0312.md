`x = c ? a : b` and `if (c) x = a; else x = b;` allocate registers differently (fixed a `v0`/`v1` swap, `func_80624978`); `h = x < 0xF ? 0xF : x` gives `beqz`/`move` where the `if` gives `beqzl`. A clamp's sense (min vs max) is read from the `bc1fl` delay-slot `cvt` source; one stage-6 park had it inverted.
