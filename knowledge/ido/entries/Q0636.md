In `behaviourFlyPatrolWithCallback` (B7490), reading the global pointer as `(*gBehaviourExtraState).y` fdiffs 0, while the equivalent `gBehaviourExtraState->y` does not. The two spellings are not interchangeable for IDO 5.3 -O2 here. The match keeps its comma FAKE instead, since `(*p).f` would only be an odd-spelling steer; record it as a hint, not a fix.
