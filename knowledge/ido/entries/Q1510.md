Supersedes the explanation in the round-17 history note (agent Rev17L) on actorCollisionResolveSurfaceHit's site e9a088, which attributed the empty `do {} while (0);` to colouring priority. The block split works through per-block interference (Q0523): it ends the block of the CSE temp `udiv{ucvt{clamp},8}` before pushX/pushZ are computed, so they stop interfering with it and pushX can take its register. Any codeless block boundary in that slot matches (Q1363 point 2).

- The CSE temp (cand 244: `div.s $f0` at 0xc6c, last use 0xc88) takes FP reg 24 ($f0) with or without the split.
- With the split: pushX/pushZ (cands 64/69, homes 104/100(sp)) take 24/25 ($f0/$f2); normal[0]/[2] (cands 163/171) take 26/27 ($f12/$f14).
- Without it: 64/69 take 25/26 ($f2/$f12, including `lwc1 $f2,232(sp)` at 0x9c8); 163/171 take 27/28.
- The clamp variable (cand 227) only moves in the colouring order and keeps reg 5 both times.
