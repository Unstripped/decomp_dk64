A deadsweep-style test of a **memory variable** is not free for ugen even though as1 deletes it: `if (((!i) && (!i)) && (!i)) {}` at the end of case 3 (i spilled at `0x158(sp)`) made ugen emit `lh $24,344($sp); bne $24,0,$76` — gone from the asm (as1 drops the jump-to-next and the dead load) but `$t8` had been allocated and freed, so the function-end `li 0xDF000000` moved from `$t8` to `$t9` and every `lui t8,0xdf00` duplicated into the cases' exit delay slots differed. Removing the FAKE fixed all six. Conversely a dead test of a register variable (`if (angv) {}`) or of a global whose load uopt hoists (`if (list != 0) {}`) still allocates a ring register for the hoisted comparison; check the `as1probe keep` listing, not the asm, for what a FAKE costs.
