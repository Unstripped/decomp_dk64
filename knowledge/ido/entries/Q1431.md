EXTENDS Q0763, Q0546 (see Q0551, Q1238): reading through a pointer expression before (or after) the pointer local is assigned (`x = ((T *)a->p)->f; p = a->p;`) makes the load a CSE web and the local a separate variable that takes a register (v1); ugen emits `move $3,$2` and as1 forwards it into the local's later use (`lh t9,212(v0)`), so the local adds register pressure (attacker pushed into its 0x38(sp) home across the call) without showing. When the target has one load of a->p (0x24) it is a steer, not a fix (lead load-count ruling).
