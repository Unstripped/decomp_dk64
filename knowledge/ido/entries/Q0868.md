Compiler spill temps sit below the named homes (Q0251, Q0331), so a value carried across a call that is stored into a named local's or a parameter's own home (not the temp area), while a separate local grows the frame by a word, was assigned to that variable in the source: such a 'reused local' FAKE is ORIG (playerUpdateHorizontalVelocity: forward's home 76(sp) at 0x3530/0x3574 and 0x3108; arcadeUpdateDonkeyKongStage2: the new index in the parameter's home 0x30(sp) at 0xc000/0xc010).
