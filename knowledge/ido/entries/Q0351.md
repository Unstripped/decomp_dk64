`trunc.w.d + mfc1 + sw` to an arg stack slot means the param is `u16`/`s16`/`u8`/`s8` (`s32` gives `trunc.w.d + swc1`); a `u8`/`s8` param makes the call site narrow with `lbu`/`lb`/`andi` where the target's plain `lw` needs `s32`.
