In `playerTakeDamage`, an expression assigning a float field was written as `... / (f32)4;` with a FAKE comment claiming the cast was required to emit `div.s` rather than multiplication by 0.25f. Writing natural integer literal division `... / 4;` against an f32 numerator already triggers floating-point division in IDO 5.3, emitting `div.s` identically.
