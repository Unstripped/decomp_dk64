In `gfxSwapGeometryModeCullBits` (global/17B90.c, cleanup HackgfxSwapGeometryModeCullBits2, ledger-only 679349ab, base 49040ebe), drafts 17676c, 3d5cd4 and 0d68f0 showed that uopt colours unconstrained candidates in candidate-bit order, which is the order in which they first appear in the ucode. Writing `op = 0` up front makes `op` bit 0, so it is coloured first and gets `$v0`. In the natural body, w0 is bit 7 and gets `$v0` instead (level-5/6 traces). When a variable is stored and then read again later, here by the trailing dead `command` test, it stays a variable live to the end of the loop. Its defining value, the ushr shift, then keeps its register across the mask test (`andi a3,v1,0x600` at 0x278). Natural second uses (a combined condition, else-if or early continue) are forwarded at input and lose this, scoring 42/52. In draft c82f14, a one-op RHS (`op = w0 | 0x400`) was substituted into the next use in the same block (Q0550). Only a kill keeps `op` a variable with two stores.
