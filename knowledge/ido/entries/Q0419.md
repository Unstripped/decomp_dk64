each GBI `{ Gfx *_g }` block owns a 4-byte home below the declared locals, and the spill-temp slot parity follows the home count: pointing k packets at one function-level `Gfx *_g` removes k homes. Locals cost their size (s16 vs s32 changed the frame by 8); unused/promoted locals declared before the first real scalar sit above it; an unused local array reserves its words; frames of leaf functions reserve nothing for unused f64s. `uopt_trace -l 5` prints each local's vreg offset (`isvar ... -Nvreg`) from the frame top. Defake lever: a hand-expanded packet (kept to avoid a macro's `_g` home) can become the macro when the code is otherwise identical and only the frame grows by 4 per macro; delete as many named locals (a copy local, a packet pointer) to give the words back (`menuRenderOptionDescription`: `gDPLoadTextureTile_4b` and `gSPSetOtherMode` for two packets, `xx`/`loadTilePacket` removed).
