arcadeUpdatePlayerEntity (arcade/0.c) had five `*(u16 *)D_807ECDE8 & mask` puns at the right of non-short-circuit `|` chains. `D_807ECDE8->button & mask` matches once it is the LEFT operand of the chain: the operand order of `|` decides the evaluation order of the loads.
