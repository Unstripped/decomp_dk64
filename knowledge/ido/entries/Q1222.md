Like a subscripted operand (Q1069), a right operand of `+` that holds a call is emitted first. In global/68EA0 modelEmitterSpawnScaledParticle, `gModelEmitters[i].x + (randomNonNegative() % 10000 % 300 / 20.0 - 7.5) * s` gives the target product + field add.d (0xa10/0xa84/0xaec) with the call results in cfe hidden temps 40/44/48(sp); the jitter-first order with inline calls is 57, named randX/Y/Z locals 57. The merged form relies on IDO evaluating the three randomNonNegative() calls left to right (C leaves that order unspecified).
