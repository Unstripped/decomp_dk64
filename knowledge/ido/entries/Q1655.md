`f(y + (c ? a : b))` gives the ternary its own frame word below every local. A ternary assigned to a variable first gets none. cfe evaluates a ternary operand of a commutative add first, so `add.s fd, f_tern, f_y` comes out for either operand order. renderModelAnimatedSlot `add.s` at 0x597c.
