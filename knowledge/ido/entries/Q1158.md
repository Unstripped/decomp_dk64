When a reuse FAKE needs an int-width local but the frame has no free word, a block-scoped snapshot local from another case can be merged with it into one function-level local declared last: both uses then share one cfe home and the frame keeps its size. Evidence: 42630 csCondEval f6b679 (fixed): case 0x1C's entry value and case 0x17's argument snapshot became one `s32 value;`, frame stays 0x78. Lead ruling: ordinary C (one generic value local with the same role in two disjoint cases).
