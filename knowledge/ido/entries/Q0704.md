In menuInitMainScreen (menu/3E10.c) five menuSpawnUiElementEffect calls pass `(void *)(s32)&D_80721444`. Dropping the casts, or retyping the extern to u8[]/s32, shrinks the frame 0x38 -> 0x30, grows .text by 16 bytes and rematerialises the address for each call instead of one lui/addiu hoisted ahead of the argument loads. Evidence: .scratch/CleanMenu3E10/t_a.c, t_f.c, t_g.c, trace_menuInitMainScreen.log.
