menuUpdateElementTargetAndColor (cfe ucode of a hack-free body):

- The outer jump-table switch on `type` reads the local directly (`xjp`); the inner compare-chain switch on the same local copies it into an s32 temp (store/load per compare). Adding `default: break;` keeps that temp: the compare-chain lowering makes it, not a missing default (one of Q0944's open candidates).
- Two compound-call wobbles in different statements (`y += mathSinF(t) * 10.0f`) share one f32 temp word.
- The f32 temp and the s32 switch temp never share a word, although their live ranges do not overlap.
- Frame order: block-scoped locals, then the f32 temp, then the s32 switch temp (-52, -56, -60).

In a function that already has a compare-chain switch, replacing an ascending `x == A || x == B` test with a switch shares the existing s32 temp: no frame word is added (extends Q1654), only colouring changes (0x931c/0x932c, 55/57 lines).
