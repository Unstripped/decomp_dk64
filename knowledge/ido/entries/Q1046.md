The guard can depend on a constant-folded conditional branch at the case entry: `if (1) {}`, `if (0) {}` or `while (0) {}` before the case's first statement, or wrapping that statement in `if (1) { ... }`, works; placing it after the statement, or using `;`, a label, braces or `(void)0`, does not. Narrows Q0698 for switch cases.
