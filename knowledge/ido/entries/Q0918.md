In `lensFlareDrawSource` (src/global/103AB0.c, 2026-11-15 cleanup unit), the target's `G_SETCOMBINE` display-list command has the words `0xFC119623`/`0xFF2FFFFF`. Decoded, that is `gDPSetCombineMode(..., G_CC_MODULATEIA_PRIM, G_CC_MODULATEIA_PRIM)`, with the same combiner in both cycles. Rounds 20/21 used a different combiner macro and blamed the resulting mismatch on the frame or stack layout, so time went into the wrong lever. The procedure is to decode both combiner words from the target bytes into their a/b/c/d color and alpha fields for each cycle before you choose a `G_CC_*` macro. Only after the immediate constants in the `lui`/`ori` pairs match should you look at the remaining diff as a frame, register or schedule problem. A wrong combiner shows up as different immediates and can hide or imitate other differences.
