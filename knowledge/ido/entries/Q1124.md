Polishglobal80150b (unit "2026-11-16c: cleanup Polishglobal80150b", merge d932a13c), src/global/80150.c tagBarrelActorUpdate (frame/stack layout; related Q0601): a local array's element order and the declaration order of separate scalar locals are interchangeable stack-layout levers: `f32 coord[3]` with reversed indices (coord[2] = x) and `f32 effectX; f32 effectY; f32 effectZ;` in natural order give the same slots (x at 0xa8, z at 0xa0) for locals live across calls; z,y,x declaration order swaps them. A reversed-index array that only reproduces slot placement is a hidden steer replaceable by natural scalars.
