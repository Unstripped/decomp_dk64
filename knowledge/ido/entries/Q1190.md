In 42630 csCmdExec, `if (found) {}` (e8c5ea) kept the entry-initialised `found` flag of case 0x1C off a2. Merging `found` with the u8 `b` flag of cases 0xE/0x7D into one function-level `u8 bit` declared at found's position (home sp+0xC3; pitch7 moved into the freed byte at sp+0x7F) removes the guard: the merged candidate's 0xE/0x7D pieces already prefer a3 (a call's fourth argument, andi a3 at 0x301c/0x30ec), so uopt colours its entry piece a3 too (move a3,zero 0x2140, sb/lbu a3,195(sp) 0x3848/0x385c, bnezl a3 0x38a0). Keeping b's position instead scores 2 (spill home sp+0x7F). Lead ruling as in Q1158: ordinary C, one generic flag local in disjoint cases.
