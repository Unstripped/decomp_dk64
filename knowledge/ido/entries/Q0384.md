three effect spawners in 89250 matched only with `if (x)`: the explicit compare on a `u8` param creates a zero-extended (`ucvt`) candidate that gets its own spill instead of the param's caller home slot (`sb`/`lbu` at `0x4F(sp)`).

A related shape is a redundant `?1:0` before `||`. In `(x ? 1 : 0) || f()` the target materialises a temp: `beqz`, then `li v1,1` in the delay slot, then `sltu`. A plain `x != 0 || f()` drops the branch (uopt `ucg1` on the cvt result). The natural form is a flag local set in an if/else (`if (x) flag = 1; else flag = 0;`), which reproduces `move vN,zero; beqz; li vN,1` then `sltu`; the else assignment matters (init-to-0-then-if is 3 lines off).
