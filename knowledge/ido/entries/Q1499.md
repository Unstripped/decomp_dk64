Extends Q0143 (a missing trailing `break` gives one shared exit; see Q0538, Q0344, Q1152): in a jump-table switch that ends a loop body, an explicit empty `default: break;` makes every arm's exit carry its own copy of the loop-test reload. `default: continue;` compiles the same as `default: break;` (fdiff 0). Without a default arm, every arm branches to one shared exit.

Evidence: boot/3D20.c vsprintf (unit Cleanvsprintfc, merge 4e290fff):
- With the default arm: 9 copies of `lbu v1,0(t3) / li a0,37 / b 0x13a8 / move v0,v1`, at 0x8f8, 0xd4c, 0xd5c, 0x1050, 0x10d4, 0x1218, 0x12fc, 0x138c and 0x139c.
- Without it: every arm branches to one shared exit at 0x1340; 1221 -> 1197 words, fdiff 49.
