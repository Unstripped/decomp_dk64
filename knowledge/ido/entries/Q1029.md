A store that uopt deletes can still do the work of two dead tests: it (a) keeps a preceding compare chain alive like a trailing dead `if`, and (b) adds the blocks a dead two-term test would. It stays a steer when the target lacks its arithmetic. If the same chain is computed into a fully dead separate local, uopt deletes the whole chain.

Evidence: playerTurnByStickX (global/E4090.c): after `if (v > 2048) v = 4096 - v; if (v > 400) v = 400;` the store `v = v * (field / 400.0);` (the live line in the sibling playerInputUpdateMoveTargetWithDeadzone2) replaces a trailing `if (v > 1.0f) {}` and a two-term dead test in the clamp arm; fdiff 0, two FAKE lines -> one (still FAKE: no mul.d at 0x1a88). The chain on a dead `dist` local is deleted whole (11).
