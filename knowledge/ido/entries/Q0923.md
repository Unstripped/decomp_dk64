textMarkerRenderString (global/9DD70.c), commit 69922c (fixed). Take a byte local whose address is never taken. `v = K; call(); v &= M;` folds to a single `li` of (K&M). `v = K0; ...; v |= K1; call(); v &= M;` behaves differently. uopt folds K0|K1 into the store (`li 0x13; sb`), then after the call it reloads the variable from its home and masks it (`lbu; andi; sb`), matching target 0x4704-0x474c. So when a byte constant is stored before a call and reloaded and masked after it, write it as a compound update on the earlier value (`flags |= 0x10; ... flags &= ~1`). Don't use an address test (`if (&state == 0) {}`) or `volatile`. The worker inferred this and did not trace the mechanism. Also, at c7e04f, `switch (actor->unk155)` can be written directly once the flag updates are natural. The 15-line cost of the direct switch came from the other steer.
