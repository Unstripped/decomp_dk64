In `pathPlaybackAdvance`, `x += (s32)f; f -= (s32)f;` (the cast repeated) instead of `whole = (s32)f; x += whole; f -= whole;` lowered register pressure enough that the steering pad dead-store/dead-if pair the file had carried became unnecessary (cleanup round 9: FAKE 5 -> 3). Before keeping a pad or dead-if steer around a float-to-int split, try the repeated-cast form.
