long display-list writers matched only once every packet used the standard macro (`gDPSetPrimColor(glist++, ...)`, `gDPLoadTextureBlock`, `gDPSetTileSize`); hand-assembled `0xFA000000 | ...` words rotated `$t5`-`$t9` across later packets (`effectBuildGdlTexture32x32RGBA`, `effectBuildGdlTexture32x32RGBAWithPrim`, `effectBuildGdlTexture64x64CI4`).
