In func_806B13B4 (global/B5EC0.c) the K&R spare argument reads the switch selector in the case block, keeping it in $a1; without it uopt creates a switch copy candidate uadd(sel,0) live from the dispatch block into the comparing case block, coloured $v0 (move v0,a1 in the beqz delay slot at +0x240). The only other match found is a copy-temp selector (`t = sel; switch (t)`), itself a steer, so the proto-steer stays.
