Calling `func_80726EE0(0)` when declared with an unneeded second argument (`s32, s32`) caused IDO to believe `0x14` was an argument to both `func_80024D0C(1, 0x14)` and `func_80726EE0(0, 0x14)`, causing `li a1, 0x14` to be emitted in the delay slot of `jal func_80726EE0`. Calling `func_80726EE0(s32)` correctly allows `0x14` to be hoisted before the preceding branch likely instruction, leaving `move a0, zero` in the delay slot.
