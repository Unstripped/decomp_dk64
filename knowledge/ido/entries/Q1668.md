In playerStateWaypointAimUpdate, `cur = gWaypointCursor; next = gWaypointCursor + 1;` matches: the target loads gWaypointCursor once (`lw t0,0(a3)` at 0x3438) and keeps `next` as its own variable (`addiu t1,t0,18` at 0x3460, spilled to 56(sp)). Every spelling through `cur` (`next = cur + 1`, `&cur[1]`, `next = cur; next++;`, `cur` assigned just before `next`) lets uopt copy-propagate `next` into `cur`'s offsets (`lh t7,18(t0)` / `lh t8,22(t0)`), grows the frame 0x50 -> 0x70 and scores 156. The second read of the global is an ilod that copy propagation does not see through (Q0510), which is what keeps `next` a variable. Because the target loads the global once, the re-read is a steer by the load-count rule and is tagged `// FAKE` (site 543a88).
