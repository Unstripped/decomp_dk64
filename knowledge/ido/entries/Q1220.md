In global/ACDC0 hudPauseMenuIconUpdate, the wrap `if (i >= 5) i -= 5;` with a literal lets uopt strength-reduce the i<<2 web across the decrement (`addiu v1,v1,-0x14` after `addiu v0,v0,-5` at 0x490c instead of `sll t4,v0,2`). A count held in a local (`s32 kongCount = sizeof(sPauseKongColors) / sizeof(sPauseKongColors[0]);`) blocks it and matches (fe48fd fixed).
