uopt's redundant-constant elimination deletes an arm's `x = K` when x already holds K on entry (it was set to K before the `if`), so the arm carries no `li`. The only form found that keeps the arm's `li` is an intervening store that kills x between the two assignments. Related (Q0789): `count++` twice from zero stays an `addiu v0,v0,2` in the variable's register; only an expression uopt folds after copy propagation gives `li v0,2`.

Evidence: mpDrawScoreGlyphs (multiplayer/0.c): 0x844 `li v1,255`; the early alpha load (5df330) is the killing store, and removing it gives text 11. audioBuildTask (global/6710.c, 0x550 / 0x580; 8e31ef/#2 kept).
