When assigning the return of an unprototyped or `s32`-returning function to an `s32` local with an explicit cast `temp_s4 = (s16) func_800253C8();`, IDO emits `sll $s4, $v0, 16; sra $t6, $s4, 16; jal func_806119FC; or $s4, $t6, $zero`. Declaring `func_800253C8` as returning `s16` or omitting the cast avoids the sign extension and schedules `or $s4, $v0, $zero` directly into the delay slot.
