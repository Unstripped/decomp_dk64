A local initialised before a loop and incremented inside it (`x = 0; ... x++`, s16) but never read is not removed: it keeps a callee-saved register, and the init (`move s5,zero` at 0x60) and narrowing increment (`addiu s5,s5,1` + sll/sra at 0x1a8-0x1b8) appear in the target (func_806BD170, global/C1E70.c). Such instructions mark original code (`// ORIG:` with offsets), not a register steer.
