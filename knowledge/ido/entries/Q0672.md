Under IDO 7.1 -g every local has its own stack slot, so a parameter copied into a local before use shows as `lw` (param home) / `sw` (local slot) / `lw` plus an 8-byte larger frame. In 141520 n_alSynSetFXParam this is the libultra ALFx shape `struct FxParamState *fx = fxType;` passed on to n_alFxParamHdl: write the typed local, not an untyped copy tagged as a steer (Rev18B, round 18).
