In vsprintf's exponent digits, a dedicated `short` hundreds-digit local (with `pad[4]` -> `pad[3]` to keep the frame) is still copy-propagated into its `sb` like the single-use division result of Q1062, but it aligns the later t-ring registers: 3 lines vs 55 for an `int` local, 25 for `unsigned short`.
