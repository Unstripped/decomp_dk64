File src/global/109660.c, gfxDrawMapBackground. Both gotos u8a880/#2 stay goto-hand kept with offsets (Q0591). 1. CONFIRMS the 2026-11-17f 15050 lever on a jump-table switch: a colour local set in three cases and feeding one gDPSetFillColor at a goto join keeps the F7000000 constant in the shared tail (one `lui 0xf700` at 0x38bc). The colour stays in t0 across the join and the frame grows 0x40 -> 0x48. So three per-case F7000000 stores in the target mean three source calls. 2. PROPOSED RULE, extending Q0953/Q1209: in a single jump-table switch where N cases each run their own prefix and then share one tail, only the case written just before the tail can fall into it. That leaves N-1 forward jumps. When the switch end is the common exit for the other cases (here 22 branches to 0x39d0), `break` cannot supply those jumps. Removing the gotos then needs a second dispatch (Q1156: no threading), a loop or `do {} while (0)` with `continue` (Q1208/Q1209), or Duff-style case labels (Q0953); all are steers. The minimum goto count can be read straight from the target asm, and such gotos are the original's structure (goto-hand). Related Q0591, Q1156.
