cfe evaluates every switch selector into one shared int temp (-12vreg in actorTargetCourseHostUpdate: cand 39, stored bits 43/144/145) and reuses that same temp for a later inline `?:` result. uopt colours the temp once (here 4 = a1), so the selector of both subState switches and the 0x303/0x304 animation argument share a1 with no user local: `lbu a1,341(s0)` at 0xd18/0xf40, `li a1,772` at 0x12c0, `li a1,771` in the delay slot at 0x12d4 before `jal` at 0x12d8.

A user local that holds both the selector and the argument (the old FAKE form) is a steer standing in for this temp. The natural source switches on the field directly and writes the argument as `c ? A : B` in the call. Each half alone (field switch with the local kept, or a separate anim-id local) is fdiff 1; together they give fdiff 0.

This supersedes Q0162's claim that the shared register needs a reused user local. Found by LeverScout26 and confirmed by the merge (858decc1).
