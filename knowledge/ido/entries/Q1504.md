In the IDO 7.1 `-g` files (`GLOBAL_G_FILES`), indexing a table as `K - x` compiles to splat's interior label at element K indexed by `-x`: `D_80756490[127 - x]` is relocation-equivalent to `D_8075658E[-x]`, and `(&D_80756490[127])[-x]` works too, so the split label can be retired. This contrasts with Q0955/Q0854, where at `-O2` this kind of fold misses.

Evidence: global/1435C0.c n_alEnvmixerPull and _pullSubFrame (unit Polishglobal126260, merge 4ac3d202).
