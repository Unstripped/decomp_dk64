Extends Q1127/Q0908. In global/36880 modelPendingQueueInsert, nesting the loop body under `if (gPlayerSlots[i].active != 0)` and writing `if (gPlayerSlots[i].active == 0) continue;` give different blocks for uopt's priority accounting; the continue form removed the dead `if (px) {}` (40ccd1) and the `zPtr` alias with fdiff 0. A dead guard after an if/else join inside a loop body can be standing in for that continue structure.
