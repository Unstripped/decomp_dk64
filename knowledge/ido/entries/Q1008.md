A later redefinition of a variable (`offset = (u16)offset` after `b = f(offset)`) blocks copy propagation of the earlier value like a dead store, but it does not create a separate narrowed variable ahead of the chain: the narrowing is emitted where it is used.

Evidence: eepromSaveRecords (global/104F0.c): the redefinition puts `andi t8` before `addu a0,s5,t8` (66 lines) instead of the target's separate `andi v1` at 0x1cfc ahead of the b chain (0).
