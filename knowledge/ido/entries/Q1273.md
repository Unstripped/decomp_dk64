File src/global/11BC10.c, effectInterpolationUpdate 6e5dc3 (kept). Refines Q0737, Q1234 (2026-11-17f correction), Q0860, Q1069: cfe emits a right operand holding a subscript or `*p` load first only while the statement's ucode stack is empty; an operand emitted after a sibling value keeps source order and its `sub` gets no `swp` (Q0737's side-effect case is one instance). Evidence (ucode line 289 at 852c9300): `p->progress` emits `end[i]; start[i]; swp; sub`; `(*p).progress` emits `progress; start[i]; end[i]; sub`; `p->progress * (start[i]-end[i]) + end[i]` pushes end[i] first then source order. Any explicit `*` in the operand triggers it, including `(*effect).interp->progress` (permuter).
