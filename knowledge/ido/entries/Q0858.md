In carryObjectUpdate (global/9DD70.c) nested call results in arguments take cfe hidden temps (the u32 one shares the switch-selector slot, Q0522) and add no uopt temp words; the missing temp-area words came from expressions used twice in one block (inline `(a - b) * (a - b)`, Q0205), so naming the differences loses slots. Frame = uopt def Mmt len + save/outgoing area (0x48 here), rounded to 8: comparing cfe and uopt def Mmt len gives the uopt temp area size. The 0x70 word is the strength-reduced IV of `k * 2`.

**2026-11-15c addendum:** more evidence that nested call results own words: modelEmitterSpawnScaledParticle (global/68EA0.c, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackmodelEmitterSpawnScaledParticle2", 0012cea1) matches with the three random calls inline as arguments and only scaleFactor declared; cfe's hidden temps for the nested call results take exactly the words the named randX/randY/randZ homes took (40/44, and 48 owned but unused).
