In 936B0 projectileSparkleUpdate (0x384c-0x387c) the non-andi constant -2 is loaded once (`li v0,-2`, placed before the flags branch),
stored by `sh` and reused by `and`, for both spellings. The chained form was an m2c artefact; the two-statement form is the natural C.
