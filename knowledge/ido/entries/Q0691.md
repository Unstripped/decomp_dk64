In `raceRenderCoinHud` (race/7BD0) a redundant `f32` copy existed only to grow the frame by 8 (0x80 -> 0x88). An unused `s32 pad[2]` (or `f64 pad`) reproduces it only when declared FIRST; declared later, or as a plain `s32 pad`, it is pruned (fdiff 1). Refines Q0642 for frames that need two words.
