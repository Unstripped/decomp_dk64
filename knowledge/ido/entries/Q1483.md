Refines Q1241 (a local aggregate initialiser reproduces a function-only .data template): when the template is copied into the local on every pass of a loop, the initialised local is declared in the loop body, and its position among the block's other locals follows the target's slot order. With configs/durations declared in an inner block after pick/angle, they took lower slots (configs at sp+0xBC, not sp+0xC4) and failed; with every case-6 local declared in the loop body, in the original order, it matched. Related Q0905, Q0056, Q0386.
