IDO folds `x / 4.0f` into `* 0.25f`; a divisor variable assigned `4.0f` before an intervening clamp keeps the target's `div.s` (`func_8071B89C`).

**2026-11-16d correction (HackeffectInterpolationUpdate2, merge d3665c0c):** a constant-valued divisor local is not needed: an int-literal divisor keeps `div.s` (Q0354), so `(f32)timer / 4 * 3.0` matches func_8071B89C (now effectPuffExpandFadeUpdate) with no divisor local (`lui at,0x4080` at 0x49a8-0x49c0); only a float-literal power of two (`/ 4.0f`) is folded into `* 0.25f`.
