In B2CE0, consolidating the `behaviourSpawnExplosionAndSound` proto steer (removing PROTO_SKIP and the local unprototyped declaration) also let a stray 4th argument from m2c (`&gActorGlobalState`) be dropped. The callee takes 3 arguments, and the extra one only loaded an unused $a3 under the unprototyped declaration (fdiff 0).
