Extends Q1307 point 4 (a `(u16)` operand cast emits an `and 0xffff` ugen record that as1 later deletes): the deleted `and` still takes a scratch register from the ring wherever the cast sits, so moving the cast moves which register the ring hands out. Placed on the other operand of the earlier subtraction (`dx = (u16)col - st->gridX`), it shifts only col's `andi` register (t9 instead of t8 at 0x744; 2 fdiff rows). Related Q0583 (ugen scratch ring).
