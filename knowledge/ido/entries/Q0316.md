`if (!(f & 2)) f |= 0x40; else f &= ~2;` gives `bnez` with the `|=` as fall-through; `switch (g) { case 0: case 1: }` gives `beql`/`beq`/`b` where an if-chain does not; `if (x & 0x80000000)` gives `sll 0; bgez`; `(flags << 8) < 0` must be an explicit shift, not a bitfield.
