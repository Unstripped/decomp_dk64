cfe lowers a multiply by a power of two and the equivalent shift to the same ucode, so switching between `x * 4` / `x *= 4` and `x << 2` / `x <<= 2` cannot change ugen's scratch-register ring or any later codegen (measured in hudPauseMenuRender).
