within a basic block uopt replaces a load of `x` after `x = E` by `E` (E becomes a CSE candidate whose colour hints ugen, and a var the target spills to its home gets a temp slot). Kill it with a def of an operand between store and use: a dead store `v = 0;` when v is dead after, or the no-code `v = (1 | w) ? v : v + 1;` (int). A no-code kill on a local copy of a formal is copy-propagated away.
