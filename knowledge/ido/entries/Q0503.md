`.alias`/`.noalias` records come only for registers derived from `la`; an indirect store through a cast address (`*(s32 *)(u32)&D = ...`) avoids the re-emit; any non-`.loc` directive between a block's final `b` and the next label blocks as1's entry-stall hoist; a pointer local initialised in its own statement before the `for` gives the FIFO `lui/lui/addiu/addiu` preheader order.
