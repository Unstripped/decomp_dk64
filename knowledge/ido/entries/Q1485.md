Extends Q1241/Q0905: the .data template of a function-local initialised aggregate is emitted at the function's position in the file, in source order among the file-scope .data definitions. To keep a template at its carved address, every .data global defined between the old template global and the function must move below that function; otherwise the layout shifts.

Evidence: global/103AB0.c (unit Polishglobal103AB0, merge 0dad2ce0): D_80754C08/D_80754C48 moved below zbufferReadDepth so its template stays at .data+0xd4; screenFadeUpdate's template is last, at +0x1d8.

The same rule was reported independently by unit Polishcritter0b (merge 19f78d0a):
- critter/0: with critterScaleFactor left above critterInitGroupType3, its template landed at .data+0x780 instead of 0x77C (fdiff 1); moving the definition below critterInitGroupType2 gives RELOC-EQUIV.
- boot/1630: sNoExpPakTextLine1-6 had to move below noExpPakThread.

Related Q0838, Q0802.
