Extends Q0656. Indexing a row of a 2-D byte-pair table, `s8 t[][2]`, scales the index as `i * 2` (an `sll`); uopt then shares that value with an `i * 2` already computed nearby (here the s16 goal-table index `gWorldGoldenBananaGoals[i]`) and drops the `addu` that rebuilds it. The target (critter/3340.c critterBananaFairyUpdate) has `addu t3,t2,t2`, i.e. the `i + i` spelling into a flat `s8 []`: `pair = &gWorldSecretGoalDiscounts[worldIndex + worldIndex]`. So the split-off table (TypesU2, gate 3ddcbd5b) stays a flat `s8[16]` of (discounted count, kong id) pairs: `[8][2]` with `pair = t[worldIndex]` is 2 instructions shorter.
