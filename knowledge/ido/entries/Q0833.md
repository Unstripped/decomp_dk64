Do not assume `for (; c;)` and `while (c)` compile alike: in boot/3D20 vsprintf every `while` respelling of a `for (; width-- > 0;)` padding loop costs 2 words (13 respellings measured). Keep the `for` form there and measure before converting loop styles.

Evidence: vsprintf (boot/3D20.c, merge cc138d52).
