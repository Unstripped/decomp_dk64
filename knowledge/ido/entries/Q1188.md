In CEAE0 playerUpdateCommon, `(u32)attackerIndex != gActivePlayerState->playerIndex` (s8 block local `attackerIndex`) needed the cast because the local was an isvar numbered before the playerIndex load. Reading `gActivePlayerState->attackerPlayerIndex` directly in the test and as the `gPlayerSlots` index makes both operands loads numbered by first appearance: plain `attackerPlayerIndex != playerIndex` gives the target's field-first `beq t2,v0` at 0xe54 (playerIndex first: 1). Before keeping a cast that orders a compare, try the compare without the local.
