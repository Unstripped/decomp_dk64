A local struct initialised from a global in its declaration (`T t = gTemplate;`) is copied at the declaration point, before any statement, in declaration order of the initialised locals. Declaration initialisers in call-then-copy order therefore reproduce a target that copies a template before a NULL test.

Evidence: jetpacAllocActor (jetpac/4FC0.c, FAKE 1 -> 0).
