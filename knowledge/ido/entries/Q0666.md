A prototyped s16 parameter makes the caller narrow an int-typed register value with `sll`/`sra` at the call site; a u8, u16 or s32 parameter, or no prototype, avoids it. The real owner prototype `dlSetTextureScaleAndFlip(s16, s16, u8, u8)` matches only where the caller's values are already s16.

**2026-11-15c refinement:** the local feeding such a parameter should stay `s32` even when its only use is the s16 argument. In playerStateSetFlagsByActorType (global/EFDC0.c, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup PolishglobalEFDC0c", 3e2aadad) a switch-assigned local passed only to `camStartPath(Actor *, s16, u8)` matches as `s32`; declaring it `s16 ev` adds the sll/sra sign-extension and an sh/lh spill across mathAngleBetweenPoints2D (fdiff 1).
