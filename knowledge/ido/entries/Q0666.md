A prototyped s16 parameter makes the caller narrow an int-typed register value with `sll`/`sra` at the call site; a u8, u16 or s32 parameter, or no prototype, avoids it. The real owner prototype `dlSetTextureScaleAndFlip(s16, s16, u8, u8)` matches only where the caller's values are already s16.
