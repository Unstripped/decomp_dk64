ugen's scratch registers `$t6/$t7/$t8/$t9` (`$14/$15/$24/$25`) come from a **FIFO free list**: an allocation takes the front of the queue, and a register is appended to the back when its value dies (at its last use, freed in textual operand order: `addu d,a,b` frees `a` then `b`; `sb v,0(p)` frees `v` then `p`; a dead def is freed immediately). The initial queue is `[14,15,24,25]`; labels and branches do not reset it. Simulating this over a whole `t.s` listing (`as1probe keep`) reproduced every ring register of three textBlitGlyph variants and of the old park with zero mismatches (script `.scratch/argon/tbg/fifo.py`, prints the queue at each label).

Using it backwards is only a hypothesis generator. The FIFO model holds for ugen's emission order (the `t.s` listing), but the target is known only as final asm, after as1 has scheduled it, hoisted across blocks (XBB, Q0548), filled delay slots and renamed registers. Inverting the target's ring registers therefore requires assuming which ugen instruction each final instruction came from and in what order ugen emitted them. A ring register that seems to be the queue front (a narrowing `and X,$10,255`) followed by an arm that needs X earlier than the back of the queue says only that, *under that provenance mapping*, something differs: possibly extra ugen alloc/free events that as1 deleted (redundant narrowings, forwarded reloads), but equally a different ugen order or block placement of the visible instructions. Before searching for C that adds a hidden event at a specific place, establish provenance: compare your own pre-as1 listing (`as1probe keep` `t.s`) with your own final asm for the same block to see which instructions as1 moved, merged or renamed, and only then map the target. Known as1 renames: the `or` whose result is stored and immediately reloaded takes the reload's register, and `move` targets fold into the producing instruction.

Extra narrowings of a u8 store (`x = y; x &= 1;`, `x &= 0xFF`) make as1 collapse the `move/andi/move` header to one `andi`. They are not a way to add an invisible ring event.

**Landed (leftovers revisit 13, `textBlitGlyph` matched, no FAKEs):** (1) rename the park's `t.G` ring register fields all at once to the target's names (`.scratch/R13_sonnet_xhigh_2/rn.py`); full=0 proves the diff is ring names only. (2) A per-statement DP over alloc/free event programs (`.scratch/R13_sonnet_xhigh_2/chain*.py`) finds where hidden events must sit; model a narrowed web's observed name as the narrowing result's register (as1 renames coalesced webs, forwarded reloads and webs whose `and` it deletes to the later web; webs coalesced into variable registers are unobservable). With those rename semantics the DP had a unique solution: `dst = dstRow;` had to move right after the `dstRow` line (entry-end queue `[14,25,15,24]` instead of `[25,15,24,14]`), and the shift statements needed a hidden mask event (Q0374).
