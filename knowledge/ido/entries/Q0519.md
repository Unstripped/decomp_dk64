cfe puts the deeper operand of `*` on the left; `CHAIN * (j = i + 1)` puts the assigned operand left instead (`multu s2,t9`). Matched `eepromValidateSaveSlots` with `((u32 *)D_807ED6A8)[idx]` instead of `(BASE + 0x800)[idx]`, which folds 0x800 into the load displacement.

Corrected 2026-11-15 (HackeepromCopySlot, site 2e4194, pre-existing now tagged): the embedded assignment is a dead-store steer, not evidence of a live `j`: `SIZE * (j = i + 1)` is the only spelling giving `multu s2,t9` at 0x14b4, and `j` is reset before any read; `SIZE*(i+1)`, `(i+1)*SIZE`, a separate `j = i + 1;` and `j*SIZE` all give `multu t9,s2`. Treat it as a cfe operand-order lever and tag it.
