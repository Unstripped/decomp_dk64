Many permuter runs that reach score 0 on a FAKE site only replace one steer with another: `if (1) {}`, an empty do-while, a goto to the adjacent label, or a `(0, x)` comma wrap (Rev16G, round 16, across boss/0, 6AD90, 70FD0, 80150 and CEAE0). Record those as `permute nohit` with the hack named in the note; take a best=0 output only when it is ordinary C.
