Generalises Q0188 from u8 to every unsigned width. With a u8/u16/u32 `siz` parameter in renderRotatedSprite, the caller hudPauseMenuRender materialises `li a3,2` (0x1638/0x16b8) although the int constant-2 web sits in $s8 (`li s8,2` at 0x12a0); an s32 parameter gives `move a3,s8`; s8/s16 add sign-extension.
