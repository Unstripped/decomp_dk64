uopt folds a single test of a local defined as `x + 1` into a compare of x against -1: `tq = quot + 1; ... if (tq)` becomes `li at,-1; beq quot,at`, even when tq is defined in another block or also feeds `loopCount += tq`. The target's `addiu v1,quot,1; beqz v1` needs a second use of the same value on the same path; a use on a sibling switch arm does not count.

Evidence: pathPlaybackAdvance (global/127B00.c), variants M1/U1/T2 (f5ff39).
