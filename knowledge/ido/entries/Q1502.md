A guarded do-while with two counters (`i`, `byteOffset`) matches as one `for` that initialises both: `for (i = 0, byteOffset = 0; ...; i++, byteOffset += 8)` (Q0830's rotation). Putting `byteOffset = 0;` before a one-counter `for (i = 0; ...)` swaps their saved registers (s0/s1): the order in which the two counters are initialised decides their register order. Related Q0557.
