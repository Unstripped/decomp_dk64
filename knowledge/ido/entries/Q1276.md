Extends Q0737 to integer multu (see Q0519): with D_807ECEA0 as a scalar, `(i + 1) * SIZE` keeps the written order (`multu s2,t9`) and `SIZE * (i + 1)` does not; with the constant-index element `D_807ECEA0[0]` cfe evaluates SIZE first in either order. The `_sprintf` destination addu in eepromSaveRecords (0x1b84) flips the same way under the retype.
