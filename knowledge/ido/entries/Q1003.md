IDO 5.3 emits a file-scope `static const f32 k = 1.2f;` into `.data`, not `.rodata`, and its load in a scalar's right-hand side is a plain `lod` (not an `ilod`), so it does not stop the scalar's forwarding. A named float constant therefore cannot replace a literal that the target loads from `.rodata` (boundary of Q0822/Q0558).

Evidence: raceSteerTowardTargetWaypoint (race/6650.c): the named constant grows .data by 16 bytes and the scalar is still forwarded.
