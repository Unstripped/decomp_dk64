Extends Q0606/Q0813: indexing a `void *` global through a cast, `&((T *)g)[i]`, puts the base first in the `addu`; the typed spelling `&g[i]`, or `g[i].f` at every use, puts the offset first. Base-first is reproduced only by `&g[0] + i`, a no-op `(T *)` cast, or `p = g; p += i;`. The last form is new relative to Q0813, where `entry = p; entry = &entry[i]` stays offset-first.
