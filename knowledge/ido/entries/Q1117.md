Same unit/function (cfe arguments): a matching first argument narrowed with sll/sra rules out an unprototyped (K&R) call as the cause of an unmasked narrow second argument: the caller saw a prototype with the narrow first parameter. A local `(s32, s32)` or K&R `()` declaration drops the font sll/sra (target 0x8c8-0x8cc) and gives move a0,v1 at 0x8cc instead of move a0,t1 at 0x8d4. See also Q0779, Q0486.
