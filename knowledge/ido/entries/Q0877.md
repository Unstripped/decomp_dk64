In camFloorSnap (global/201B0.c) a local loaded at function entry in the same uopt block as the first call gets a needregsave cost there; its priority drops to 0 and it is split and left on the stack (141 lines). A no-code label between the load and the call (a dead `if (1) {}` anywhere before the call) restores the caller-saved t2 with saves around each call; a label after the call or a bare `{ }` does not. Narrows Q0563 (1) to the defining block and separates it from Q0559's node count; the dead test stays FAKE.
