an int literal to an f32 param emits `lui/li`; a float literal used twice becomes one shared FP candidate (and a `0.0f` used in many calls can starve a later constant: spell it `0.0`/`0`); `* 2` (int) emits `mul.s` by a loaded 2.0f while `* 2.0f` emits `add.s`; `(f64)x * 0.5f` stops sharing the 0.5 double across blocks; `/ 2.0` and `* 0.5` are separate constants; IDO rounds the literal `0.029` to `…0419` (write `0.028999999999999998` for `…0418`).
