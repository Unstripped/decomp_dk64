In effectFallingSparkUpdate (global/11BC10.c) the target reloads `effect->motion` after a call by reading the field again. With `f32 *state = effect->motion` the local only adds a spill (sw/lw v1,0x54(sp)); writing every access as `*(f32 *)effect->motion` matches and retires the tagged re-read (bfb579).
