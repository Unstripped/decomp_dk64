In an else-if chain testing one masked value, writing the common subexpression out in every condition (`(w & 0x600) == 0x200` / `== 0x400`) lets uopt CSE it once into a register and order each compare constant-first; assigning it to a local first gives the reversed operand order. Prefer the repeated expression over operand-order hacks such as `(op | 0) == K`.

Evidence: gfxSwapGeometryModeCullBits (global/17B90.c, FAKE 6 -> 5): `bne t3,v0` at 0x2b4 and `bne s0,v0` at 0x2cc with the masked value CSE'd into v0; this replaced `(op | 0) == 0x400`.
