Arcade data carve (2026-11-18 static-data unit, ArcadeCarve): defining the 0xE220-0x26790 pool (~156 definitions) at the top of src/arcade/0.c gave identical .data but flipped 0.c's .bss order (gArcadeLifts vs arcadeUpdatePlayerEntity's function statics), changing 20 %lo words in .text. Scans with n dummy `int dK = 0;` definitions near the top: the order is right for n mod 256 in [168,255] U [0,23] and wrong for [24,167], period 256. Only the count of definitions matters (int d[1000] counts as one; file-scope statics and tentative definitions count; unused extern declarations do not); a definition after the last bss user has no effect. Fitted: at base lifts = 232, statics = 88 (statics first); with the 156 definitions lifts = 102, statics = 166 (lifts first). A symbol takes its number where it is defined. Refines Q0565 (first use): within a TU, .bss is emitted in ascending (symbol number mod 256). Practical rule: when a data carve reorders .bss, the data may belong to a separate data-only TU (here src/arcade/E220.c, linked ahead of 0.c, whose .data starts 16-aligned at 0x8004A790).
