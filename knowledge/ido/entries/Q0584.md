Two candidates with equal priority (here `fadeInput0 = hud->unk0` and `fadeInput4 = hud->unk4`, each used by one compare and one move, adjsave 16.5 both) are coloured in **candidate-index order**, and the index is the order of first appearance in the ucode — not the declaration order. In case 2 the target loads `unk4` first (`lwc1 $f12,4(t3); lwc1 $f2,0(t3)`) yet `unk4` holds `$f12` and `unk0` `$f2`; writing the loads unk4-first flips the colours of both case 2 and case 3 (`c.lt.s $f2,$f12`), writing them unk0-first flips the load order. The fix is a first appearance that generates no code: a dead `fadeInput0 = hud->unk0;` in the prologue (removed by dead-store elimination after the candidate table is built) gives `fadeInput0` the lower index, so case 2 can load unk4 first and still colour it `$f12`. Using the variable for real earlier (loop 1's `fa`) also fixes the order but costs a `mov.s` there. `uopt_trace.sh -l 5` shows it: `>>>active<<<{id} IDX REG` with identical `adjsave`, the lower IDX taking `$f2` (25) and the other `$f12` (26, `forbidden: [24 25]`).
