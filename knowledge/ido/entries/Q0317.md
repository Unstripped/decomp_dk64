`(s16)(x + c) * 4` passed to an `s16` param is a 5-temp chain, `(x + c) << 2` (narrowing left to the param type) a 3-temp chain; each float→int conversion (`trunc.w.s`) and each `(s16)` cast in an int chain consumes an invisible scratch temp even when its `sll`/`sra` folds away. Count missing allocations from the third operand of `cc -S -non_shared` output (`trunc.w.s $f4,$f0,$8`) instead of guessing. `(x + c) * 4` is distributed to `x*4 + c*4`; `<< 2` keeps add-first. Dead expression statements do not shift numbering.
