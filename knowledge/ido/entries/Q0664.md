Under IDO 7.1 -g a value reused inside one `&&` condition is not reloaded, but splitting the condition into nested ifs (separate statements) reloads it from its stack slot. Use that instead of `volatile` locals (13ECE0 __n_* at 8073A130/8073A2A4).
