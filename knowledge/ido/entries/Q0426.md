two equal `0.0f` args in a2/a3 become `mtc1 zero; mfc1; mfc1` (spell the second `(f32)0`); an integer `0` store to an f32 field or `x > 0` in an abs ternary gets its own `mtc1 zero` instead of a shared zero register; `(f64)(v ^ 0) * 0.5` keeps a runtime conversion that `v = 0xFF` would fold; a narrow local (`s8 m = 0xFFF7`) blocks constant propagation and keeps `-9` in a register; `expr / 255` and `255.0` are separate `.double` entries; `crc ^ -1` is `li at,-1; xor`, `~crc` is `nor`.
