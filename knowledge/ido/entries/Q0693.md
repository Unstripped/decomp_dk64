In arcade/0 a no-op `(i ^ 0)` in a `!=` loop test survives local optimisation as a `uxor` candidate and stops EQ_INEQ from swapping the `bnel` operands; seven natural loop spellings all lose the target order, so the site stays a tagged steer.
