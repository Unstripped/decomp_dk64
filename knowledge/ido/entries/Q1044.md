(a) u8/u16 scalars live across calls get coloured; s8/s16 stay memory-resident (onGround adjsave 0.6 vs 0). (b) Local arrays are word-aligned (`u8 x[2]` below an s16 pair lands at -28, not -26). (c) Block-scoped locals never share slots between sibling blocks, so nesting cannot replace a reuse. (d) One merged guard can replace two coupled dead guards but may push a live range across a call's argument setup.
