Value-named s8 locals for a field load and its write-back (`gx = st->gridX; ... st->gridX = gx + dx;`) take t-registers without dead tests. A subtraction of variables only, though, is copy-propagated into a later compare and creates ucvtl candidates. In C0EE0 enemyGridStateStep, any new 4-byte local grows the 0x88 frame by 8 (Q0976). 716d92 permute: Setup baseline 995, best 75, 900 s, 31636 iterations.
