Where a pointer field is tested and then dereferenced, the placement of the named copy decides its register: `if (p->f) { t = p->f; v = *t; }` (copy inside the taken branch) loads into `$v1`, while `v = *p->f` directly or `t = p->f` before the test load into `$v0`.

Evidence: slotQueueGetValue (global/FCCD0.c): the in-branch copy matches the target's v1 loads at 0xb44/0xb60/0xb68/0xb74.
