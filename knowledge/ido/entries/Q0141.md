passing a live variable as an extra trailing argument to a K&R-declared callee (`void func_8072DE44();` called as `func_8072DE44(0x2D7, sel)`) pins it to `$a1` by argument-register preference and fixes the allocation of other temps; the target's `$a1` at the call is the real evidence (`func_806AFB58`, `func_806AE588`, `func_806B13B4`).

The inverse also happens: a spare argument that the original did NOT pass steers colouring the wrong way. `textDrawStringVtx` (leftovers revisit 13) passed a stale fourth argument (`yoff`) to `vtxSetQuadGradientColors`, which its definition in 103AB0.c takes with three; declaring and calling it with the real arity freed `yoff` from the a3 preference, and the value then fell into the first argument register the callee does not use once higher-priority ranges had taken the callee-saved ones. Check callee arity against definitions (`unit_check.py protos`) before keeping any extra argument.
