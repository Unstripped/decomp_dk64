NEW (see Q0474): `width * 10 + *f++ - '0'` builds `uadd{ucvt 1255}{umpy 1437}` with the lower-numbered expression left (`addu t0,v0,t6`); `width *= 10; width += *f++ - '0';` puts the variable left (`addu t0,t6,v0`, target). Brackets, reversed operands, `10 * width` and a separate `f++` keep the one-line order (vsprintf 0x320/0x3cc).
