the macro's own shape `{ Gfx *_g = (Gfx *)(dl++); _g->words.w0 = ..; _g->words.w1 = ..; }` keeps post-call reloads in their home registers; `gDPSetScissor` with variables does not match (the target computes `(uly & 0xFFF) | 0xED000000 | (ulx & 0xFFF) << 12`); it does match once its coordinates are real struct fields (`rect[0]`..`rect[3]`) rather than locals or casts (`gfxDrawBackgroundQuad`, `gfxClearScreenSetViewportScissor`, 109660, cleanup round 10); multi-command macros (`gDPLoadTextureBlock`, `gDPLoadTextureBlock_4b`) must sit on ONE source line, and a random search over vertex-store order plus one-line joins matched two glyph-quad builders (as1 reorders stores within a line, often in reverse). Some functions need the raw spelling where the macro matches elsewhere: `hudDrawCountPair` and `hudPauseMenuRender` (ACDC0.c) keep `dl->words.w1` written before `w0` with a separate `dl++` for `gDPSetPrimColor`, and raw words for `gDPPipeSync`/`gSPEndDisplayList`, because the macro form fails fdiff there (cleanup round 9; kept as tagged steers).

Another instance in 126260 `actorBuildLightDisplayList` (cleanup round 13). The hand-built words `0xDC08...` (G_MOVEMEM), `0xDB02...` (G_MOVEWORD) and `0xE7...` matched once replaced by `gSPLight`, `gSPNumLights` and `gDPPipeSync`, with the same store order.
