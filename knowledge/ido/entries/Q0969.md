In `schedDlDebugDump` (src/global/13980.c, cleanup unit 2026-11-15, fixed in 9a3ebd), where the zero initialisers sit in the source sets the order of their `move rX,zero` instructions at 0x11a4 after as1 scheduling. Putting each initialiser directly before its first use, with `colorIdx = 0` just above `if (mode != 0)` and `found = 0` just above the for loop, gave the target order. This replaced a dead `if (G && G) {}` that the earlier match had used to force the same order, so a fake statement of that kind can be a sign that the initialisers are in the wrong place. This adds to the statement-form levers in Q0483, such as `var = 0; do {...}` versus `for (var = 0; ...)` moving `move s0,zero`. Before adding dead code, try moving the initialisers.
