A dead read placed before a loop contributes its block the same way the loop's own entry test block does: once a constant-trip loop is written as a do-while (no entry test), the dead `if (G) {}` has no effect, and neither block alone breaks a colouring tie that needs both.

Evidence: eepromSaveRecords (global/104F0.c): with the CRC loop as do-while, `if (D_807ECEA0) {}` scores 52 with and without; the 179/181 tie is not broken by either block alone.
