In global/114270.c a value-named `age = K; ... alpha - age` was an artefact of m2c's local copy of a field (`v = p->f; flag = v < K; p->f = v + 1;`). With the literal, uopt forwards `ules{v,K}` into the following `if (flag == 0)`: the flag's store disappears and its compare lands in a ugen temp (t9) instead of $v1.
