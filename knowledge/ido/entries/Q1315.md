EXTENDS Q0767/Q0559, the 2026-11-17f 7130 loop-form lever (see also Q0890, Q0093, Q0399, Q0908). The rule (uopt loop/ constant/cse-hoist): a pre-tested `while` whose entry test is always true (`i = 0; found = FALSE;` just before it) still keeps its entry-test blocks, with edges straight to the block after the loop. Anything computed only inside the loop is then not available on those edges. Consequences: - a constant that a later loop reuses is loaded again at the after-loop block (`li a0,-1` in the exit branch's delay slot, at 0x54c and 0x590); - a later `a[i]` recomputes its address from i (0x63c-0x670) instead of reusing the loop's induction pointer. A do-while has no skip edges, so it shares both. uopt -l5 evidence: the while form loads its one -1 constant (lr 192) at bb9 and bb16, and bb16's predecessors include the entry-test blocks bb6/bb7; the do-while form loads it only at bb7 (lr 190). This one rewrite replaced three FAKE kinds: a dead typed read, an unsigned/signed constant split and a no-op `i * 1` index.
