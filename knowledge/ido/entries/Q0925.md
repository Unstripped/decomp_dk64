In global/FCCD0.c the file-local K&R `s32 slotQueueGetAddress(player) u8 player;` was retyped to ANSI `SlotQueueSlot *slotQueueGetAddress(u8)`. The header was regenerated, and the function has no other caller. That one change retired all the caller steers: slotQueueGetValue's `pa = &player` (ccffb3) and dead-if 4ccde5, and slotQueueAddValue's `(u32)player` (78fc23). FCCD0 FAKE count went from 10 to 7. Under K&R, the u8 param shares the `(int)` conversion with the s32-param uses, and the steers were working around that. Under the u8 prototype, an s32 local holding `(u8)i` adds a `move` (0x49c). Declaring that local `u8` instead gives `lbu a0` followed by an `sw a0` word store. That word store is the u8 value's SPILL, not evidence of an s32 local. This revises the Q0808 frame-census reading that was queued for slotQueueAddValue. Check a callee's K&R/ANSI form before keeping caller-side width FAKEs. Merge e4b2e515, ledger c32f53cb, base f5885203.

**2026-11-15c refinement:** retiring a K&R prototype steer can require removing hidden call-site masks in the same edit. In bitfieldSet/bitfieldClear/bitfieldHasMappedFlag (global/FCCD0.c, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackslotQueueInit3", cf977754) an explicit `x & 0xFF` passed to a u8 callee only steered under the K&R s32 declaration; under the u8 prototype plain `x` emits the same `andi a0,a1,0xff` (0x1428/0x1468/0x14ac) and keeping the mask adds andi+move (+16 bytes). Likewise bitfieldHasMappedFlag's `andi t9,v0,0xff` at 0x14cc is the u8 return conversion of an s32 value, not a source mask (PolishglobalFCCD0c, a1bb4d3c). The K&R artefacts in slotQueueInit are corrected in Q0808/Q0886.
