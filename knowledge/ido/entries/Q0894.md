In `vsprintf` (boot/3D20.c, commit 9bc6c1), the target has `bne a0,v1`. Comparing the loaded value directly (`*f == '%'`) instead of a variable puts the constant on the left, the same way the `(c ^ 0)` steer does (uequ on an ilod, constant left). But it also adds one use to the converted-load candidate ucvt(*f). That raises its adjsave from 73.5 to 78.5, past the competing `len` (78.0). Because ranges are coloured in descending priority (Q0163), ucvt(*f) now takes a0 and the constant 37 moves to a1. The swap shows up across 0x160-0x1b4 and at every case-end reload, 29 diff lines. So an operand-order fix (Q0488) can cost a register swap elsewhere. Before using a lever that adds a use, check the adjsave margins of nearby candidates.
