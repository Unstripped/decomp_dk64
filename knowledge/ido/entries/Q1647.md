This extends Q1116 to a u16 pointer load. `while (*p != 0)` compares the load's int promotion in the entry block, where a parameter living in a0 blocks it from a0. The bare `while (*p)` keeps the promotion loop-only: it takes a0 and emits no code. It is the seventh register of Q0682, with no ghost.

Like Q1230, the spelling changes regalloc and emits no code, which settles Q0682/Q0724 for this function. inputRemapButtons uopt trace; `and a3,v0,t0` at 0xbc8.
