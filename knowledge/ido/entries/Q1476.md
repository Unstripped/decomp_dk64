IDO does not reassociate a left-associated float sum to combine its literal addends: `y + 48.0f + 4.0f` is `(y + 48.0f) + 4.0f` and keeps two `add.s` with two literals. Writing `y + 52.0f` makes .text 16 bytes shorter (fdiff 1). Two consecutive literal adds in the target are two source literals, not a constant to simplify. Contrast Q0936 (a product of two adjacent literals, `0.35f * 0.5`, is folded) and Q1130 (uopt reassociates some field sums).
