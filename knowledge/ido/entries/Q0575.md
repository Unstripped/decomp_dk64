In polygon clipping loops, an explicit pointer copy `cur = (MapVec3 *)arg0;` was originally introduced as a fake steer to give `cur` an earlier candidate priority over `arg4` (/ coloring). Writing the predecessor element pointer as `prev = cur + (arg2 - 1);` naturally chains `prev` from `cur` and preserves candidate discovery order in uopt without requiring fake steering comments or artificial assignments.
