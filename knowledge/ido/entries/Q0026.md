`lui $a2, 0x427C` is `63.0f` for `void func_80609140(s32, s32, f32, f32, s32, s32);`; keep that prototype (used by every overlay) and pass float literals.

So passing an `f32` field to an `f32` parameter in argument slot 3 compiles identically to passing its bits through an `s32` parameter: the float travels in `$a2` either way. A `*(s32 *)&field` pun kept for such a call is not a steer; type the declaration's parameter `f32` and drop the cast (`actorChildEffectUpdate` calling `actorCopyPoseAndScale`). A `union { f32 f; s32 w; }` local instead of `*(s32 *)&pitch` makes `pitch` memory-only (a `swc1` to its home slot at every definition), while the address-taken f32 with the pun stays in `$f2`. The pun is the cheaper spelling here (`sfxUpdateLevelAmbient`, 7130).
