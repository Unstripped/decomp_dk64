In `zbufferDepthToDistance`, `mid = -1; if (depth == mid)` instead of `if (depth == -1)` gives the constant its own web, which changes the register choice of the later `lo`/`hi` loop variables (15 register differences with the literal). Such a local stays tagged `// FAKE` (a value-named constant local) until a natural form is found.
