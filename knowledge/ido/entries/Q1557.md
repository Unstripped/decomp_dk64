minecart/0 minecartFollowTrack dfe0c8: a u8 local built up over `jt op= x` statements has priority 2.0/4.0/6.0 for 1/2/3 assignments
and at 6.0 outranks a 5.0 pointer local (v0). Any assignment whose right side is an expression is folded into its later use (temps
addu t5 / andi t9); a bare load through a pointer is not (lbu v0,0x40(s1) 0x1da4). So a single-block local cannot both outrank the
pointer and emit nothing in v0 (three-statement copy full=2, two-statement 140). The reused loop counter i stays FAKE.
