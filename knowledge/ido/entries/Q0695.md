In `mapNodeAssetParse` the plain `gMapZones[idx]` spelling differs from the target only by an s0/s1 swap between `p` and the slot address. Anything that splits the uopt basic block right after the `p += vertCount * 6 / 2 * 2` advance (in tests, a goto to an adjacent label) matches fully, so the kept `s32 slot` hack is standing in for a block boundary, not for the temp itself.
