Extends Q1629: an ordered address compare as a loop exit test (`sltu` + `bnez`/`bnezl` against an end address hoisted out of the loop, no unroll) is a construct no index bound reproduces. A literal trip count unrolls (42630 csModelIdTableReset: `bne` after 4x) or is LFTR'd to `bne`/`bnel` (C0EE0 enemyGridStateStep 0x508). So `&a[i] < &a[N]` is ORIG when the target shows the sltu (cc0f6b at 0x18; 706cd2 at 0x4dc / 0x508; ruling R4 2026-11-24b). Q0582's "LFTR gives sltu" applies only to non-constant bounds.
