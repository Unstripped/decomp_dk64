In hudPauseMenuRender the block-split steer d4f3b3 (`if (sPauseKongUnlocked == 0) {}` at the function end) also matches as `if (0) {}` or `if (1) {}`: the orphan label alone gives the split (Q1662). It cannot stand in for a guard whose job is to keep a value in memory: b371d1 `if (list == NULL) {}` scores 5 as `if (0) {}` after heapFreeDeferred. Both stay steers; the probe tells which kind a dead `if` is.
