`x <<= 2` enters `ushl{x}{2}` at the assignment, and later pointer arithmetic `D + x` reuses that candidate; `x *= 4` and `&D[x]` (`uixa{D}{umpy x 4}`) do not. So writing the scale as `<<=` gives a later call-crossing `D + x` spill temp an early candidate bit, which moves it up in spill-slot order. `&a[i]` (uixa) and `a + i` (ushl/add) never CSE with each other.

Evidence: menuEvaluateSelectorSpline (menu/3E10.c, FAKE 3 -> 0): the spill temp's bit 28 -> 14 (t0 sp+0x2c, a3 sp+0x28 at 0x97dc). Details: drafts/quality/dossiers/menu/menuEvaluateSelectorSpline.md.
