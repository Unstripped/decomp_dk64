Hackvsprintf12 (ledger 778fd04d, body of merge 13dc7afc), boot/3D20 vsprintf: open-coding each of the 25 uses of the tagged `PUTC` do-while(0) macro (d0cc22) on its own shows that only three uses steer: the numeric zero-pad loop (59 lines when open-coded), the exponent-sign else arm `PUTC('+')` (4; the then arm `PUTC('-')` is 0) and the hundreds digit (67). The other 22 uses open-coded together score 0; all 25 open-coded score 71. A macro tag's steering can be confined to a few uses: measure per use before treating the macro as load-bearing everywhere, and name the steering uses in the tag.
