When a function taking a leading pointer/integer followed by 3+ floats calls a callee, IDO reserves space for caller home-slot spills above the frame; declaring an unused local 3D vector (`f32 pad[3]`) below the parameters places the callee-saved spill slot at `0x18($sp)` rather than `0x1C($sp)` (`actorAppendModelNode`).
