In arcade/0 arcadeDrawTitleText, `if (D_8004C6DC & 0x182) D_8004C6D0 = D_80032C20;` with the store on the if line (else on the next line) puts `addiu v0,%lo(D_8004C6D0)` before `addiu t8,%lo(D_80032C20)`. Splitting the store onto its own line, writing the whole if/else on one line, or a ternary each give text=2; as1probe shows only removing the then-line `.loc` fixes it, so the layout is kept and tagged `// LAYOUT:`.
