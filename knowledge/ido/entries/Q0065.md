writing a squared 3D distance as one flat sum `(x-px)*(x-px) + (y-py)*(y-py) + (z-pz)*(z-pz)` without temporaries makes IDO keep the three coordinates in callee-saved `$f20`/`$f22`/`$f24` (frame 0x20); with temporaries the leaf keeps them in `$f12`/`$f14` (frame 0x18) (`playerGetMinSquaredDist3D`).
