In `schedDlDebugDump` (src/global/13980.c, 782b9f, fixed), the neighbouring .bss variables were carved into one struct. A 12-byte carve of 4-aligned members starting at `0x807F0564` was placed at `.bss+0xa8` instead. That 8-byte alignment padding grew the unit's `.bss` from `0xb0` to `0xc0`. A 16-byte carve starting at `0x807F0560`, which is 8-aligned, kept `.bss` at `0xb0` and matched. This extends Q0395: the rule that 8+ byte objects in `.bss` are 8-aligned covers structs as well as arrays, whatever their member alignment. A carve that starts at an address ≡4 mod 8 will move and grow the section. Widen or shift the carve so it starts on an 8-byte boundary, and treat any leftover neighbours as interior labels.
