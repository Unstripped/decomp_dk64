the dispatch `lbu v1; move a0,v1; beqz v1; move v0,v1` (copy for the case compares in the delay slot) comes from `u32 x = f->u8field; switch (x)` with `x` used only by the switch, or from a copy chain through an existing local (`var_a0 = F; x = F; y = x; switch (y)`); the arms re-read the field. `s32 x` emits no switch copy, `u8 x` swaps `v0`/`v1`, and two-variable forms put the moves in the wrong order (`func_80028440`, found by a 1052-variant statement-order search).
