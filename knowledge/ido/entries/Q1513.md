Refines Q0908's block-split class (related Q0583, Q0698): "any spot in the competing range" does not hold when that range ends at the return. A class-1 block split then works only between the last store before the return and the load of the returned local. In hudPauseMenuRender, `if (1) {}` between `gSPEndDisplayList(dl)` and `return ret` matches, while the same `if (1) {}` at the end of case 3 or before the end-DL scores 347, the same as removing it.
