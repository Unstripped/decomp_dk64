In functions where scalar 4-byte variables must land at odd 4-byte boundaries (`0x3C, 0x40, 0x44, 0x48, 0x50`), IDO aligns the top local variable to the frame boundary (`0x54` for a `0x58` frame). Inserting an unused `s32 pad;` at the very beginning of the local declarations consumes slot `0x54`, shifting all subsequent variables down by 4 bytes so that all scalars align to their expected slots.
