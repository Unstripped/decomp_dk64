unconstrained uopt candidates are coloured in order of first reference (LHS before RHS); dead early assignments (`rx = arg3;`) or statement reordering move a variable to a lower register. Constrained ones go by adjsave, which rises with reference count (`head = A; head = (mid - head) >> 3;`).
