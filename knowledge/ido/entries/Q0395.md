initialised `.data` scalars are 4-aligned; in `.bss` `u8 x[3]` aligns 2, `[4..7]` aligns 4, arrays of 8+ bytes align 8 (any element type). Retyping a carved object wider makes its neighbours interior labels (`undefined_syms.us.txt`).
