`(p + i)->f` builds the field address as a separate shared `uadd` candidate with the other `+` operand first; `p[i].f` folds the address into the load and loads the field first in the enclosing add. In `modelEmitterSpawnScaledParticle` this shows as a `div.d`/`cvt.d.w` schedule difference; the `(p + i)->` spelling matches.
