In IDO 5.3 on MIPS big-endian, declaring a 5th+ parameter as 'u8 arg4' natively emits 'lbu $reg, 0x13(sp)' due to stack slot alignment. Declaring it as 's32 arg4' and reading '*(u8 *)((u8 *)&arg4 + 3)' is an unnecessary hack that should be replaced with the natural narrow parameter type.
