When dead guards keep a local coloured by lengthening its range past a loop, check whether a same-role value elsewhere in the function is carried by another local: giving that use back to the guarded local supplies the range naturally.

Evidence: menuRenderOptionDescription (menu/E550.c): three empty guards (`if (x) {}` in the tile loop, `if ((s8)menuOptionDescFade && xo) {}` twice after it) kept xo coloured after the hoisted `y<<2` / `(y+0x29)<<2` candidates. xo is the screen x everywhere, but the description x was carried by the tile column `x`. Using xo there gives its candidate bb 9-13,17,18 at adjsave 104.67 (just below the hoisted shifts' 105); x loses its post-loop range and stays in v1; all three guards go (fdiff 0).
