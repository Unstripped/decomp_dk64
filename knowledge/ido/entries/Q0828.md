as1 schedules by source line (Q0396, Q0321, Q0149): two loop updates written on ONE line (a comma, or a one-line `for`/`while` body) let one update's s16 truncation (`sll t2,v1,0x10`) move ahead of the other's `addiu`; on separate lines they miss by that instruction. The ordinary-C form is a counting loop with the increment in its header, `for (hundreds = 0; val >= 100; hundreds++) val -= 100;`, which replaces a comma FAKE; tag it `// LAYOUT:` because it depends on line layout.

Evidence: hudDrawRollingCounter (global/90D80.c, 0x1d40): separate lines full=2..8; FAKE comma retired (merge a89ccabe).
