raceOpponentBehaviourUpdate (race/0.c) had two reuse steers, and both were fixed by removing the local rather than by giving the tagged use a local of its own. (a) `stateValue` was reused across two cases. Spelling the dispatch as `switch (controller->unk35)` directly (case 5 increments the field) removed the switch use, and the freed local became the plain `pathId` local (f88a4d). (b) Reading `gCurrentActor->y` in place at each of its two uses matched where every local spelling failed. uopt merges the repeated indirect reads into the same single candidate that the reused local had produced (see Q0820 for how candidates are keyed) (5c6e36). Rule: when a local seems to need reuse for an unrelated value (Q0739), first try removing that local from all of its uses. Merge 9af6884b, ledger 2f2cd9c5, base 7d7d5cfe.
