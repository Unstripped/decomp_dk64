With `if (A == NULL || A()) { body }` the body reuses the condition's address on the NULL path; `if (A != NULL && !A()) continue;` makes the body reload and recompute it at the join (volumeTriggerUpdateAll, global/1332B0.c: beqzl with the reload in its delay slot at 0x3f8, recompute at 0x410-0x420), replacing a mixed-spelling re-read through a second pointer.
