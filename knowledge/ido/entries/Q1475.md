The operand order of a float relational compare against a constant is not a lever: `0.0f < v` and `v > 0.0f` give identical code, both as an `if` condition and inside a ternary used as a compare operand (fdiff 0). Write whichever reads naturally; a reversed m2c order is not a steer to keep. Compare Q0474 (uopt canonicalises `==`/`!=` operand order) and Q0454 (integer compare operand orders that do matter).
