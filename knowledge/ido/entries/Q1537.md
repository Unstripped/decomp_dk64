3E10 menuUpdateElementTargetAndColor: the compound `elemActor->y += mathSinF(t) * 10.0f` takes a cfe temp word (-52, sp+68).
Routing the sine through `radius` (unused on that path) frees the word for a block-scoped `MenuUiRgba *color` (3 FAKE reads removed),
with the same uopt candidate number (64). With the call result in a variable, `x op= v * K` loads x before materialising K; `x = x +
f() * K` evaluates the product first and recolours the FPRs (case 7 0x8f50-0x8f74, case 14 0x93cc-0x93ec). The two
`radius = mathSinF(t);` lines are FAKE (R5: the sine is used from f0, never stored).
