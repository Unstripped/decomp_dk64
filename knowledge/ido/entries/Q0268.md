A dead `if` / `do {} while (0)` the permuter inserts before the last statements of one arm usually asks for its own join block: write an `if`/`else` instead of the early return, or a `switch` instead of `if`/`else`, and the block appears naturally (`playerCheckSlopeLaunchArc`, `playerStateRunDecel`).
