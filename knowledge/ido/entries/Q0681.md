An explicit `(s32)`/`(s16)` cast on a `u8` array index (`&gPlayerPadState[(s32)gCurrentPlayerIndex]`) gives the stride constant (0x34) its own web, exactly like `^ 0` or `+ 0`, and ugen then expands that multiply as shifts instead of `multu` with the shared 0x34 register (`playerInputRegisterAnimatedEffect`). `(u32)`/`(u16)` casts and plain pointer addition do not. Such casts are hidden steers, not fixes.
