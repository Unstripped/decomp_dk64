The shape `lbu a0,off+3(sp)` from an int variable's home followed by `sw a0,slot(sp)` is an s32 local assigned `(u8)i` (a narrowed copy kept in its own word), not a dead guard on the loop index.

Evidence: slotQueueInit (global/FCCD0.c): target `lbu a0,155(sp)` from i's home + `sw a0,120(sp)`; the s32 local retired the dead guard ab9e8a (director-accepted).

**2026-11-15c correction:** this reading is wrong. The word at sp+120 (`sw a0,120(sp)` at 0x4a0) is uopt's spill of the `(u8)i` CSE temp for an ANSI u8 callee, not a declared `s32 player = (u8)i`. The s32 local was an artefact of the K&R slotQueueGetAddress declaration; with the ANSI u8 prototype (Q0925) the natural `&inv->kong[f(i)]` + `slotQueueGetAddress(i)` matches (slotQueueInit, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackslotQueueInit3", cf977754).
