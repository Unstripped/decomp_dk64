In `HackraceOpponentBehaviourUpdate2` (kept function bcda7c, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackraceOpponentBehaviourUpdate2"), the chained spelling `s16 = u16 = (s32)expr` matches: the value store-forwarded (Q0499) for the u16 copy keeps its u16 narrowing, and ugen folds that narrowing into the float-to-int conversion, so no `andi` appears. The target at 0xd10-0xd48 reads `mfc1 $t3` / `sh` / `addu $t4,$t3,$t3`. If you use a single u16 local stored to its home instead, the narrowing is lost on the store and re-applied at every use as `andi ...,0xffff`. Writing `u16 * 2` drops the mask but emits `sll` rather than the target's `addu x,x,x`. So the doubling-by-add shape and the missing mask both depend on the forwarded chained-assignment value. This adds to the u16 width notes in Q0486.
