Every declared local owns a frame word even when it is never spilled, so deleting register-only locals can shrink the frame. Restore the size with an untagged pad declared AFTER the last kept local; growing an earlier pad array instead shifts the slots of the locals declared after it.

Evidence: audioBuildTask (global/6710.c): deleting two never-spilled locals shrank the frame 0x40 -> 0x38; `s32 pad2[2];` after the last kept local restores it with cmdCount still at sp+0x34; growing the earlier `pad[1]` to `pad[3]` moved cmdCount to sp+0x2c.
