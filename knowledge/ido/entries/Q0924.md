In bossFollowerActorUpdate (boss/DAA0.c), one `s32 id` holds both the else-arm anim id ($v0 at 0x11c4) and the mirrored-arm opposite side ($v0 at 0x1520). Its merged isvar range adds the adjsave of both uses and reaches 4.0. That ties the CSE candidate umpy{id}{2} (4.0), and `id` wins on candidate number (62 < 150), so `id` gets $v0 and the `*2` index gets $v1 (0x1530/0x1538). With separate `animId`/`oppSide` locals (1.5/2.5), v0 and v1 swap, giving a 9-line diff. Three dead guards had been standing in for that tie. History: 37830d/56bac3/e261f0 fixed it, then d51071 (copy-only) kept it FAKE, and the file went from 3 FAKE to 1. Rule: when a tie decides a v0/v1 pair, a shared local holding two values of the same width is the lever the guards were emulating.
