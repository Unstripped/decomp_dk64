When every case of a switch stores the same constant, uopt hoists the store (and its `li`) above the switch. A target `li` before the dispatch therefore does not prove that the original had a pre-switch store; compile the per-case form without the store before claiming one (for example in an `// ORIG:` note).

Evidence: func_806C8220 (global/CC800.c).
