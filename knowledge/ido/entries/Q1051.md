Against an array-load product, cfe evaluates the right operand first when it holds an array-element load: `sub4->scale[0]` (the existing union array view for scaleX) on the right matches, `->scaleX` on the right gives full=1. Refines Q0737. The lead kept the scale[0] spelling tagged.

**2026-11-16d extension (TypesOwner batch_8 part G, merge a0567b00):** the array-element rule also holds for an integer `&`: in critter/3340 critterBananaFairyUpdate the operand order at 0x9cc follows `D_807ECD48[0] & D_80029F90[D_80029F80]` once D_807ECD48 became an array element (the scalar read needed the other source order).
