IDO 5.3 uopt does not unroll a counted loop when its induction variable has a later definition after the loop; a dead `i = 0;` after the loop exploits this. A real later use or redefinition has the same effect, so look for one in the original before keeping the dead store as a steer.

Evidence: noExpPakThread (boot/1630.c, 880d3e kept: the source has no real later definition).
