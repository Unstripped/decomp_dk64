A parameter whose address escapes is not in the register candidate table even when the only escape is an unused local holding its address (`void *dm = &csId;`). Without the escape the s16 parameter becomes candidate 28 and only its call-free loop piece is coloured (t4, the hoisted `lh t4,210(sp)` in global/42630 csCmdExec). A caller-saved register coloured that way leaves ugen's temp ring for the WHOLE function, so every temp renumbers from the first case body (0x2184 t4 -> t5), far from the loop. The `uopt_trace -l6` candidate table shows it.
