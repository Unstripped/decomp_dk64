Pointer induction variables that uopt derives from an index are advanced by ugen at the statement incrementing the index, so a store placed after the increment uses a -1-element offset from the advanced pointer. That offset is part of source order (ugen emits it; as1 does not add it): place the increment where the target's offsets change.

Evidence: screenBlurApply (global/2DE90.c).
