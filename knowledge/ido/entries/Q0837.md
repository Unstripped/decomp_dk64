Owner-side proof for a caller's own declaration (extends Q0486): if the callee narrows or masks a parameter at entry and retyping the owner wider changes the owner's code, the owner's narrow type is right; a caller that passes the register without the prototype's masking (no narrowing before the jal) was compiled against a wider declaration, so its local declaration is the original view (`// ORIG:`). The same holds for a type-blind owner (it only stores the byte) when no single owner type matches in every caller. A 4-argument local declaration of a 3-parameter callee is likewise ORIG when the target loads a3 before the jal and the owner homes only a0-a2. Owner fdiff failure alone is not enough (Q0662); the call-site registers are the evidence.

Evidence (callers in global/42630.c, merge 2e019041; proto-steer sites 17 -> 10): owner modelAnimEntrySetUnkC (54150, sll/sra at 0x15c0/0x15c4; an s32 owner fails, 6592 vs 6608) with no narrowing before jal 0x29e0; owner sfxSlotAllocateSource (7130, `andi t7,a3,0xff` at 0x2c28; 33664 vs 33680) with a2/a3 unmasked into jal 0x306c/0x3144; type-blind effectSetDepth (119370, `sb a0` at 0x354): s8 fails 478F0/80150/89250/9DD70, s32 fails 119370, caller `li a0,1` at 0x51cc vs `andi a0,s4,0xff` under u8; hudSpawnCountdownTimer (owner A7710): `lui`/`addiu a3` &D_807F61F0 at 0x2600/0x2604 into jal 0x261c.
