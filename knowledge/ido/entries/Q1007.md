cfe/ugen canonicalise the operand order of a commutative `&` (`~7 & x` and `x & ~7` give the same ucode and candidate numbering). A colouring tie decided by first-appearance numbering (Q0900) therefore cannot be flipped by swapping operands inside one statement; the constant has to appear in an earlier statement.

Evidence: eepromSaveRecords (global/104F0.c): swapping all four sprintf masks leaves the 179-line diff unchanged.
