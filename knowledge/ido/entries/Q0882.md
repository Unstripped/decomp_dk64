In eepromSaveRecords (global/104F0.c) `volatile u8 *contReadPending = D_807ECD08; while (*contReadPending != 0) sleepMilliseconds(1.0f);` emits an unfolded pre-test (`lui/addiu t2` + `lbu t3,0(t2)` at 0x1dcc-0x1dd4) and reloads the pointer from its stack home in the loop (0x1de8); plain u8/void/s8/u32 pointers fold or colour the address (39) and a direct global poll is hoisted (224). A laundered second pointer in front of a poll can therefore point to a volatile poll in the original; the flag here is cross-thread (eepromThreadMain polls it, F600 controllerUpdate clears it), so the volatile is correct C (director ruling, ladder.md).
