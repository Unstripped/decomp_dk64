a prototyped narrow (`u8`/`s16`) param makes every caller narrow the argument (`sw a0` + `lbu`, or `andi`); callers passing `a0` raw mean a word-sized param. `collisionStoreSurfaceNormalAngles` matched as `(s32 unused, f32, f32, f32, u8, u8, u8)`: the unused word param still gets its entry `sw a0,home`, and the `lbu a0` is the stack `u8 arg4` modified in place. That removed a nine-nested `& 0xFF` fake and matched three callers. K&R definitions are no fix with floats (f32 params arrive as doubles). Check param signedness (`s8` vs `u8`) before any temp-numbering hack.
