`t->f += e` reloads after the store, `t->f = prev + e` forwards the register; `return sqrtf((e2 = X) / d2);` stores and reloads `e2`, a separate statement keeps it in a register; `volatile u8 v` gives a fresh `lbu` at every use; an address-taken local (`pv = &sp6F`) stops `lbu` hoisting across calls; a two-case `switch` written `case 0xBF:` then `case 0xB8:` reproduces the beq-0xB8-first layout that `if/else if` does not.
