Two consecutive loops that each reset one function-scope counter form a single uopt live range. Its priority sums both loops' uses (adjsave 310 vs 155 for a block-scoped counter), which outranks the loop pointer (255) for the lower register. In actorSub140CreateFromData this replaced `j += 2; j--;` split-increment steers: 0x2180-0x21b4 and 0x2238-0x2270 (`move a0,zero` / `move a1,a3`).

The counter must sit in the old pad slot to keep the homes: placing it beside the pads shifts sp+0x4C to 0x48 (score 8). The `move` order at the loop heads follows the source order k, j, dst.
