On an s16 local, a constant-minus step followed by a multiply keeps its literal steps only as two statements: `a = 0x3C - a; a = a * 5;` emits `li 60; subu; sll/addu` with no sign extension in between, while the merged `(0x3C - a) * 5` is distributed and folded to `negu` + `addiu 0x12c` (Q0859). A target showing the `li`/`subu` pair before the multiply was written as two statements. In the same function, a separate local for `remaining - 0x5A` loses the move coalescing the target has. Related Q0116 (two-step constant multiplies).
