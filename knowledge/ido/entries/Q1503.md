Extends Q1154 (a constant store to an extern global leaves its address web; `T *p = &G;` keeps the address in a register): the rule does not depend on the global being extern. With `jetpacInitPending` now defined in the same TU, plain access still gives `lui`/`lbu %lo(sym)` and drops the address that must stay in v0 (0x1168/0x11a8), so `u8 *initPending = &jetpacInitPending;` stays.
