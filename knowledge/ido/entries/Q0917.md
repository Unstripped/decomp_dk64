In lensFlareDrawSource (global/103AB0.c), commit 37e973 brought FAKE from 1 to 0. Before the fix, using the `gSPTexture`/`gDPSetCombine` macros grew the frame past the target 0x1C0. The fix dropped `pad2[2]` and moved the locals that only ever live in registers (`facing`, `colorScale`, `alphaScale`, whose homes at 0xF8/0xEC/0xE8 are never read) above the scalars that really live in memory (`screenX` 0x108, `screenY` 0xF4, `alpha` 0xF0). With that order, `screenY` and `alpha` keep their target homes and the three macro `_g` blocks land at 0xDC..0xE4. Rule: before writing raw GBI words to avoid macro `_g` homes, check which locals are actually read from memory and reorder the register-only ones. Home words are assigned in declaration order, highest first, so their position decides where the in-memory scalars and `_g` slots fall. This is the second case after menuRenderOptionDescription (menu/E550.c, Q0880). Merge 82113476, ledger 294e64b2, base 50711206.
