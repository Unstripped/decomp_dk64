In `if (*p++ & x)`, cfe evaluates the masked load into a temp (M -8vreg) before it emits the post-increment of p. p is redefined before the branch, so uopt keeps the temp as a candidate. Its bit precedes x's, so it is coloured first: a3 at 0xbc8 in inputRemapButtons (`and a3,v0,t0`). This replaced a separate test local in the FAKE form.
