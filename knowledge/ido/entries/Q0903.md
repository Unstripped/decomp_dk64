In `HackslotQueueAddValue` / `slotQueueAddValue` (merge ec24aa00; fixed at 5d0eea/57a90d), the target has `sw a0,40(sp)` in the delay slot of the `playerGetResolvedKongId` call and `lw a1,40(sp)` at 0x12ec. This stores a u8 param's value into the lowest locals word and reloads it with `lw` for an s32 argument. The slot is uopt's spill of the `(int)` conversion of the param, not a declared local, so adding a FAKE copy variable to produce it is wrong. Count the frame before adding any copy variable: 8 declared locals, 1 cfe argument temp (sp+0x2c) and this spill fill the frame exactly. The separate `world` local fits only after the FAKE copies are removed. This extends the frame census in Q0808 and the argument-temp rules in Q0427. Unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackslotQueueAddValue".
