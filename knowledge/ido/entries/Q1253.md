File src/global/42630.c, csCmdExec c14926. Extends Q1157, Q1194: the dead read borrows a3 between the two despawns and splits the `&gCsCountdown` address range; the middle piece stays out of a register only when that block has a single address use. A dead read of timerActor or active adds a second use, so the middle piece gets v1 (lui/addiu v1 at 0x268c/0x2690, one extra instruction, score 5); the hostActor read shares the second despawn's argument load (one use) and matches. A guard on bit's entry value without assignment does not split the range (9).
