In 55B20 (site f68e4b), the target loads `litVtx` into v0 and forms a1 from it. Every pointer-typed spelling of the arguments loads straight into a0, so the s32-typed arguments (a FAKE prototype steer, with 2 W712 warnings) are what select v0. It stays kept.
