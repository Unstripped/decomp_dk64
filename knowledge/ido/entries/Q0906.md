In `bonusControllerUpdate` (src/bonus/0.c, 31a06c, FAKE 1 -> 0), the guide and dossier said the fake `pv = &challengeType; ... *pv == 3` kept challengeType at sp+0x6F and the frame at 0x78. On the current body that is outdated. Without pv, the frame stays 0x78 and challengeType stays at 0x6F. The only change is that uopt's spill temp moves from sp+0x38 to sp+0x3c, which affects 12 `sw`/`lw` lines at 0x468-0x940. This happens because pv's home (vreg -52, sp+0x44) sat above the two cfe ternary temps (-56/-60), and spill temps start below the lowest home (Q0521). An unused `s32 pad44` declared at pv's position reproduces the layout. It is an untagged pad per AGENTS.md. Q0361 says unused scalar pads reserve nothing, so this case is a counterexample to that rule. Rule: if a steer-only address-taken local affects only its own slot, replace it with an unused pad local at the same declaration position. Then check the absolute `sp` offsets.
