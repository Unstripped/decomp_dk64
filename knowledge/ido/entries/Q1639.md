In menuUpdateLightFadeActor, `gCurrentActor->fadeValue != 0xFF` (either side) makes uopt emit neq $a0,$v1 -> li a0,255 / beq a0,v0 (0x1e8/0x1f8); copying the field into an s32 local first gives neq $v1,$a0. Removing the m2c copy local removed the FAKE constant local `maxLevel = 0xFF`. With the local kept, these did NOT flip it: (*p).f, raw u8 read, compare-only copy, declaration swap, widths, 0xFFL/0xFFU, block-scope local (scores 1-41). A constant-valued s32 local keeps its operand order only when assigned before the intervening call (jal 0x1dc); u8: no. Measured in this function only.
