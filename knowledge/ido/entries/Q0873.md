In activeActorBoxOverlapTest (global/36880.c) f32 `minX..maxZ` bounds set before the loop and read only in short-circuit compares are forward-propagated into the loop; the subtract/add stay after the coordinate load, but the radius conversions take f0/f2/f16 and the coordinate loads f18/f22/f24 (0x43d4-0x4418). This replaced a dead s16 copy and an alias steer (a permuter hint translated into natural locals).
