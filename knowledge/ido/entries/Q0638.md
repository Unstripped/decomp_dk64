The permuter's pycparser front end fails on `x * sizeof(T) + sizeof(s32)` and on a one-line `p = x; do {`. Rewrite those lines (split the statement, or precompute the size) in the permuter base only, then translate the hint back into the real copy (Rev16E, round 16).
