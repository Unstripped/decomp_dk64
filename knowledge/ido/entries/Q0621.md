In `camPathCommandDispatch` (2C910), the target computes `(u8 *)D_807F5D6C + -(a * 4)` (a `negu` followed by `addu`). Retyping the array to `struct GfSndState *D_807F5D6C[]` and writing `D_807F5D6C - a` or `&D_807F5D6C[-a]` under IDO 5.3 -O2 emits a different sequence and fails fdiff. The byte-offset spelling is load-bearing, so two W709 warning sites stay kept.
