an f32 literal also used in compares/stores is promoted to an FP register and passed as `mfc1 aN`; the target's `lui a3,imm` needs the int-bit literal through an unprototyped declaration. `0.0f` used several times becomes `mtc1 zero,$f0; mfc1`. Lerps `x = x + (r - x) * k` (not `+=`), call results inline as multiply operands (`a = f(b) * c`) and precomputed `fx`/`fy` call args fix `$f` order.
