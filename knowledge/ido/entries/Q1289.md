REFINES Q0800 (related Q0160, Q0344, Q0461, Q0591, Q1209): uopt moves a block up to replace the unconditional jump that targets it only when that jump is its only predecessor, nothing falls into it, and the block itself ends in an unconditional jump (the moved block loses its `.loc` lines). A `default:` falling into `case N:` can never be moved, so ugen keeps `b default` and as1 leaves Q0800's orphan store (a consequence of the uopt step, not its cause); `do {...} while (0)` makes no unconditional jump. A goto target label whose only predecessor is the goto and that sits BEFORE `case N:` gets the post-store reload at its head (0x238c); with `case N: label:` the reload goes at the end of the jumping block (0x2368, 3 lines): the reason behind Q0344's "label before case N".
