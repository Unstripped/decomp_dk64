declaring `s16 func_806CC10C(s16, s16);` grew `func_80665564`'s frame from `0x60` to `0x68`; `s32 func_806CC10C();` restored `0x60`. `textGetStringWidth` is defined K&R in 100180, so its generated prototype is `s32 textGetStringWidth();`. In `gfxResourceCreate` (1025E0), only a local `s32 textGetStringWidth(s16, u8 *);` plus its PROTO_SKIP gives the `move a0,t7` s16 narrowing before the `jal` and the `sw a3,0x7c(sp)` placement; with the unprototyped header declaration, fdiff is 1. Prototyping the definition in 100180 as `(s16, u8 *)` would retire the steer pair in every caller.
