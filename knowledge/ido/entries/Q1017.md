A selector test written as `switch (k) { case 0: case 2: ... }` instead of `k == 0 || k == 2` emits the same instructions but adds IR blocks across the loop, lowering the adjsave of loop-invariant ranges the way a dead `if (1) {}` does. Where the target really performs that test, the switch can replace such a block-count steer.

Evidence: actorCollisionRunInteractionTable (global/7A510.c): both forms give beqzl/bnel against a constant register; the switch reproduces the dead `if (1) {}`'s effect on actorB (s0) vs the gActorGlobalState base (s1).
