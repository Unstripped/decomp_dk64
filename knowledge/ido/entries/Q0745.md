mpDrawHud (multiplayer/0.c) starts with `s32 matchOver = D_807552E4.matchPhase >= 2; s32 paused = gGlobalPauseFlags & 3;`. The target loads both globals (lw at 0x126c/0x127c) before the gDPPipeSync/gDPSetScissor stores through glistp, which may alias them, so the read order is fixed by the source. These early locals are source, not steers (tags 33ea3b/924c0a dropped).
