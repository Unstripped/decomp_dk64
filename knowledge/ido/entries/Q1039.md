Resolves the Q0558/Q0907 boundary. A local cached from a memory load (`r = p->radius; t = r * 1.2f;`) is forwarded and its load shared, so `t` gets a register. Reading the load inline at every use (`t = p->radius * 1.2f;`, `x - p->radius` in later blocks) gives each block a load with count 1; uopt still merges them into one register ($f2) and `t` keeps a real swc1/lwc1 pair, uncoloured (0xd64/0xd68). Rule: before keeping an array, copy or guard to make a local keep its store/reload, delete the cached local and read its source inline.
