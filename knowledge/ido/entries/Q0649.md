`behaviourUpdateContactAndState` really takes one argument (the 12E800 definition is `(s32 arg0)` and writes a1 before reading it), yet the extra 2nd argument at the B2CE0 calls is load-bearing: dropping it and the PROTO_SKIP pair gives fdiff 1.
