A K&R `u8` parameter used directly as the switch selector is not masked: cfe fills the switch temp with `lbu` from the low byte of the parameter's home. A target with `andi a0,0xff` at entry, a word slot reloaded by `lw` for the jump table and a later store had a separate int local holding the narrowed parameter.

Evidence: screenWipeStart (global/2DE90.c).
