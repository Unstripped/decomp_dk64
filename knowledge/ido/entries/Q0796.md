A K&R `u8` parameter used directly as the switch selector is not masked: cfe fills the switch temp with `lbu` from the low byte of the parameter's home. A target with `andi a0,0xff` at entry, a word slot reloaded by `lw` for the jump table and a later store had a separate int local holding the narrowed parameter.

Evidence: screenWipeStart (global/2DE90.c).

**2026-11-17f correction (CleanscreenBlurApply 4a8bee9e):** Same file, screenWipeStart a8594a (fixed by director ruling). CORRECTS Q0796/Q0140: the signedness of the global a K&R u8 param is stored to changes how the param is held: `extern s8 G; switch (type) ... G = type;` narrows the param once at entry (andi a0,0xff 0x34), stores the cfe switch temp (0x54) and re-reads it for the jump table (0xb0) and the `sb G` (0x154); `extern u8 G` reads lbu from the param home at every use. An entry andi plus a reloaded word slot does not prove a separate int local: check the stored global's type first. sScreenWipeType is asm-defined (165D50 .bss), no C definition; its only other read (screenWipeDraw switch, lbu 0x3ec + sltiu 8) is the same under s8 (cases 0..7: uopt may use lbu).
