In tagBarrelCheckKongFlag (site 871b3e), a one-element local struct array read as `info->found` (array decayed to a pointer) makes the read a CSE candidate, as the tagged pointer local `p` does. `info.found` / `info[0].found` does not. Neither form gives a hack-free match: without the alias the found load lands in v1 where the target uses v0 (10).
