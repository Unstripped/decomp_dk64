Refines Q1122 (splitting a reused local adds a home) and Q1198: the frame does not always grow. In BA790 func_806B5A90 the case-0x37 fade step and the light step share one home in the target (`sw v0/zero,64(sp)` at 0x494/0x49c/0x4a0, `sw t7/v1,64(sp)` at 0x618/0x61c, reloads at 0x4b8/0x620); giving the fade step its own local shrinks the frame 0x50 -> 0x48. In A6280 actorSinkAndDespawn, splitting `i` or `node` grows it 0x68 -> 0x70. Read the stores to the shared home, not the direction of the frame change, as the evidence that the original reused the local (BA790's `x` carries `// ORIG:`; A6280's two sites are tagged FAKE).
