an argument passed with plain `move` where the callee is defined with `u8`/`s16` params (`effectWaterRenderSurfaces(Gfx *, u8, u8)` in 63EC0) means the caller had no prototype; declare `Gfx *effectWaterRenderSurfaces();` locally. The same holds for an `s32` local passed with `sll`/`sra` or `andi 0xFF`: those are conversions at a prototyped narrow parameter, so the local itself is `s32` (`mapChunksRender` `idx`).
