bonusBigBugBashGameUpdate (bonus/2690.c, site df8fd5) had `fx = 1 * 0.0f; // FAKE` to keep a second FP zero in $f20. The plain double literal `fx = 0.0;` does the same (a separate literal web); `0.0f` would share the literal register.
