When `func_80729E6C()` is declared `void (void)` rather than taking arguments, IDO uses the branch delay slot of `jal func_80729E6C` to spill live local `temp_a1` (`sh $a1, 0x2E($sp)`). Passing arguments to `func_80729E6C` forces IDO to use the delay slot for argument setup and causes an extra store before the call.
