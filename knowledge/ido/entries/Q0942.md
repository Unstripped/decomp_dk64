In projectileBounceParticleUpdate (global/936B0.c, kept at 9121f0; ledger-only fcb9632d, base 602f275c) the target keeps {dx, dx*dx, sum} in `$f0` and {dy, dz} in `$f2` (0x35cc-0x3604). It matches only when dx/dy themselves are reused, register-only, as the running sum. This is not own-home: the dx/dy homes are never stored. A separate f32 local does not inherit a register freed earlier in the same block. A separate dz never takes dy's `$f2`, even with dx squared in place (8 rows). A sum chain through a separate local (`dist = a; dist += b`, `d*d + dist`) is forward-substituted (35-42 rows) unless it is self-referential or loads from memory. Up to 7 named locals keep the frame at 0x78 wherever they are declared, and an 8th grows it to 0x80. The permuter ran 900 s and went from base 904 to best 679 without giving a natural hint.
