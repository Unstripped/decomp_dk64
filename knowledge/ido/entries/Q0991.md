A narrow struct field copied into an int-sized local before being passed as an s32 argument is loaded into `$v0` and moved to the argument register; passing the field directly loads it straight into `$a1` and reschedules neighbouring loads. Such a named temp is ordinary C, not a hack.

Evidence: actorScriptedCommandUpdate case 0xD (global/C4620.c): the target's load into v0 + `move a1` (806BFBF4+0x13b0) needs an s32 or u32 temp; passing the u8 field directly also moves the gBehaviourTargetActor address load, and u8/s16/u16 temps or a read-once-then-test form each leave fdiff 1.
