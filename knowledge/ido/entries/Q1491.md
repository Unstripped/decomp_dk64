Extends Q1459 (re-reading `p->f` in the test merges the two reads) to a re-read in a later statement: in `task = sc->head; if (!task) return; sc->head = sc->head->next;` uopt merges the two `sc->head` reads into one `lw v0` and copies v0 into the local's saved register in the branch delay slot (`move s1,v0`). Reading through the local instead (`sc->head = task->next`) loads straight into the local's register and swaps the s0/s1 save order. Related Q0994.
