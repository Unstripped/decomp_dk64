Extends Q0849: when consecutive loops share one counter, each next loop's `i = K` start is the later definition that stops uopt's EQ_INEQ step from rewriting the `<` exit. Exactly the slti loops in the target are those followed by a loop on the same variable (arcade/0 arcadeDrawStage3Background: slti at 0x3770 vs li/bnel at 0x37d4). One counter reused by consecutive loops is ordinary C.
