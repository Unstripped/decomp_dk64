Under IDO 7.1 `-g` the increment of a two-iteration counter loop belongs in the loop condition (or a `for`): `i = 0; do {..} while ((i += 1) < 2)`, `for (i = 0; i < 2; i++)` and `do {..} while (++i < 2)` give identical objects (fdiff 0), while `i++;` as its own statement before `} while (i < 2);` adds 16 bytes.

Evidence: configureCSPOutputMode (global/13C920.c).
