A declared scalar spills to its home slot; only expression (isop) and field (issvar) candidates in `coloreditems` get `spilltemps` slots, which start right below the lowest declared local, top-down in candidate (first-appearance) order, rounded to 8 for f64 (holes stay even when the candidate ends up callee-saved). Preheader temploc order of hoisted invariants follows first-use order in the loop body. A scalar declared after an array takes `lowest - 4` and pushes every temploc down.
