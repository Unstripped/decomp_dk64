For `D[i][0] == st->f` cfe emits the subscripted table load first in either source order, and uopt then reverses the equ operands (`beq t2,t7` / `bnel a0,t4`). Any subscript or dereference on the other side (`st[1]` with `s32 *st`, `(*st).f`, `cs->a[0]`, an array field) is emitted first, and once both sides are subscripted source order decides. So typing a u8 * view as its struct field or an untyped s32 block as `st[1]` retires `a - b == 0`, `(*st).f` and punned-byte steers. Evidence: bossPickWaypointStep (boss/F0D0.c, beq t7,t2 at 0x220), csCmdStepSequenceProgress (global/478F0.c, 0x70e0/0x7138; as1probe ucode --cfe).
