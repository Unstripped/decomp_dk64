From `modelSlotResolveAssetTextures` (revisit 12): (1) `size = tmp * 8` with a one-op right-hand side is copy-propagated into its uses and re-CSEd as an expression temploc placed below the cfe temps (frame +4 per temp); a dead store to the operand right after it (`tmp = 0;`, FAKE) or a multi-op right-hand side keeps `size` a declared variable with its own home and register. (2) `x = a - b; x = x >> 3;` as two statements gives the block-local x two stores and a load, so its savings stay positive and it is coloured (t0), and its reload after a call takes the same register, so a following `y = x * 8` emits `sll t1,t0,3` directly; `x = (a - b) >> 3` leaves the chain in ring temps. (3) Webs of one variable share its colour, so reusing an early variable for a later value carries its register and wins ties by the lowest candidate bit. (4) Only `res |= f()` emits `or v1,v1,v0` (res first); `res = res | f()` and `res = f() | res` both give `or v1,v0,v1`; the `|=` cfe temp takes the vreg word after the declared locals. (5) `(T *)(u + s)` with `u32 u` and `s32 s` emits the converted operand first (`addu a1,t1,a0`); both u32 or pointer arithmetic give `addu a1,a0,t1`.
