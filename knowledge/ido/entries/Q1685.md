uopt treats one local as a single candidate across disjoint switch cases (Q0181, Q1055). In chaserEnemyStep (BA790) the local holds the case-0x37 fade/light values (stored to its home 64(sp): 0x494/0x49c/0x4a0/0x4b8, 0x618-0x620), the case-0x33 move flags (0x298-0x2b8) and the case-0/4 chase script id (0x360-0x378). Short roles that never cross a call leave the candidate's other pieces uncoloured, so they spill to the home; splitting either short role off (48 and 72 bodies, every declaration position, block scope, register, widths) or inlining it (ternaries, if/else calls, arithmetic forms) colours the local whole, removes the home stores, shrinks .text 2928 -> 2912 bytes and usually the frame 0x50 -> 0x48. Q1517's claim that the split grows .text and that the reuse is register-only is wrong: the home stores are memory evidence for the merge, and the reuse passed the three-part census (R-oildrum), so both FAKE tags went.
