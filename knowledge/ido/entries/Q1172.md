In race/0 raceOpponentFollowWaypoints two locals each carry two roles across calls (vx/vz offset, then velocity; tmp height, then heading), so the roles share callee-saved FP registers. Splitting them into separate locals grows .text by 0x50 but leaves the frame unchanged, so the reuse stays FAKE (149188, e79659, tagged pre-existing).
