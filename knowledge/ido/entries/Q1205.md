A do-while(0) macro used uniformly at the uses of a long-lived local adds blocks to its range and lowers its priority through the block-count divisor (Q0163), which can flip its order against a cfe temp. Evidence (vsprintf): elen 25.19 -> 21.94, below the `||` temp (23.33), so elen gets a1 and the temp a0; it needs 6 of the 7 e/f-arm uses (with 5: 42 lines).
