In `projectileRenderSpriteRing` (global/9BF30.c, 153bd8), the fix removed the file-local `gSPMatrixShared` and used the gbi `gSPMatrix` macro, taking FAKE from 2 to 1. The cfe frame census showed that all `switch` statements in the function share a single switch temp slot (-276); there is not one per switch. Each gbi block `_g` gets its own home. To free the word that the 18th block's `_g` needs, the fix declared a register-only function-scope local in the unused pad region (after `q1`) and changed `f64 pad[2]` to `f64 pad`. That kept the in-memory scalars at -180..-196, cfe len at 272 and the uopt `idx*4` spill at 0x7c. Lesson: when a macro conversion adds `_g` homes, take the extra word out of pad arrays rather than moving real locals. Merge 171f13f5, ledger 130150b3, base 8976068a; unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackprojectileRenderSpriteRing2".

**2026-11-15c refinement:** (projectileRenderSpriteRing, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup RereviewMixed1115c", 625cbb82) a nested macro (gDPLoadTextureBlock) still gives each inner gDPSetTextureImage/gDPSetTile block its own `_g` home, so nesting saves no frame. The one free word below the user locals is uopt's spill slot: one extra home moves the `idx` spill 0x7c -> 0x78 (Q0880).
