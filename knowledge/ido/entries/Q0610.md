An s32 local holding a float's bit pattern (`0xC2B40000`) and moved into an FPR compiles the same as the plain `-90.0f` literal (both load the constant through `lui`/`mtc1`), so such integer spellings are not steers: write the float (`matrixMakeRotationX`, cleanup round 9-10).
