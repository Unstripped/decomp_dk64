7CA80 actorAllocate computes `(u16)((headerSize + 7) & 0xFFF8)` twice; the CSE is spilled `sw t6,0x24(sp)` (0x350) and reloaded `lw t8,0x24(sp)` (0x378). Dropping only the FIRST cast leaves the second cast applied to the shared spilled value, which ugen then reloads as its low half (`lhu 0x26(sp)`) instead of the word. Dropping both gives the same instructions with registers renamed (0x340-0x3b4). Extends Q1565.
