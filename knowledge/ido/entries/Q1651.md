Some targets start a second counter after the loop guard while the loop counter starts before it. A guarded do-while with a source offset local and a delayed `dl = next` steer then becomes a plain `for (i = 0; i < n; i++)` with the offset written `i * K`. uopt rotates the loop (Q1616) and strength-reduces `i * K` into its own IV, started after the guard. A source offset local starts before the guard, which was Q1299's score-2 miss. `move sN,v0` then fills the bnez slot without a steer. Locals assigned before the for and used only inside it are computed after the guard. A constant bound lets LFTR remove the counter (`slti s0,120`).

raceRenderSpeedBoostHud: 0x5ec, 0x5fc, 0x628, 0x6c0. raceRenderCoinHud: 0x968, 0x96c, 0x9a0, 0x9a4, 0xa14; both loops share i, and `s32 pad[2]` still owns the frame (Q0691).

This supersedes Q1299's "both kept". It also explains why an index-form try that kept the do-while scored 120: as1 filled the bnez slot with the IV increment.
