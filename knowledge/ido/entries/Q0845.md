Boundary of Q0844. Rewriting a steered equality chain as a sentinel switch helps only where the target's compare order or structure differs from what the short-circuit C gives. An `||`/else-if chain whose target compares appear in source order with a byte reload per compare (gfxDrawTexturedTileGrid 0x3cb4-0x408c, bonusControllerUpdate 0xb20-0xb44) is the original chain: a switch cannot carry the removal of the nearby steers there. A two-case switch can match the block shape exactly and still differ only in the selector's register (projectileSpriteRingUpdate, `beqzl a0` 0x84c then `bne a0,1` 0x854); a switch over a chain that the target already compares in order swaps the s0/s1 colouring (actorCollisionProcessHit 0x24c-0x25c, 37 lines).
