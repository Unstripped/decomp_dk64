An s register saved in the prologue and restored in the epilogue with no other use is the signature of a candidate kept alive only by code-free tests (a dead-test steer), not of a missing variable: no natural use reproduces it.

Evidence: modelSlotFreeAssetTextures (global/36880.c): s1 is stored at 0x46a8 and restored at 0x4730 with no other use; the `asset` candidate is kept live across the loop only by tests that emit no code.
