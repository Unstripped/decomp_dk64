With an ordinary `for (...) { if (skip) continue; ... }` IDO puts the `i + 1` computation into each arm (`addiu s3,a1,1` three times) and produces the induction walks itself, so m2c output shaped as a `next = i + 1` do-while plus byte-offset walk hacks rebuilds as a plain for+continue (`mpDrawScoreGlyphs`).
