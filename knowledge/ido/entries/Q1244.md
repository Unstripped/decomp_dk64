In a merged case group, re-testing the switch's selector field (`if (gCurrentActor->state != 0x46)` inside `case 0x28: case 0x2A: case 0x46:` in boss/52B0 bossMadJackStep) does not reload the field: uopt keeps the dispatched value in a second register live into the case (`lbu a0,0x154(s0)` 0x2b8, `move v1,a0` 0x2c0) and compares it there (`li at,0x46; beq a0,at` at 0x840). So a merged-case form of a cross-case goto costs a compare plus a selector copy register, not a reload (the target's 0x28/0x2A arm has neither: goto u2103c kept).
