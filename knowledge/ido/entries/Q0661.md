`zbufferReadDepth` (103AB0) really returns u16: `return (u32)result >> 3;` with a u16 return replaces `((u32)result >> 3) & 0xFFFF` on s32, and the caller then drops its value-named `invalid = 0x7FFF` local and compares `depth == 0x7FFF`.
