In C0EE0 enemyGridStateStep the empty default of `switch (st->state)` gets its own block 0xa58 (bound check `beqz at,a58` at 0xc4,
gap entry 5 -> 0xa58, join 0xa60) holding its own copy of the post-switch gCurrentActor reload. Removing it changes allocation
(a3 -> a0) and shrinks .text 3984 -> 3824. Q1499 covered the loop case; this one is a plain switch. Under the empty-default ruling
(own block -> ORIG) it is an ORIG candidate (pending.md).
