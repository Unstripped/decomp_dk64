tagBarrelCheckProximityAndFall (global/80150.c, merge 373a55e2, unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HackthrowableBarrelUpdate", record de1b02): the target stores a known constant and then reloads it right away (`sb 1,0x47(sp)` at 0xbf8, `lbu 0x47(sp)` at 0xbfc). This happens at a join where every incoming path stored the same constant. Only memory-forced storage reproduces this pattern. Either a `volatile` local or a local whose address escapes works. A non-volatile local gets constant-folded at the join, or gets coloured to $s0 (see Q0742 for the width-dependent colouring). You can check for this signature in the target alone. When you see it, rule out non-volatile spellings and go straight to a volatile or escaped-address spelling (Q0300) before you spend time on the permuter.

**2026-11-20 correction (CleantagBarrelCheckProximityAndFallc, re-measure at 6064fcc9):** memory-forced storage is not the only
source of the store-then-reload at the join: a non-volatile `s32 spawn` that uopt never colours (cand 0 splits into pieces 125/130,
both "-ve save") reproduces it at word width (`sw t2,0x44(sp)` 0xbf8, `lw t3,0x44(sp)` 0xbfc). Only the `u8` spelling is split and
given $s0 (Q0742), folding the join to `li s0,1`; so the `volatile` in tagBarrelCheckProximityAndFall is needed for the byte width
alone (sb/lbu 0x47(sp)). See Q0191.
