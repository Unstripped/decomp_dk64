In `tagBarrelOrbitKongs` (global/80150.c, unit HacktagBarrelOrbitKongs2, merge cfdb7ca8, record 46dea1a1), kept probes b38837/1cc068/c2023d/d45e68 show that uopt keeps a dead u8 mask chain only if every link is present: three cast-mask lines plus a final `u = u` or `u = (u32)u`. Any subset of 1-3 of those lines is deleted by uopt. So are chains of 2-6 simple-mask lines and chains built from `u = u + 1`. When the full chain survives, it reaches ugen and can still change register allocation upstream. as1 then deletes it, so no instructions from it appear in the output. This is a register steer with no visible code, which makes it one of the coupled steers in Q0602: removing a single link does not just weaken the effect, it turns the whole steer off. Use it together with the cursor-local regalloc lever in Q0744 for this function. Unit "2026-11-15: cleanup HacktagBarrelOrbitKongs2".
