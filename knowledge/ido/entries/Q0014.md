In `func_80024914`, referencing `D_807FBB40` directly via `D_807FBB40->...` at entry causes IDO to materialize `&D_807FBB40` into temporary register `$v1` before `$sp` adjustment. Because no global pointer local is maintained across function calls, IDO does not allocate a callee-saved `$s1` register for `&D_807FBB40`.
