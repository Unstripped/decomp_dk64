ACDC0 hudPauseMenuIconUpdate (frame 0x58): the nine named homes fill sp+0x34-0x57 and the first uopt spill temp is sp+0x30. Any tenth local (or a hidden word) pushes the spill temps from 0x24/0x30 to 0x20/0x2c, so splitting a reused local needs another word freed; when none can be, the reuse stays a steer (six R5 reuses tagged 2026-11-20). A narrow local beside one without a narrow partner still costs its word (Q1198).
