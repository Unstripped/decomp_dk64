read the target's `sb`/`sh`/`sw` offsets and place unused locals of the right size and position (three leading `u8` fixed all of `func_8072D13C`'s slots; a leading unused `f64` added 8 frame bytes); `f32 one = 1.0f` for every `1.0f` operand reproduces a register-held constant. IDO packs u8 scalar locals downward one byte at a time, while a u8 array is 4-aligned. In `bossMainStep` (F0D0), scalars plus unused `u8 pad1` / `u8 pad2[3]` reproduce the gappy byte layout a FAKE struct used to pin. Switching an array element to a scalar can flip the `beql` operand order; swap the comparison's operands to restore it.
