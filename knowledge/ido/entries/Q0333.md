each use of a float literal adds 0.5 adjsave and all uses of one literal form one function-wide web: a 2–3-use constant outranks single-def f32 locals (1.5) and takes `$f0`/`$f2`, and an 8-use call-argument `2.0f` was hoisted into callee-saved `$f20`. Split webs by spelling (`2.0`, `2`, `2.0f` per call; a late `x = 0.0f` store as double `0.0`, else the call-argument zero becomes `li a1,0`), or raise a local's adjsave with extra defs (reuse it for other temps, `v = t->f; v = expr - v;`). In blocks with complex expressions ugen reserves `f4 f6 f8 f10 f16 f18`, overflowing to `f12 f2 f14` (`func_8002CBEC`, `func_80026F04`, `func_8002BF98`, `func_80028AD0`, `func_8002AED8`).
