as1 orders by source line, not statement order. A for-init statement carries the for line's `.loc`, which is also the `.loc` of the loop preheader hoists (li/la/li.s), so its `move rX,zero` is scheduled after earlier lines and among the hoists. A zero init on its own line before the `for` orders before later-line inits, and two inits on one line may appear in either order.

Evidence: actorNodeRingSpawnRings (global/C1E70.c): the target has `move s1,zero` at 0x5c before `move s5,zero` at 0x60; both orders of two inits on one line match (full=0), while the for-init form and a split header each give 2. Kept as `// LAYOUT:`.
