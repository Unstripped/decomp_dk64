struct-array glyph access `((Glyph *)tbl[font])[chr].w` instead of `((u8 *)tbl)[chr*4+2]` cut two parks from ~100 diff lines to ~10; `arr[k]` instead of a byte offset lets uopt strength-reduce the index into a late candidate; `(u32)arg0 * K` in a row base creates the row-0 pointer IV; `(u32)arg4 == 5` stops CSE of a narrow parameter across a call.

A one-pass loop `for (i = 0; i != 1U; i++)` with `s32 i` compiles the same as `u32 i; for (i = 0; i < 1; i++)` (`csCmdSpawnSparkAndLight`): the readable form is the unsigned counter.
